## Idea

The Enemies Project (12 episodes, 2025–2026) is a natural experiment in our core thesis. A mediator
puts two ideological enemies in a room, lets them debate, then demonstrates on camera that **the debate
changed nothing** — and only *after* a structured understanding process do they soften, drop qualifiers,
and admit each other's considerations.

The hook: **"Arguments accomplish nothing" is only true without verified understanding.** With it, the
same content that bounced off lands — Aumann's agreement theorem in the wild. This piece dissects the
moment that flip happens, names the mechanism in our vocabulary, and ends on why the goal is not to
replace the mediator but to make his highest-leverage moves available at near-zero cost.

## Core constructs (the public contribution)

### 1. Debate-without-verification vs verified disagreement (Aumann)
Aumann: rational agents with common priors who share posteriors cannot agree to disagree. The unmet
precondition is *common knowledge of meaning*. Debate skips it — people update against a strawman, so
no dissonance fires. Verified understanding (paraphrase-confirmed) supplies the precondition. Honest
caveat: affiliative/identity priors (a9/a27) make convergence partial — which is why softening, not
conversion, is the usual result.

### 2. Three layers: values / beliefs / dislike (ties to definitions.md flip taxonomy)
- **Values** don't change — they're the solvent (shared wound under opposite politics).
- **Beliefs/positions** change slowly, at the margin: less extreme, more nuanced, new considerations
  admitted. A Position Flip in slow motion, or a Fork.
- **Dislike** drops fast.
The Enemies Project mostly produces **Fork + verified disagreement** (definitions.md:418), not full Flips —
but it has no instrument to measure the marginal position shift. CP does. *Why CP differs: broad value
topics yield Forks; precisely-worded anti-points force Flips.*

### 3. "Inhabit, don't adopt" = cognitive understanding (a17)
Role-reversal is paraphrase-to-confirmation maxed out. The flip marker is linguistic: the qualifier drops
("saying it without the but"), or *"you got me."*

### 4. Dissected flip moments (the anecdotal evidence — the heart of the piece)
For 3–4 episodes, reconstruct: **(a) the two positions → (b) the trigger line → (c) the reaction →
(d) how it de-escalated → (e) the consequence.** Candidate moments (verify quotes against the analysis doc):
- Cop & protester — Afeni voices something Kevin never said; *"This woman has actually seen me."*
- Palestinian & Zionist — Eleazar drops the "but" during role-reversal.
- Trans & MAGA — Nancy's on-camera apology after being accurately played.
- Apostate & believer — *"the word traitor feels so far away."*

### 5. The ending: democratize the mediator, don't replace him
The corpus quantifies the cost — *"a professional freaking mediator, hours of talk."* CP's thesis: take
the 4 load-bearing moves (paraphrase prompt, depth-check, loaded-word flag, role-reversal cue) and make
them available without a scarce human. The artistry (real-time safety read) doesn't transfer; the
structure does.

## Enrichment (2026-06-15)
Source: "Verified understanding and Aumann agreement" (2026-06-15)
Applied to: a-spec body

Sharpens Core construct 1 (Aumann). Dialogue has **two stacked Bayesian layers** — (i) inferring meaning (grounding) and (ii) updating on propositions *given* shared meaning — and Aumann's theorem assumes layer (i) is solved exactly and for free. CP lives in the regime Aumann explicitly *excludes* (different priors, unshared semantics) — which is the regime where the product is even coherent: "understand without agreement" survives precisely because real interlocutors hold different priors. Framing correction for the draft: verified understanding is best read as a **meet / infimum** (the common floor — meet of partitions, intersection of lexicons, min of estimates), **not** a multiplicative weight; the founder's "verified-understanding × position" intuition slots more cleanly as a Geanakoplos–Polemarchakis *announcement round over meaning* than as a Bayesian posterior weight. Self-concealing failure mode to name: the recursion runs cleanly and returns a *confident* answer when lexicons diverge undetected — no native alarm, because every agent trusts the shared floor.

## Enrichment (2026-06-28)
Source: "Truth-vs-understanding primacy dialogue with an interlocutor" (2026-06-28). See a42.
Applied to: Core construct 1 / 2 (verified disagreement, Fork)

Concrete illustration for "verified disagreement, not conversion": the **wolf and hare in the pit**. Both fall into a pit they cannot escape; the hare insists "we're on a sunlit lawn." The wolf understands the words and rejects them as false. The value of the wolf paraphrasing the hare's position is *not* to agree about the lawn — it is to establish whether the disagreement is **real or merely verbal** (is the hare lying, hallucinating, voicing fear, or just wanting the wolf to stop complaining?). Only the check tells you which layer the fault is on, so you don't spend energy debugging the wrong one. This is the diagnostic reading of the protocol: the paraphrase does not resolve the conflict, it *classifies* it — capacity gap vs. value conflict vs. lexicon mismatch.
