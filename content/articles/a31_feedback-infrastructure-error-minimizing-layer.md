# Feedback Infrastructure — Verified Understanding as an Error-Minimizing Layer

> Working title — not final. `[FOUNDER DECISION: title]`.

## The idea

Feedback is an error signal. Today, when a party files a complaint (or gives feedback of any kind), there is no guarantee the receiver actually understood it — so the signal stays noisy, contested, and hard to act on. Layering verified understanding on top of existing feedback systems makes the error signal **purer and more actionable**: verifying comprehension of feedback reduces the error in the error-correction loop itself. Framed at the limit: *verifying understanding of someone in need is a public service.*

## Why it's article-worthy (not a product pivot)
