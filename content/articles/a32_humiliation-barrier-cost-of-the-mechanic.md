# The Humiliation Barrier — What Verification Costs and Why Most People Don't Pay It

> Working title — not final. `[FOUNDER DECISION: title]`. Alternatives: "The Cost of the Mechanic" · "Why a One-Line Protocol Stays Unused."

## The idea

Verification has a one-line protocol, yet almost no one runs it. Why? Because naming a low comprehension number — or asking someone to paraphrase — feels like admitting you're slow. The barrier isn't ignorance of the method; it's the **social cost of deploying it**. This deserves its own slot in the story arc: between the space (the gap exists) and the redistribution (what changes once it's measured) sits *what it costs to deploy verification, and why most people don't.*
