## 1. Core thesis (positioning spine)

Cognitive psychology has the *need for cognitive closure* (Kruglanski & Webster): the drive to seize an answer and freeze on it. Psycholinguistics has the *grounding criterion* (Clark): the point where two people mutually believe the listener understood "well enough for current purposes" — and stop checking. The first governs closure on *validity*; the second, closure on *meaning*. The same seize-and-freeze dynamic runs on both, but the meaning case is worse for two reasons. First, the referent that would correct a validity error is the world, which pushes back; the referent that would correct a meaning error is only the other mind, and it arrives late, lossy, and after the cost is paid. Second, people don't even run their grounding criterion on accuracy — felt understanding tracks warmth and similarity, not comprehension — so the criterion is routinely met falsely and silently. Call this *premature grounding closure*. Its consequence: it sits *upstream* of debiasing, because you cannot correct someone's bias through a channel whose meaning never grounded. Verified comprehension is therefore not a debiasing technique but the precondition for one.

## 2. The key distinction (validity-closure vs meaning-closure)

Validity-closure is self-correctable in principle: the world is a shared referent that pushes back. Meaning-closure has only a *lagged and lossy* referent — downstream behavior eventually reveals the mismatch, but late, partially, and after the coordination has already failed, by which point hindsight bias has rewritten both parties' memory of what was agreed. So premature meaning-closure is far more weakly self-correcting than validity-closure, and effectively self-concealing in real time.

*(Note: do not write "no referent" or "the only referent is the other mind." That overclaims and is the first thing a reviewer pulls. The defensible claim is "lagged, lossy, post-hoc referent.")*

## 3. Conceptual lineage and placement

The underlying object — recursive mutual belief — was formulated independently across fields, which is itself worth noting (convergence as a sign the object is real):

- Lewis (1969): "common knowledge"
- Schiffer (1972): "mutual knowledge*"
- Aumann (1976, "Agreeing to Disagree"): formal/game-theoretic common knowledge
- Stalnaker (1973/1978/2002): "common ground"
- Higgins/Echterhoff: "shared reality"; Pickering & Garrod (2004): "interactive alignment"

Clark & Marshall (1981) identified the **processing paradox**: humans can't compute the infinite recursion, so they short-circuit it with heuristics (e.g., physical co-presence). The *grounding criterion* itself is Clark's (Clark & Wilkes-Gibbs 1986; Clark & Schaefer 1989; Clark & Brennan 1991; Clark 1996).

**Placement of the Min Principle:** a tractable replacement for the uncomputable recursion — a bounded paired estimate (joint comprehension capped by the lower self-assessment), descended from Pinker but solving the Clark–Marshall problem. This is the precise, citable slot. *(Flagged for inclusion.)*

## 5. Empirical backing already in hand

**People substitute feeling for accuracy.** The sharper part of the thesis has solid empirical support. *(Flagged for inclusion.)* Felt understanding / perceived partner responsiveness is driven substantially by warmth, emotional similarity, projection, and motivated reconstrual rather than by actual comprehension:

- Feeling understood can predict relationship quality more strongly than the partner's *actual* responsiveness (Gordon & Diamond 2023 review).
- Greater emotional similarity → more *perceived* responsiveness (Sels et al. 2020).
- Perceived responsiveness reflects motivated reconstrual, not just enacted responsiveness (Lemay & Neal 2014).

**The relational substrate already moves bias.** Perceived partner responsiveness enhances intellectual humility and weakens self-serving biases — better-than-average effect, overclaiming, hindsight bias (Reis et al. 2018). This is ammunition for the upstream-of-debiasing argument, with one caveat: it shows *felt* responsiveness affects bias directly; the dossier's distinct claim is that *verified* grounding gates debiasing *uptake*.

## 6. Upstream-of-debiasing argument

Interpersonal debiasing ("you have bias X," then discussion) presupposes that the message about the bias was grounded. If grounding fails, the instruction is mis-received and cannot work. Strongest for *communicated* debiasing; weaker for *self-administered procedural* debiasing (a checklist), though even that was learned via an understood instruction once. Since most real-world debiasing in teams and coaching is interpersonal, the precondition bites where it counts.

This replaces the earlier "benchmark to prove separateness" framing. The right experiment is an **interaction**: debiasing-alone vs grounding-then-debiasing.

**1. The corroboration-not-proof status clause (the `[/cp worth clarifying in a 35?]` marker).** The load-bearing distinction that protects the whole framework from the justificationist trap: **verified understanding is not a proof, it's a visible lack of disproof.** Keep that exact phrasing. The paraphrase round is a *falsification attempt* on the hypothesis "I understand you" — survive it and the claim is corroborated, never proven; a later-surfaced misunderstanding isn't a failure, it's corroboration overturned by new evidence (exactly how it should behave). Candidate status clause to attach wherever the *canonical definition* of verified understanding lives (definitions.md, not floating):
> **Verified understanding** — a state in which a paraphrase round surfaced no detectable difference between intended meaning and reconstructed meaning. *Status:* corroboration, not proof. It asserts the absence of detected disagreement, not the presence of identity, and remains defeasible by any later-surfaced gap.

Terminology resolution: use **corroborated** in the technical layer (Popper coined it to escape the truth-connoting baggage of "verification"); keep **verified** as the public/product term, explicitly anchored to its *engineering* sense ("a check ran and passed," like a verified build / verified email). Both, scoped; the status clause bridges them.

**2. Democratization-via-protocol metaphor (the `important methaphots for value prop` marker).** The mechanism that would justify "CP democratizes the comprehension precondition for Popperian critique" is **externalization of tacit skill into explicit protocol** — the same move by which the surgical checklist democratized safety expertise, or double-entry bookkeeping democratized financial control. That analogy class hands you the measurement template (outcomes pre/post protocol across operators of varying skill) and connects directly to **H-GapCompression** (democratization = gap-compression). Candidate value-prop material; also a36.

**3. Popper-gap prior-art shield — synthesis + operationalization, not discovery (the `popper gap` markers).** Strengthens §3 / §9. The recursion the founder located: comprehension-verification isn't only a missing step *inside* the method — it's **presupposed by the adoption of the method** (to agree "we'll corroborate, not prove," we first have to corroborate we understood "corroborate, not prove"). The recursion is the *tell it's right*, not a leak: a justificationist comprehension claim is self-refuting under self-application; the corroborationist one survives it (a non-self-exempting epistemology, same shape as pan-fallibilism). Prior art to cite so a reviewer doesn't say "reinvented":
- **Gadamer** (*Truth and Method*, 1960) — understanding is *prior to* and presupposed by method; closest "sits underneath the apparatus" precedent (but fusion-with-tradition, and hostile to method, not extending it).
- **Wittgenstein, sharpened by Kripke** ("Kripkenstein") — no inner fact proves you grasped a rule/meaning; only public agreement. The "no proof, only failure to find a difference," at the level of meaning.
- **Habermas** — comprehensibility (*Verständlichkeit*) as a validity claim / precondition; named it, never operationalized a check.
- **Davidson** (radical interpretation) + **Clark–Marshall** mutual-knowledge regress (answered by the Min Principle).
Calibrated verdict: **synthesis + operationalization, not discovery** — state it defeasibly; these five are the lineage (academic legitimacy), not the competition. Taxonomy handle: a **domain epistemology** — a fallibilist epistemology *of intersubjective comprehension* — formed by crossing critical rationalism's *method* with the hermeneutic/communicative tradition's *subject matter*, then operationalized.
