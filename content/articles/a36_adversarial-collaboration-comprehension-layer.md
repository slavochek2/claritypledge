## Source

## Audience
Forecasting / rationalist / EA / AI-safety researchers. Adversarial collaboration is revered there (Kahneman; Mellers & Tetlock ran the canonical versions). This piece imports nothing foreign — it slots into a method they already honor.

## The idea
Adversarial collaboration (opposing researchers jointly design the test of their disagreement) has a perennial weak point: there is no protocol ensuring each side actually understands the other's position before they argue over evidence. The clarity letter from an opponent **is** that missing comprehension handshake. Frame: *"CP is the comprehension layer adversarial collaboration has always lacked"* — recast as the missing **formalized** layer (see Honesty fix).

## Honesty fix (the lesson from Habermas / Lakatos in the same conversation)
Drop "always lacked." Kahneman was famously meticulous about stating opponents' positions fairly — AC *has* done comprehension work, just ad hoc and dependent on rare temperament. The defensible claim is **operationalization, not discovery**: "the missing *formalized* layer — turning a temperament-dependent practice into a teachable, repeatable protocol." A referee who knows how carefully Kahneman worked rejects "always lacked" on sight.

## The structural move that makes it un-dismissable
Frame the piece as a **pre-registered prediction**, not an assertion: *"AC pairs who run a ratified comprehension handshake before designing the study will talk past each other less, and reach more decisive joint conclusions, than pairs who don't."* That does three jobs at once — positioning, a pre-committed falsifiable prediction, and a research proposal in the exact methodological idiom the audience honors. Hard to dismiss someone handing you the experiment that could prove them wrong.

## Predictions carried
- **H-PopperianIncrement** (hypotheses.md) — CP's falsification-distinctive layer beats a matched active-listening-only control on error-catching + decision quality (guards against the "rebranded Rogers" objection).
- **H-GapCompression** (hypotheses.md) — democratization = the novice–expert comprehension-fidelity gap narrows under the protocol.

## Guards
- **No purity test.** This is an offer of infrastructure, not a test of who's a "real truth-seeker." The moment "declining CP = not a real fallibilist" creeps in, the researchers most worth reaching walk. Willingness is evidence of openness; declining is not proof of its absence.
- What lands hardest: one documented AC that stalled on *mutual misunderstanding* rather than real evidential disagreement — concrete proof the bottleneck exists rather than is assumed.
