# Earned vs Assumed Trust — The Instrument That Tells You Which Kind You're Holding

> Working title — not final. `[FOUNDER DECISION: title]`. Alternatives: "The Trust You Never Checked" · "Earned, Not Assumed."

## The idea

You can trust someone who never verified they understood you — and you routinely do. That trust is one of two things, and the difference is the product. **Earned trust:** they've demonstrably bridged your gaps. **Assumed trust:** it *feels* mutual but was never checked — built on the feeling of being understood rather than the fact of it. The mistake is never "extending trust" (humans must; we can't verify everything). The mistake is **not knowing which kind you're holding** — and assumed trust is, by structure, designed to hide that it's assumed (the illusion of common belief operating exactly as defined: self-concealing).

So the sharpest one-line product claim isn't "builds epistemic trust" — it's: **makes the difference between earned and assumed trust visible, then lets you earn the assumed part.** The instrument doesn't manufacture trust; it tells you which kind you have.

## The self-priming mechanism (the strong sub-claim)

The same instrument also surfaces the *predictable* beliefs that block verification ("admitting I didn't follow makes me look weak"; "disagreeing will rupture us"; "they should just get me"). Clearing those is step-zero of the method, and it's elegant: **the instrument clears the very beliefs that prevent the instrument from being used.** The tool that removes the obstacle to the tool — self-priming, so buy-in doesn't need a separate intervention.

## Honesty caveats (load-bearing — keep them in the piece)
- Don't claim the tool "creates epistemic trust" — the field (Fonagy/Csibra) treats epistemic trust as developmental and hard-won. Claim the narrower, true thing: it builds the *verification practice* that **earns** epistemic trust in a specific relationship. Verification is the faucet; epistemic trust is the water level (flow vs stock).
- The claim "the literature names verified understanding as the goal but doesn't operationalize it" is **unverified** — confirm against the mentalization/epistemic-trust literature before publicizing. Do not inherit it as fact.
- Stay in lane vs CBT: surface only the *verification-blocking* beliefs, not clinical beliefs broadly — otherwise a therapist reads it as cognitive-restructuring turf-encroachment.
