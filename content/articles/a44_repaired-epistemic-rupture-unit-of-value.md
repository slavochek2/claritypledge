# The Repaired Epistemic Rupture — Naming the Unit of Value

> Working title — not final. `[FOUNDER DECISION: title]`.

## The idea

Every product has a *unit of value* — the discrete, countable thing the customer can point at each time — distinct from the *outcome*, the cumulative state that's the real reason it's worth it. Conflating them is how you end up selling something slow, invisible, and gameable.

For this protocol the unit is **a repaired epistemic rupture**: a *disagreement-driven and surfaced* strain — really a misunderstanding — metabolized by verifying "do we actually understand each other's position." Not all ruptures: not withdrawal/silent-drift (those are real churn but not this), not fees/scheduling/transference/bad-fit. Narrow on both axes — disagreement-driven *and* surfaced — is the whole strategy, not a weakness.

The **outcome** is repair *capability* — the compounding muscle each repair builds, so the next is cheaper and bigger ruptures become survivable. Same shape as the Flip model: you point at one Flip (the unit), you deliver comprehension (the outcome). Here: you point at one repair, you deliver capability. **Listening calibration is the gear** that connects them — each on-platform repair quantifies calibration; better calibration makes the next repair cheaper; cheaper repairs are how capability compounds.

## The discipline (why it matters)
- Unit on the marquee, outcome in the engine room. Pitch/price on the *outcome* (capability, or worse retention) → back in the trap. Anchor value on the discrete, demonstrable *unit*; the outcome is the story of why the units repeat.
- **Unit of value ≠ unit of billing.** "Repaired rupture" is what you *point at*, not an invoice line — metering per-repair is nonsensical. Billing is group sessions / 1-on-1 badging / coach certification `[FOUNDER DECISION: pricing]`. Per-repair survives only as a *proof-of-value metric*, never a price.
- Value-prop row: headline = the felt outcome (ruptures get repaired instead of ending the relationship); second clause = *and the capability compounds* — durability, not headline.

## The capability ladder (narrowing)
Surfacing a big rupture depends on having repaired small ones first: experience → understand → commit → low-stakes rehearse → reward → only then usable at high stakes. Consequence: **the tool can't first appear at the big rupture** — it must be introduced early, on small disagreements, and (founder's refinement) the earliest small reps may be worked **methodology-only, no tool**, before any instrument. Adoption-requirement and falsification risk in one: if a practitioner can only get buy-in once trust is already strained, the ladder can't be built.

## Arc
ARC-4 (The Assumption Nobody Questioned) — the assumption that you sell "comprehension" or "retention" rather than a discrete, repeatable repair.
