# Why "Understand Me Emotionally" Self-Defeats — and What Works Instead

> Working title — not final. `[FOUNDER DECISION: title]`.

## The idea

Demanding emotional understanding first tends to trigger shutdown rather than connection. The mechanism is the be-spontaneous trap crossed with avoidant deactivation: avoidant withdrawal is triggered by *anticipated* unmet demand, and information alone can't disarm an anticipatory affective response. So "just understand how I feel" asks for exactly the thing the demand makes harder to give.

The order that works is the reverse of the intuitive one: **cognitive understanding precedes emotional understanding.** Verifying "I've got what you actually mean" is a low-threat, checkable move that doesn't ask the other person to feel anything on cue — and it's the ground that makes felt understanding possible. This is why a paraphrase-and-confirm protocol is not a colder substitute for empathy; it's the precondition empathy quietly depends on. It also marks the boundary against NVC / emotional-attunement work: same goal, different and earlier entry point.
