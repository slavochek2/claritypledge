# The Metric That Wasn't

**Arc:** ARC-1 (Tested and Tightened) — with a twist: the claim wasn't tightened. It was **abandoned and replaced**, and that's the story.
**Source conversation:** "Shared meanings and group coordination" (2026-07-16), ~20 rounds of pressure-testing.

## Idea

I started with a pitch I liked: *shared meanings are the group-level outcome nobody measures; the more shared meanings, the smarter the group.* Teams measure individual performance and outcomes; nobody measures the thing that actually makes coordination cheap.

The claim did not survive one hour.

**The objection I couldn't answer:** "the more shared meaning, the smarter" is a **convergence** claim — and convergence is precisely what a Popperian should be suspicious of. A group at maximal shared meaning has no error correction left in it. That's the groupthink limit case. I was selling the thing my own framework exists to prevent.

I did not repair it. I replaced it. That distinction is the article.

## The replacement — cheaper, testable, and it became the actual pitch

> **"Right now, in every conversation you're having, there's a number — how much you actually understand each other. You're already at that number. You just don't know it. Nobody has ever asked. We ask."**

No convergence claim. No promise that more shared meaning is better. Just: *the quantity exists, it's unmeasured, and it's measurable.* This became the event copy.

## The repair I rejected (and why naming it matters)

The available patch was: *it's not the **amount** of shared meaning, it's shared meaning about the **right layer** — shared understanding of what each person **means**, so disagreement stays about the world rather than about words.* Common ground as infrastructure **for** productive conflict, not a substitute for it.

That's a good sentence. It's also a different sentence than the one I said, and stapling it on afterwards would have been retrofitting a claim to survive an objection. Worth showing the reader both moves and why the second one is cheating.

## What else broke, and what held

**Held — the architecture, in one sentence:**
> The number was never the gate. **Stakes is the gate, paraphrase is the instrument, and the min is an effort allocator and a sovereignty guarantee** — nobody's estimate can be talked up.

**Held (with teeth) — the norm inversion.** Today, not answering "0–10, how much do you understand me?" is normal. It should be the abnormal thing. The Bicchieri-shaped resolution: the **number itself stays a costless convention** (no penalty for a low score — that's what makes honesty free), while **refusal to disclose** is the mixed-motive costly act that needs the sanction. You reward the ritual, not the outcome of the ritual. Get that backwards and you've built an instrument that punishes your own best practitioner.

**Broke — the min is blind to convergent error.** The min protects against *divergent* miscalibration (one high, one low). Both parties at 8 and both wrong → min is 8, gate opens, illusion intact. And by my own account the illusion of recursive understanding is **correlated, not idiosyncratic** — so the blind spot sits exactly where the thesis says the danger is. Partial answer: at high stakes, paraphrase fires regardless of the number, so *stakes* — not the min — covers convergent error where it matters. Mid-stakes convergent illusion remains an accepted residual cost. Say so; don't paper it.

**Open — the pledge problem.** Bicchieri's real objection aims at the name: individual commitment doesn't shift behaviour unless the reference network's expectations visibly change. A pledge can change the signer and still fail to change the room. Argument for pledging in dyads and groups, never alone. Unresolved.

## Why article-worthy

- A founder killing his own metric in public, with the transcript of what killed it.
- The convergence/error-correction tension is a real intellectual problem, not a straw man — and it's the one every "alignment tool" walks into.
- The convention-vs-norm split (sanction the refusal, not the score) is a genuinely load-bearing mechanism design most people get backwards.
- It ends with an honest open item rather than a resolution.

## Open questions

- Should calibration be scored at the **dyad** level, not the individual? ("You two run at 3 rounds to convergence" is a fact about a pair, carries no individual verdict, still resolves.) Untested; possibly the better object.
- Does the replacement pitch survive contact with a buyer, or is it just less falsifiable?
