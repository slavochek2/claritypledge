# Three Gaps, Not Eighteen

> Working title — `[FOUNDER DECISION: title]`. Alternatives: "The Criterion That Killed Fifteen of My Own Articles" · "When a Pattern Becomes a Template."

## Arc

ARC-1 (Tested and Tightened) — a claim stated boldly (a series of "X named the problem and never formalized it" pieces, one per thinker), each link tested against a criterion, most links exposed as not qualifying, and a tightened version offered with the vulnerability named.

**Why this is publishable rather than housekeeping:** the author is the one who was about to over-claim, and the piece is the pruning. That is the same costly-signal move as a55 and a63 — an author who publishes his own pruning is doing the thing the product sells.

## The situation to open with

The project holds a documented plan for **eighteen thinker-conjecture events**, one per thinker, each framed as "I think [Thinker]'s framework is missing something — come prove me wrong" (`docs/lean-canvas.md` §Channels). Alongside it, a habit had formed of naming each one a *Gap*: the Popper Gap, the Pinker Gap, the Deutsch Gap, and — for about a day — the Weaver Gap.

Two separate conversations, on consecutive days, independently flagged the same hazard.

> "Three instances is the point where a genuine pattern and a rhetorical template become indistinguishable from outside. Someone will notice the shape before they evaluate the content, and the template reading is cheaper for them than the pattern reading."

> "If you publish eighteen 'X Gap' pieces, the form reads as a template and readers will discount the two that are real."

The convergence is the lede: **the form of the claim was about to devalue the content of the claim, and nothing in the plan would have caught it.**

## The criterion

Without one, "gap" is a house style rather than a claim. Proposed test — **all three required**:

1. The framework's central result has a precondition involving mutual understanding.
2. The author left that precondition unspecified.
3. Without it, the result weakens or fails.

## Applying it — the pruning, stated honestly

| Thinker | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Aumann** | **Strongest — stronger than Popper** | Common knowledge is not a hidden presupposition; it is an *explicit premise* of the theorem. You are not finding something buried, you are pointing at the stated antecedent and asking how it is ever satisfied. Formal, checkable, hard to wave away. |
| **Popper** | **Strong** | Falsification presupposes engaging the actual objection, not a reconstruction of it. |
| **Deutsch** | **Moderate** | The claim is in-principle, so "no stopping rule" is a *consequence*, not an oversight. Still qualifies; grade it accordingly. |
| **Pinker** | Moderate | He formalized the recursion; the missing piece is a termination procedure. |
| **Edmondson** | Plausible | Psychological safety measures willingness to speak, not whether what was spoken arrived. Speaking up and being understood are different variables and the instrument captures the first. |
| **Locke** | Weaker | Goal specificity is assumed measurable from the goal statement, not from the recipient's reconstruction. |
| **Weaver** | **Not a gap — and that is better** | See below. |
| **Bicchieri, Ostrom, and most of the rest** | **Not gaps** | They do not presuppose comprehension verification in any way that breaks their results. They are applications, adjacencies, or supports. |

**Recommendation that follows: three gap articles maximum — Aumann, Popper, Deutsch.** Everything else becomes event material, where the conjecture format is doing different work and repetition is a feature rather than a tell.

## The Weaver case — the most interesting result

Weaver's 1949 introduction to Shannon split the problem three ways: Level A (technical accuracy of symbol transmission), Level B (semantic accuracy of conveyed meaning), Level C (effectiveness of resulting conduct).

The claim the author was about to make — *Level B was named in 1949 and never developed into anything operational* — **will not survive contact with the literature.** At minimum:

- **Bar-Hillel & Carnap (1952)** built a formal semantic information theory on logical probability, which promptly produced the paradox that a contradiction carries maximal information. **Floridi's** veridicality requirement is the standing repair.
- **Juba & Sudan (2008)**, *Universal Semantic Communication*, formalizes goal-oriented communication between agents with no shared prior, success defined by a verifier.
- The **semantic / goal-oriented communication programme in 6G engineering** cites Weaver's Levels B and C as its literal charter and has been well funded since roughly 2021. **This is the one that gets thrown at you.**
- In psychometrics, the **cloze procedure (Taylor, 1953)** descends directly from Shannon's 1951 guessing experiments. Reading comprehension assessment is an enormous operational field.

And Weaver did not merely name the level. He proposed a *semantic receiver* performing a second decoding matched to the recipient, and introduced *semantic noise*. He also asserted Shannon's mathematics would generalize upward — the weak part of the essay, and **Shannon himself warned against exactly that extension** in his 1956 "Bandwagon" editorial. The accurate version: Weaver named the level, sketched an architecture, claimed the existing math would cover it, and was probably wrong about the last part. That is a better story than "he left it blank," because it explains why it stalled.

**So Weaver fails criterion 2 — and failing it is the good outcome.** He named Level B himself and said he was not solving it. You cannot claim discovery. You *can* claim to have filled a **stated open problem**, which is a cleaner and far less contestable position than claiming to have found something buried.

## The reframe that survives — and it is sharper than the gap claim

> **Every operationalization of Level B installs a third party who already knows the answer.**

Bar-Hillel and Carnap have a logician holding the state descriptions. The 6G systems have a designer holding the task metric. Cloze tests have an examiner holding the key. Clark's grounding experiments have a confederate who knows the target referent. Remove the omniscient third party — leave two people in a room with only their own self-reports as evidence — and Level B has no instrument at all. Seventy-seven years on.

Three reasons this does more work than the emptiness claim:

1. **It is falsifiable in the right direction** — someone must produce a two-party, no-referee instrument to defeat it.
2. **It does not require having read everything.**
3. **It is structurally identical to the Min Principle** — the whole problem is that the only available evidence is two self-estimates, which is precisely why the bound is the lower of them rather than something a referee could adjudicate.

**One flag to carry in the draft:** Weaver's Level B as phrased is still a *one-directional* fidelity question — sender's intent versus receiver's uptake. A joint quantity bounded by the weaker of two self-reports is not quite Weaver's B; it is a hybrid sitting between B and C. Claiming Level B outright mislocates what the project actually has.

## The closing turn

The criterion cost fifteen articles and returned three. It also converted the best of the discarded fifteen — Weaver — from a weak discovery claim into a strong "filled a stated open problem" claim.

That is the general lesson, and it is the same one the product sells: **a form that always returns "yes" is not a test.** Eighteen gaps found by a method that had no way to find a non-gap is not eighteen findings. It is one habit, applied eighteen times.

## Connections to make, not to over-make

- **Reddy's conduit metaphor (1979)** is the citation that earns the Weaver line its place: the transmission model did not merely fail to solve Level B, it installed a folk theory in which meaning travels *inside words* — which is the mechanism producing the illusion of comprehension the project claims to measure.
- **`features/p1026`** (research-programme rigor layer) is the internal sibling: this article is the *content-side* application of the same anti-degeneration discipline. The criterion here does for public claims what the novel-prediction field does for hypotheses. Cross-reference; do not merge.
