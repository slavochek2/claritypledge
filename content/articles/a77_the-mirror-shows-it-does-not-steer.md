# The Mirror Shows, It Doesn't Steer

> Working title, `[FOUNDER DECISION: title]`. Alternatives: "Upgrade the Harness, Not the Person" · "Universality Is Given; Its Exercise Is Not."

## Arc

**ARC-1 (Tested and Tightened).** The founder asked whether a tool could tweak a person toward the state that maximizes win-win. Tested against Deutsch's own frame, the bold version broke; the version that survived is narrower and buildable.

## Idea

**Starting point:** Deutsch says people are universal explainers. Universality is a jump, not a scale, so you can't make someone "closer" to it. What varies is how well it is exercised, which is cultural (anti-rational memes, static vs. dynamic cultures) and technological (external memory and tools count as part of the explainer). *Universality is given; its exercise is cultural and technological.* That is the layer ClarityPledge works on.

**When to verify, as an inequality:** check when *p(misunderstanding) × cost of acting on it > cost of checking.* Observable signs raise each term (abstract words, little shared context, agreement that came too fast; irreversible or dependent decisions, late feedback). Cheap paraphrase lowers the right-hand side. The dangerous case, both sides sure they understood, triggers none of the signs, so some decision classes need verification by default.

**Where the bold version broke ("tweak the person toward win-win"):** who sets the target controls the person; moving someone toward a state is thinking for them, which Deutsch would call manipulation however kind; a predictor favors people staying predictable, and the moments that matter are the creative ones it can't predict; once win-win is measured, Goodhart turns it into smooth agreement.

**What survived, via the founder's one-word correction ("harness"):** change the target from the person to the harness around them, as in AI systems: context, tools, memory, what reaches the model. A harness can surface the other's confirmed intended meaning, flag when you are about to reject a claim nobody made, and keep a shared record so understanding accumulates. Three conditions keep it honest: the person owns and can switch it off; it optimizes verified comprehension, not win-win; it sharpens disagreement instead of smoothing it.

**From 2026-10-18 ("Epistemology as personal harness"):** epistemology is not the harness but an upgrade to it. Human failure modes in harness terms rather than diagnoses: dogmatism = a system prompt no evidence can override; rambling = no stop condition; not listening = incoming context overwritten by your own prior output. Predicting which points the other would agree with is a verifier step most human harnesses skip.

## Why article-worthy

The mirror agent is a live product line. This piece states the line it must not cross (steering) and why, in terms an AI-literate reader already uses, and it carries a concrete design rule: free to inspect, owned by the user, scored on comprehension.
