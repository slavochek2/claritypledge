# Claude Setup Review

**Format:** Discussion group
**Audience:** Coders with AI, solopreneurs (Chiang Mai, local)
**Status:** Idea

## The Problem

Solo developers using Claude Code face three connected pain points:

1. **"I don't know what I'm missing"** — Set up CLAUDE.md and some skills, but no visibility into best practices or what others are doing
2. **"My setup is messy"** — Accumulated skills/instructions over time, now disorganized, duplicative, or contradictory
3. **"I want to level up"** — Working setup but want to learn advanced patterns from the community

## Discussion Format

1. **Reading material** (pre-work)
2. **Open questions** (framing)
3. **Discussion** (group)

## Reading Material

Participants prepare by:
- Exporting their CLAUDE.md (or equivalent)
- Listing their custom skills/agents
- Noting what works well and what frustrates them

## Open Questions

- What's the most valuable thing in your CLAUDE.md?
- What pattern did you discover that changed how you work?
- What's still frustrating or messy?
- What do you wish you knew earlier?

## Discussion Topics

- Comparing setups: what are the common patterns?
- Spotting gaps: what's missing from most setups?
- Best practices: what separates good from great?
- Meta question: should there be a tool that does this analysis automatically?

## Future Product Idea

The discussion itself might validate a product concept:

> A system where users upload their CLAUDE.md, skills, agents. The system analyzes them, asks clarifying questions, compares with best practices, and finds improvement opportunities.

**Value prop:** "Get a senior dev to review your AI workflow" — but automated.

**Who would pay?**
- Solo devs wanting optimization
- Teams wanting standardization
- Agencies setting up clients

This discussion is the first step: understand the problem space before building anything.
