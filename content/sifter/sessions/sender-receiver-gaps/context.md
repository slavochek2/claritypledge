## Key Concepts (User's Language)

- **Sender-receiver communication model**
- **Three asymmetries/gaps:**
  1. Intent → Sent message (speaker doesn't always say what they mean)
  2. Sent → Received (lost in translation)
  3. Received → Guessed intent (listener's interpretation)
- **Illusion of understanding** — people think they got it but didn't
- **Illusion of transparency** — thinking others can see your intent
- **Active listening training** — most never had it
- **Turn-taking** — both talk, nobody verifies = parallel monologues
- **Miscalibration** — confidence doesn't match actual understanding
- **Exhaustion** — always being the one who checks, always the listener
