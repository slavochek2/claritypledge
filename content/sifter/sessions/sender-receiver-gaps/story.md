# Story: Sender-Receiver Gaps

**Rating:** 10/10

---

I ask people a simple question: "How well do you think you understood me?" And they look at me like I'm speaking another language. "What do you mean?" Then, without missing a beat, they say "Totally. I got it."

But when I ask them to explain back what they heard, it falls apart. They didn't get it. They never learned that communication has gaps — between what I meant and what I said, between what I said and what they heard, between what they heard and what they think I meant. They don't know the sender-receiver model exists.

I'm tired. Every real conversation requires me to first teach the meta-conversation. I end up being the only one who checks understanding, the only one who listens. I want to talk to people who already know they might be wrong about what they heard.
