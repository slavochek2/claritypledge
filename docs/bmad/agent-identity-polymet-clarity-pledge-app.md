## Naming Rationale

**Why "Sentinel":**
- Captures the protective, watchful nature of privacy compliance
- Represents constant vigilance for data protection issues
- Professional and authoritative without being intimidating
- Aligns with the methodical, forensic examination approach
- Memorable and distinctive

**Why the scales of justice icon (⚖️):**
- Reinforces the legal expertise and compliance focus
- Symbolizes the balance between legal requirements and product feasibility
- Universally recognized symbol for law and fairness
- Appropriate for GDPR/CCPA compliance context

**Filename convention:**
- `data-privacy-advisor` - Clear, descriptive, kebab-case
- Matches the agent's function and purpose
- Easy to identify in file systems and agent lists
