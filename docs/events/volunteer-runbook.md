# Volunteer Runbook — Clarity Night

Your main job: **get every guest into the event room and prepared** before the first round. The host runs the evening.

## At the door, each guest

1. **Badge on.** Recorder? **Give them the USB mic.**
2. **Into the event room**, either way:
   - their email **"Starting in 15 minutes: join …"** → tap the button (logs them in), or
   - the event page on claritypledge.com → **Event room**.
3. **Prepare** in the room: tapping **Read summary** is quickest; the video only with headphones or in a quiet corner.
4. **Set a position on every statement.**

Late? They sit down and join **the next round**. Tell them so.

## During the rounds

- Help people **find their table** and **take their role**: speaker, listener, observer; **switch** speaker and listener when the round says so.
- Encourage the **observer** to step in, and people to **ring the bell**.
- Keep them **on the point**: talk about the statement, not around it.
- Nobody stands alone: walk them to a table with space.

## Recording

- Someone doesn't want to be recorded? Show the recorder **Pause / Resume** on their phone.
- If asked why: it's for research, and any recording can be deleted at any time.
- Never ask the room who objects, never point anyone out.

## End: remind the host

- **Group photo** (ask first; anyone can step out).
- Collect **badges and mics** (count them).

**Don't:** photograph people without asking, or repeat what you overheard.
