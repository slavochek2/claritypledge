# E-Essay-Publishing: Recognition via Content

## Experiment Protocol

**What we're testing:** Can essays build recognition in AI/rationalist communities?

**Method:**
1. Publish 3+ essays over 3 months
2. Share on LessWrong, HN, Twitter/X
3. Track: readers, comments, shares, inbound
4. Measure: recognition signals

**Topics:**
- Calibration infrastructure for personal AI
- Speaker verification as ground truth for alignment
- Metacomprehension accuracy in human-AI interaction
- Build-in-public learnings

## Measurements

- **Readers per essay** (views, unique visitors)
- **Engagement** (comments, shares, citations)
- **Inbound mentions** ("you're the calibration expert")
- **Community discussion** (LessWrong threads, HN comments)

## Success Threshold

- ≥50 readers per essay (average)
- ≥5 meaningful discussions
- ≥1 inbound mention
