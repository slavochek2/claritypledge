# Facilitator Guide

**For:** Slava (and future facilitators) running calibration workshops and sessions.
**Last updated:** 2026-03-22

---

## Workshop Format: False Belief Curriculum

### Setup (Before Workshop)

- Pre-file 8 false belief stories + extracted points on ClarityPledge (P567)
- Each false belief is the inverse of one of your 8 calibration points
- Take "strongly disagree" position on each false belief point
- Select 3-4 false beliefs for the workshop (don't try all 8 — depth > breadth)

### Flow (Per False Belief — ~15 min each)

1. **SURFACE:** Ask the opening question (e.g., "When someone says 'you don't understand me,' what do they mean?"). Let participants answer naturally.
2. **POSITION:** Show the false belief point on screen. Ask participants to rate agreement. Most will agree 8-10. This is their current belief.
3. **STORY:** Present your counter-story. Let them read it on their device (card UI) or present it verbally. The story explains WHY the false belief is wrong — with evidence, personal experience, or both.
4. **VERIFY:** Participants use comprehension slider on your story (P561). "How well do you understand Slava's reasoning?" This is NOT agreement — it's comprehension.
5. **SWITCH:** After verified understanding, ask participants to re-rate position on the false belief point. Many will switch. The position switch IS the calibration moment.
6. **REFLECT:** Ask: "What situations would have been different if you'd known this earlier?" "Can you think of a specific time this false belief cost you something?" "How might this false belief in others affect your relationships?"
7. **FILE:** Invite participants to file their own story: their past false belief, what it cost them (or could cost them), and how predictably removing it changes things.

### Post-Workshop

- Ask the qualifying question verbally: "Did this reveal something you didn't know?" If yes: "How much could a misunderstanding like this cost you?"
- For pairs: offer de-risking package — "I can do this with your co-founder, on your real decisions"
- Filed participant stories = testimonials + calibration data + content flywheel

## Emotional Safety

Before any verification exercise, check emotional readiness:
- Ask both participants: "On a scale of 0-10, how well can you regulate your emotions right now?"
- Make this visible to both parties — creates common knowledge about emotional state
- If someone rates low (<5), steer toward Level 2 topics (opinions, not values/fears)
- Paraphrasing requires emotional regulation — if the listener can't hold their reactions, the protocol breaks

---

## Dos

- Steer topics to Level 3+ (values, vision) — shallow topics = shallow reveals
- Use /live as YOUR diagnostic tool — pairs don't learn it
- File stories/points after each session (you are the scribe)
- Message the frustrated individual, deliver to the pair
- Every session = labeled calibration data
- Default to booking links and written offers, not exploratory calls
- Pay-what-it's-worth for sessions (communicate upfront)

## Don'ts

- Don't ask completed pairs for payment retroactively
- Don't run sessions on abstract/philosophical topics — values and real decisions only
- Don't confuse curiosity ("that's cool") with pain ("this cost us X")
- Don't present your points as truth — present them as your position, let the protocol do the work
- Don't skip the reflection step after position switch — that's where pain surfaces
- Don't try all 8 false beliefs in one workshop — 3-4 with depth beats 8 at surface

## Session Types

| Type | Format | /live? | False beliefs? |
|------|--------|--------|----------------|
| Workshop (1-to-many) | Group, 60-90 min | Optional (for recording) | Yes — primary mechanism |
| Facilitated session (1-to-2) | Co-founder pair, 45 min | Yes (gap measurement) | Optional — can use their real topic |
| De-risking session (paid) | Co-founder pair, 2x 60 min | Yes | First session: false beliefs. Second: their real decisions. |
