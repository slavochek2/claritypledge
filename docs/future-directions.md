## FD-1: Agent-to-Agent & Agent-Human Sensemaking

**Added:** 2026-02-05
**Trigger:** Moltbook/OpenClaw research — 1.5M AI agents on a social network, open-source agent framework with 114K GitHub stars

### The Ideas

1. **Agents using Points/Stories to coordinate** — Agents could use the agree/disagree system to test each other's understanding, stake positions, verify commitments

2. **"Calibrated Human" as agent trust signal** — If an agent's human is miscalibrated, the agent may be executing misunderstood instructions. A human's calibration score could become a trust credential for their agents. "This agent's human demonstrably understands what they're asking for."

3. **Agent-human interface verification** — Agents verify they understood their human's intent; humans verify they understood what agents told them

4. **Agents organizing human events** — Agents could facilitate calibration sessions, match humans for verification, promote practice

### Why Keep

- The "calibrated human as agent trust signal" insight is novel — addresses real agent-human interface problem
- If agent ecosystems grow, being infrastructure for agent-human sensemaking could be valuable
- Aligns with theory of change: verification at scale, √N bridges
- Low-cost API optionality could position for this wave

### Why Not Now

- **Zero retention with humans** — can't fantasize about 1.5M agents while 10 humans won't return
- **Agents don't have the problem we solve** — no ego, no strategic laziness, no social vulnerability; they can share state directly
- **Moltbook is a month old** — might explode, get regulated, or collapse from security failures
- **Scope expansion under uncertainty** — classic founder pattern of avoiding hard validation work

### Revisit When

- Human retention validated (>30% return in month 2 without invitation)
- Stories + AI verification built and working (H-AI validated)
- Agent ecosystem stabilizes (clear winners, security addressed)
- Someone with agent expertise wants to collaborate on this direction

## FD-2: Investor Angle — Calibration as Pre-Investment Due Diligence

**Added:** 2026-02-18
**Status:** Parked — revisit at Month 4-6 checkpoint

### The Idea

Investors routinely misjudge founders' self-awareness and communication clarity during diligence. Hypothesis: "Investors will pay for a calibration session with a founder they're considering backing, to verify self-awareness and communication clarity before writing a check."

**Specific version:** Founder + lead investor do a /live session on the founder's theory of the business. Investor rates: "Did I understand what they were trying to say?" Founder rates: "Did I communicate my vision clearly?" The gap becomes a signal attached to diligence notes.

### Why Keep

- Investors are a high-value ICP (enterprise pricing, B2B motion)
- Due diligence is a natural "when to use this" moment (high stakes, decision point)
- VC credibility could accelerate both Recognition and Coaching tracks
- Precedent: investors already pay for reference checks, background checks, expert calls

### Why Not Now

- Coaching track not yet validated — can't sell investors on calibration before proving it works with 10+ founder pairs
- Depends on C2/C3 data — "10 founders improved calibration by X%" is the credibility play
- VC sales cycle is slow (3-6 months) — premature before coaching traction exists
- Different motion entirely (selling to investors vs. founders)

### Revisit When

- C2 hit: first 10+ paid sessions, calibration data shows measurable improvement
- C3 hit: 5+ retainer pairs with month-2 retention data
- Dashboard ready: aggregate calibration deltas visible across sessions
- Reach out to 2-3 VCs in warm network and ask: "Would you pay for this pre-investment?"
