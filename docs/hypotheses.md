#### H-FounderWince: A seed–A founder with an active key hire feels the wince and pulls toward a room that fixes it
*Alias: H-FounderWince (formerly H-SolopreneurWince, renamed 2026-07-03 — the ICP is the individual founder as a single-person-funnel unit, with or without a co-founder; "solopreneur" wrongly implied solo. See [decisions.md](decisions.md) 2026-07-03 [product] and [definitions.md](definitions.md) "Founder (ICP)". Append-only decisions history retains the old ID.)*
**Bet (2026-07-01, market-focus shift to founders):** The active market is **founders / solo AI builders** (single-person funnel, reachable, growing — chosen on reachability, not because only they need it). The wedge Problem is a **will** problem, not a skill one: a founder (**with or without a co-founder** — the root is intrapersonal) bets months on a belief no one stress-tested and **won't reveal the gaps in their own understanding when the truth might reject what they love** — a co-founder often shares the blind spot or the hard conversation is avoided, so a peer's challenge lands as *attack*, a customer's politeness as *validation*, and the bet never gets tested. Value = verify-then-challenge (challenge made *usable* because understanding is verified first): **unlock the will → build the muscle gradually** (peers on far-off topics → own product → real customers) **→ de-risk the bet**. Confirms the canvas's will-primacy (5-move Move 1; H-AffectiveHonesty). The cohort/room is the plausible product (you can't verify-then-challenge alone), tested for demand by a 1:1 test.
**Test (discovery-first):** ~5 live 1:1 diagnostics from ~50 approached — use the method *on them* to clarify + quantify their pain (customer-convos/week + blocker). Watch: does a real **wince** land, and do they **pull** toward a room? Also tests *whether verification is even their real pain* (vs team / support / loneliness). Sell = answer the pull with the room; money enters at rung 2, not the call.
**Transform if (founder-set threshold):** below the pull threshold (e.g. <3/5 pull), OR the real pain is consistently elsewhere → re-cut the wedge or the value-prop. Open sub-bets, tested at later rungs: recurring engine ("why stay ~$99/mo", rung 3); R₀ / distribution (rung 3+); rung-1 mechanic (1:1 vs small-peer-session).
**Retention loop (named mechanism, UNTESTED — the "why stay" sub-bet):** clarity → will → action → new gaps → clarity. Acting on a clarified problem statement generates new gaps, which reopens the need — this loop is the recurring-need mechanism behind the ~$99/mo sustaining bet ("evolving bets keep the need recurring"). **Falsifier:** if rung-1-completing founders do not return with a new gap within a membership cycle, the loop does not drive retention and the sustaining bet fails.
**Transferability sub-bet (H-ProtocolTransfers, UNTESTED — the "business vs job" risk, possibly *primary*):** clarity production transfers *off the founder* — two members produce a clarity shift in each other via the protocol with the founder absent. The founder can already produce clarity 1:1; whether the *product* (protocol + onboarding) lets peers replicate it is unproven, and it — not WTP — decides scalable-product vs founder-delivered-consulting (the 5-hours-per-person ceiling that killed the pair model). **Read in delivery, not at the close:** a paid batch where the founder ran the letters and orchestrated the exchange proves WTP only; transferability is read across the 3-month cohort, so delivery must be *designed* to test it. **Falsifier:** if by ~month 3 no member pair produces a clarity shift in each other with zero founder involvement, transferability fails and the model reverts to founder-delivered consulting. See [decisions.md](decisions.md) 2026-07-03 [product].
**Acquisition sub-bet (H-FunnelRepeatability, UNTESTED — DEFERRED, phase-2):** qualified members can be sourced *repeatably* at viable cost/effort **without** a one-time warm channel (e.g. a single accelerator intro handing over a cohort). A first warm-sourced batch proves demand + delivery, NOT acquisition — that batch's sourcing was free and non-repeatable (same risk as cohort-mismatch: both need continuous qualified inflow). **Falsifier:** if batch-2 sourcing (post-warm-channel) cannot fill a cohort of qualified founders at sustainable cost/effort, the product-led-growth premise fails and the model caps at founder-hustled cohorts. **Deferred:** not an active test now — recorded so a positive batch-1 close is not misread as acquisition proof.
**Domain sharpening (2026-07-04, UNTESTED — think-first, pre-interview):** the wedge's recurring domain is **customer-development / problem-validation clarity** (not "clarity on anything" — a will-problem sold as a meta-skill is retention-hostile; the recurring domain is the retention engine, will is only the entry gate). Central positioning **bet**: *the illusion of recursive understanding is customer development's central failure mode* (founders hear what they want, believe they understood the customer, were wrong) — CP attacks it directly. Object of understanding is **layered**: the founder's own problem statement AND the customer. Community = the **upstream** peer room on a cost-ascending ladder (AI → peers → customers) with a **stop-rule ejecting members toward customers**. **Falsifier (folds into the discovery test):** if founders' felt pull is on execution/funding/loneliness rather than customer/problem clarity, OR customer-dev is not the domain that pulls → re-cut the domain (the wedge survives, the domain label doesn't). Disqualifier correction: "believes he has no customer-understanding gaps" is the *illusion signature* (see H-WTP-Pain 2026-06-18), NOT a disqualifier. #### H-ChampionYield: The highest-yield buyer is the champion who buys to raise OTHERS' will — NOT the founder fixing their own gap
> **Candidate — NOT P0, n=1, not promoted.** Filed adjacent to H-FounderWince because the *tension between them* is the point (see "Relationship" below). Placement here is for that adjacency, not a claim of P0 priority — the summary-table status is **Candidate (n=1)**.

**Bet (2026-07-09, UNTESTED, n=1):** The highest-yield primary buyer is not the founder-with-the-problem but a **champion/evangelist** who **already has the skill and the will** and buys to **increase the will of the people around them** — spread verified-understanding culture (e.g. a CEO who wants it to become how their org/circle communicates). Trigger signal, observed once (one pilot interviewee): a spontaneous *"I wish others knew what I know."*
**Why it might hold:** (a) "increase others' will" is **more legible AND more countable** than self-skill improvement — a second adoption is a discrete event, not a subjective delta (which is the very thing CP exists to instrument, so it can't double as the value read); (b) **internal consistency** — the pledge's own stated aim is *"change what others feel safe requesting from you,"* i.e. it is already a will-of-others mechanism, so the interviewee confirmed the mechanism the product already bets on; (c) **retention logic** — an evangelist's goal (spread to others) is **open-ended → never completes → structural retention**, a distinct answer to the known retention-hostile critique ([decisions.md](decisions.md) 2026-07-04) that the self-improver has a *completion problem* (solve it → they leave).
**The load-bearing risk (transitivity):** "I converted them, so they'll convert others" is unproven and is the whole bet. The champion's conversion of a third party happens **without the facilitator**; the champion is **not the founder** (less skill/time/incentive at the pitch); community practice improves **skill, not recruitment**. These are reasons-to-believe (raise the prior), NOT evidence (do not move the measurement).
**Test:** watch for a **2nd/3rd spontaneous "I wish others knew" signal** across pilot interviews before promoting (n=1 rule). Then: does a champion actually **cause a second signed pledge traceable to them**?
**Falsifier:** a champion signs enthusiastically but produces **zero second-adoption traceable to them** ⇒ value prop is vanity, kill it. **Metric that matters = a traceable second signed pledge, not enthusiasm.**
**Relationship to H-FounderWince (why not folded in):** opposite entry mechanism. H-FounderWince's engine is **low will ← believed-high skill**, moved only by **live disconfirmation** (the audit-demo wince). The champion is already high-will/high-skill and needs **no wince**. Different buyer, different funnel — the models would contradict if merged. Open buyer-identity question: winced-founder-with-the-problem vs champion-who-spreads-it. The "practice together / share what worked" community part is **H-Community-Retention** (existing), not new here.

**Bet (2026-06-29, market-focus shift):** The instrument's value in clinical practice is a structured, verifiable, **client-side, transferable** protocol for *receiving a challenge* — success = demonstrated **cognitive** grasp **decoupled from agreement and from emotional resonance** ("understood-and-rejected, both knowing why" is a clean success). Mechanism = **epistemic trust via verifiable structural commitment**: the pledge (CPA) *prevents wrong-reason mistrust* for the client and *enables honesty* for the therapist — a structural/teachable route to opening the epistemic channel, distinct from Fonagy's relational-attunement route. **Will is primary** (pre-committing to the CPA while calm produces the will to use the protocol at the breaking point instead of churning) — the differentiator vs PREP's speaker-listener (paraphrase-verify mechanic, no will mechanism). Demand side = distrusting would-be therapy clients (landing + clarity-practice-community); **payer = the therapist** (licensing). The *channel* axis (H-CoachChannel) is separate from this *market* axis.
**Shoulders (cite, never claim as discovery):** alliance rupture-repair (Eubanks/Muran/Safran 3RS — confrontation markers predict dropout); epistemic trust (Fonagy/Campbell — ETMCQ); PREP speaker-listener (closest prior art). The problem is textbook; the unoccupied wedge is the agreement-decoupled + will-primary + client-side + transferable combination.
**Test:** (a) **Demand** — qualify 2+ would-be clients who distrust therapists (low ETMCQ / therapist-tweaked variant), show the proposition, measure pull ("can I have it now," not stated interest); (b) **Supply PoC** — one therapist tries the protocol with a consenting real client at a post-felt-understood challenge moment (therapist-tries-a-technique, normal practice, no IRB), observable = client uses the clarity channel at the breaking point vs ghosts/complains; (c) **Therapist interview (learning, not demand)** — densest source of client language + market size: ask *of clients who leave, what fraction leave over surfaced disagreement vs quiet drift* (≈ real market size), and *can you introduce this early on small disagreements, or are clients only reachable once trust is already strained?*
**Transform if (two gates — *either* falsifies, not both together):** (1) **mechanism gate (core, load-bearing):** at a post-felt-understood rupture, a therapist running the protocol with a consenting client sees the client ghost/complain as usual, or it "bounces" (vigilance too high to engage) → the will-primary / epistemic-trust mechanism fails (also fails if buy-in is only available once trust is already strained — the capability ladder can't be built, since the tool can't first appear at the big rupture); this alone falsifies the core bet (revert to cofounder-dormant or a new market). (2) **demand gate (market):** distrusting would-be clients show no pull on the proposition (no "can I have it now") → the client-side market is wrong; this alone only redirects the go-to-market — the mechanism may still hold for a different buyer. (The earlier AND-framing was effectively unfalsifiable — any weak signal on either side kept the bet alive; split into independent gates 2026-06-30.)

#### H-LetterAsProduct: The public Clarity Letter scales the flip outside the room
**Bet:** A completable public Clarity Letter pre-collects the data /live operates on (positions on points and anti-points, sealed-bid understanding guesses, per-story comprehension self-ratings), making /live with the story author faster, cheaper, and more targeted than a workshop. The letter + author-present /live is the scale unit: the letter absorbs the workshop's preparation overhead so only the verification moment (paraphrase + confirm) requires both people present. The flip happens in /live; the letter makes /live reachable at scale.

#### H-WTP-Pain: Gap reveal produces urgency, not just curiosity
**Reframe (2026-06-10) — the binding constraint is *revelation*, not *conversion* (supersedes the Bet framing below).** An *important* gap, once genuinely surfaced between a pair, produces the desire to bridge it and to pay — that conversion is near-automatic; the residual blocker is frame-resistance ("buying de-risking = admitting we might break") and denial, not absence of urgency. So the falsifiable P0 question moves upstream: **can the product reliably and predictably surface important gaps the pair would not otherwise reveal — by lowering the cost and social risk of honest mutual revelation (safe proactive frame + async letter that decouples revelation from the charged live moment)?** WTP follows revelation of an *important, previously-unspoken* gap, not of a trivial or already-known one. Re-reads the evidence: "28 sessions, zero cost named" is better explained as *those sessions did not surface an important + previously-unspoken gap* (topic inadequacy — see H-TopicDepthGate, ~40% failed on topic) than as "important gaps don't produce urgency." Mechanism basis: the product makes honesty safe + cheap to reveal and bridge; it does not detect hidden gaps ([decisions.md](decisions.md) 2026-06-10 [product]). **Transform-if (two failure modes, kept distinct):** (1) across sessions where the protocol *did* surface an important + previously-unspoken gap, the pair still shows no urgency and won't pay → conversion is the real blocker after all; revert to the original framing. (2) the protocol *cannot* reliably surface important + unspoken gaps even with the safe frame + async letter → the value proposition fails upstream of WTP (the revelation mechanism, not pricing). The original Transform-if (10 pairs can't name a cost after a false-belief workshop) is retained below as the conversion-side probe.
**Bet:** Participants find the gap reveal interesting but "interesting" doesn't open wallets. Without felt pain ("this cost us X"), WTP stays near zero.
**Test:** After every gap reveal, ask: "When did a misunderstanding like this cost you something?" Track answers via P518 qualifying question.
**Transform if:** 10 pairs cannot name a concrete cost after experiencing their gap.
**Status (2026-03-18):** 28 sessions, zero pairs expressed that their gap was costing them anything. Reactions: curiosity, intellectual engagement, social warmth. No alarm, no urgency. This is the single most important hypothesis for commercial viability.
**Update (2026-03-21):** CE distribution channel deferred — will return with concrete forwardable offer. Async-first go-to-market adaptation: booking links and written offers instead of scheduling calls. Compounds the delay in testing WTP with revenue-qualified pairs.
**Update (2026-03-22):** False-belief facilitation technique identified as concrete testing mechanism. Workshop flow: surface participant's false belief via question → they position on false-belief point → present counter-story → verify understanding (P561 slider) → position switch → **reflection prompt: "What situations would have been different? What did holding this cost you?"** → they file story about their past false belief + cost. The reflection prompt IS the H-WTP-Pain test — not passively hoping pairs name a cost, but actively engineering the moment where broken belief connects to their relationship. See P567 (false belief curriculum) and [facilitator-guide.md](facilitator-guide.md).
**Risk (2026-03-23):** Purchase frame itself may repel — "buying a de-risking package = admitting our relationship might break." The "sophistication/prenup" reframe works only for founders who already self-identify as high-performing. Workshop reflection tests pain; conversion tests whether pain overcomes the frame resistance. Watch for this in workshop debrief.
**Insight (2026-03-24):** Clarity Letter three-step sequence may resolve the pricing chicken-and-egg. Letter 1 (opened during workshop) demonstrates value. /live verifies understanding and produces gap map. Letter 3 calibrates the commercial exchange using the skill just learned — PWIW with full comprehension context. The gap is legibility, not ability or willingness. Implication: the 10-letter test should measure not just "do they pay" but "can they name what they experienced." If they can name it, pricing follows. If they can't, no price point works. Byron Katie distinction: "find false beliefs" = crowded market (CBT, The Work, Street Epistemology); "find false beliefs about whether you understand each other" = unoccupied space.

#### H-ComprehensionTrust: Verified grounding raises a distinct trust component and gates debiasing uptake

#### H-NormFlip: Paraphrase-expectation shifts the norm-flip threshold
**Bet:** Once paraphrase-on-request becomes an *expected* response in a community (not just an available one), the threshold for the comprehension-verification norm to cascade drops below the ~25% critical mass predicted by Bicchieri/Centola. The expectation itself changes the learning-mode mix — not just adoption count, but the parameter that governs how all norms respond to evidence.
**Theoretical basis:** Nowak-Sigmund indirect reciprocity + Bicchieri normative expectations + Centola threshold cascade research. Surfaced 2026-05-19; founder endorsement: "this is our plan!!"
**Test:** Track paraphrase-expectation in workshop cohorts vs control. Measure post-workshop propagation rate to non-participants.
**Transform if:** Cohorts with explicit paraphrase-norm priming show no faster post-workshop adoption — the mechanism is wrong; revert to count-based threshold model.
**Status (2026-05-22):** Conjecture only. Blocked by workshop volume (need cohort sample size).
**Test mechanism (2026-05-26) — Minimum Clarity Letter as instrumented field experiment.** A minimal letter where **antipoint = the antinorm** ("asking for paraphrase is rude/slow"), **story = the definition of recursive understanding + the falsifying observation that nobody actually judges**, **point = the norm** ("paraphrase before continuing"), plus pre/post belief capture. Propagates virally while measuring: send rate (propagation coefficient), paraphrase-completion rate (compliance), rejection rate (explicit refusal vs. silent non-response — distinct signals), and the pre/post belief diff (the data). Low rejection + high completion becomes an honest dynamic nudge in future iterations ("N% paraphrased within 24h"). Caveats to design in: severe selection bias at small N (state it — strengthens credibility), distinguish received/read/responded, pre-register predictions before data. **Research direction (founder marker):** run a Bicchieri 2×2 factorial vignette to classify whether the comprehension-verification gap is a *norm*, a *habit*, or *absence* in target populations — and measure strength vs. stability across environments. Spec candidate; see [decisions.md](decisions.md). Untested.
**Deferred to phase-2 (2026-06-02):** Norm-cascade propagation is a phase-2+ multiplier (parked alongside the badge cascade, franchise, and club). Build after the first paid loop closes — it is not part of the near-term coach-distribution proof. [decisions.md](decisions.md) 2026-06-02 [product].
