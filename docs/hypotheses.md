### Entry conventions (added 2026-08-07, P1026 — forward-only)

These apply to **new or materially-updated** hypotheses. Existing entries are **not** retroactively rewritten. Markdown + named bold fields — no schema, no JSON.

- **Novel prediction** (required) — *what does this framing predict that its predecessor framing did not?* Stated **before** the test, with corroboration status: `pending | corroborated | failed`. A framing whose only content is explaining the anomaly that killed its predecessor is an **accommodation**, not a successor — and a programme built of accommodations degenerates while looking healthy on a falsified-per-unit-time scoreboard. Falsifiers guard against dogmatism; novel predictions guard against degeneration. **Both are required — neither substitutes for the other.** Corroborated entries are promoted into the [progressivity ledger](research-programme.md#progressivity-ledger).
  - Write it before the test or grade it a **retrodiction**. The same sentence written afterwards is an accommodation, and grading it novel defeats the ledger's only purpose.
- **Programme tag** (required) — `business | scientific | both`. Many are both (e.g. H-PopperianIncrement). One scoreboard, two programmes; the tag is why a separate file was rejected.
- **Core-adjacent** (flag, only where it applies) — marks a belt hypothesis whose failure **cannot** be absorbed by segment/channel adjustment. Every core-adjacent entry states an explicit **ad-hoc-ness budget**: how many auxiliary adjustments it may absorb before the next failure escalates to a **core-hit review** instead of another belt patch. Without a stated budget the flag does nothing — absorbing indefinitely is the default it exists to stop.

> **Absence of these fields on an older entry is not a defect.** The convention applies forward. `/docs-strategy-update` flags a *new or updated* hypothesis missing its Novel-prediction field; it does not flag the backlog.

#### H-AbsentCounterparty: A written problem reaches someone who was never in the room, and comes back contested

**Bet:** The Clarity Letter's value is a function of counterparty **absence**. **This executes a handoff made on 2026-08-12 that was never carried out.** That entry recorded the successor and routed it here; a grep of this file returned zero hits until today. It is registered now because the instrument exists: P1180 shipped `/slava:problem:submit` on 2026-08-31.

#### H-ComprehensionTrust: Verified grounding raises a distinct trust component and gates debiasing uptake
**Bet:** Verified grounding (the paraphrase-and-confirm protocol) raises a specific *comprehension-trust* component — "I can predict that meaning reliably transfers between us" (a sub-component of McAllister's cognition-based trust) — and sits *upstream* of interpersonal debiasing: you cannot correct someone's bias through a channel whose meaning never grounded.
**Test — four pre-registered predictions (pre-committed 2026-06-04; this git commit is the timestamp). Each is defined by its control condition:**
1. *Dissociation from solo debiasing.* A solo AOT/debiasing intervention will not improve dyadic grounding calibration; the verification protocol will. Disconfirmed if solo debiasing improves calibration as much as the protocol.
2. *Upstream of debiasing.* The same debiasing instruction produces greater bias reduction when preceded by a verified-grounding step than without it. Disconfirmed if grounding-first yields no better uptake than debiasing alone.
3. *Trust, not rapport.* Verified grounding raises a comprehension-trust measure above a collaborative task that builds equal rapport without verifying meaning. Disconfirmed if the rapport-only control raises it equally.
4. *The proxy claim.* Pre-session confidence in being understood correlates weakly with measured accuracy, and that gap narrows after the protocol. Disconfirmed if felt-understanding already tracks accuracy tightly beforehand.
**Design correction (2026-08-03, scoped same day) — the battery must be a MEDIATION design, not a before/after design.** The current battery measures pre vs post, which answers *did anything change* and cannot answer *did it change **because** comprehension was verified*. Three additions required: (a) comprehension accuracy measured as its own variable, independently of the outcome; (b) a control arm with **matched attention and rapport but no verification step** (prediction 3's rapport-only control gestures at this — promote it to a design requirement across the battery); (c) enough measurement points to test whether the outcome effect survives when the accuracy change is accounted for. **This is independently correct mediation design and would be correct with zero couples citations.**
**Scope note — do NOT read this as inherited pessimism.** The prompt for the correction was the couples literature's own failure ([Bradbury & Bodenmann 2020](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-clinpsy-071519-020546): *"changes in observed behavior do not mediate treatment effects on satisfaction"*, citing Williamson et al. 2016; *"consistent, lasting, and consequential changes in couple communication have not been documented"*; [Lavner, Karney & Bradbury 2016](https://doi.org/10.1111/jomf.12301): both causal arrows weak). **That failure is specific to observer-coded behavior → self-reported satisfaction over multi-year lags.** CP's mediator is speaker-adjudicated paraphrase accuracy — checkable against the speaker's stated meaning — and its outcome is decision quality over weeks. The reverse-arrow worry also inverts here: [theory-of-change.md](theory-of-change.md) already holds closeness-communication bias, which predicts that feeling good about a relationship *inflates* the illusion rather than shrinking the measured gap. **The design lesson transfers; the pessimism about whether the mediator is measurable at all does not. Do not pre-concede it.**
**Honest limits on the pilot (added against the "small operator can do what large trials cannot" claim, which was asserted and not argued).** At n≈10 sessions the study is badly underpowered for a bootstrapped indirect-effect test, so a null on mediation would be uninformative and the falsifier below is not reachable at that n. What 10 sessions *can* do: (i) validate an accuracy measure that is **not** speaker self-report, and (ii) produce effect-size estimates to size a real mediation study. **Requirement (a) is currently unmet** — under the Min architecture the mediator measure *is* a participant self-report, sharing method variance and demand characteristics with any felt-outcome measure taken minutes later, and the "seller as judge" problem already recorded under H-AgreementSubstitution returns in full when the vendor collects it. Naming an **independent accuracy coder** is a precondition, not a detail. `[FOUNDER DECISION: who codes accuracy]`
**Falsifier this adds (at adequate n):** if the outcome effects hold at full strength once the accuracy change is accounted for, verified comprehension is not the mediator and the theory of change routes through something else (attention, hope, the facilitator). See [decisions.md](decisions.md) 2026-08-03 and `content/articles/a63_the-critic-who-built-a-paraphrase-gate.md`.
**Transform if:** Predictions 1 AND 2 disconfirmed across the 10-session pilot — the distinct-failure-class claim (meaning-closure ≠ validity-closure) and the upstream claim both fall; the framework reverts to plain synthesis of Kruglanski + Clark.
**Status (2026-06-04):** New. Emerged from the 2026-06-03 grounding-closure synthesis (Kruglanski closure motivation × Clark grounding criterion = premature grounding closure). #### H-AffectiveHonesty: Affective safety is the integrity precondition for honest Min estimates
**Bet:** In a cold or status-threatening room, people round their comprehension self-estimates *up* to save face — so the Min bound becomes a lie and the instrument reads garbage. Affective safety isn't the soft opposite of rigor; it's the condition under which the Min number is honest. The facilitator's "how important is it right now that the other agrees?" **Test:** In the 10-session pilot, compare self-rated comprehension vs speaker-verified accuracy across rooms varying in measured safety (the agreement-importance metric as proxy). Signature: low-safety rooms show *larger* upward inflation of self-rated comprehension.
**Transform if:** Self-rating inflation is flat across safety levels — affective state doesn't move estimate honesty; the instrument is robust to a cold room and this caveat is dropped.
**Status (2026-06-26):** New. From a 2026-06-22 peer-operator conversation (an operator who manufactures a real psychological state on a false map). Bears on instrument *validity*, not just delivery.
**Feeds into:** H-ComprehensionTrust battery (prediction 4, the proxy claim), a40 ("The Precondition You Verified Away").
**Affective friction is stated openly, and it is separable from the epistemic verdict (2026-07-30, UNTESTED, n=1 — SOFTENED same day, see below):** a peer session produced a subject who names the friction plainly and repeatedly — *"I really want, but I don't want to do it, it's onerous"*, *"very difficult, difficult… It's why you eat something bitter"*, *"If it's very painful"* — while separately reporting the exercise produced nothing: *"it's not that it makes me uncomfortable… It doesn't reveal anything very. This is my problem, so then I don't believe it."*
**What this does and does not establish.** It confirms that affective friction is real, self-reported, and survives repeated cost reduction (the ask fell from ~10 hours to 90 minutes to 10 minutes; he still declined, so the objection is not time). **It does NOT establish that the stated reason is post-hoc.** A first draft read the two statements as incompatible and inferred a rationalization signature — friction first, reason manufactured after. **Corrected the same day:** they are not incompatible. *"The doing is unpleasant AND it produced nothing, so I don't believe in it"* is a coherent cost-benefit report. Inference was presented as observation; the stronger claim is withdrawn.
**What to carry into a session:** treat affective friction and the stated reason as **two separate readings** rather than assuming one explains the other. Do not argue a subject out of the stated reason before checking whether the friction is the actual blocker — but do not assume it is, either.
**Falsifier:** subjects who report friction *and* report a null result adopt at the same rate as subjects who report only the null ⟹ friction adds nothing to the refusal and this is a distinction without a difference. See [decisions.md](decisions.md) 2026-07-30 [product] (correction entry).

**Two levers move estimate honesty without touching room temperature (2026-08-03, UNTESTED, n=0 — conjecture, not finding).** This bet named only one lever: how safe the room feels. Two others are separable from it, and one of them ships with the outreach campaign whether or not it is tested.

- **Elicitation wording (accuracy lever) — the swap is REJECTED, and the rejection is conjectural.** A proposed reframe replaces *"how much do you think you understand my intended meaning, 0–10?"* with *"how clear do you think I was?"*. It genuinely unsticks people, because it flips **who pays the face cost** — a low number stops being a confession about the listener and becomes a report about the speaker. Rejected as a *swap* on three grounds: (a) **clarity has no ground truth** — clarity is attributed to a message relative to one listener, comprehension is a state inside that listener, and this instrument's target is the second; (b) what a listener can actually rate is apparent well-formedness, i.e. the **fluency heuristic** — which is precisely what inflates the comprehension illusion, so the clarity wording is predicted *more* vulnerable to false highs, and the self-sealing-illusion account says the **top** of the scale is the dangerous case; (c) *"you weren't clear"* is the standard polite carrier for *"I don't accept your point"*, so **disagreement leaks into the number** and contaminates the partition H-DisagreementDecomposition depends on. Further bound: the disclosure premise may **invert** with a friend, mentor or client, where the low number becomes a criticism rather than a confession — so the reframe may be a local optimum for the population it was informally tried on.
- **The permission line (friction lever, separable).** *"I'm fine with a low number — that's what tells me where to be clearer."* Naming 0 out loud makes the floor thinkable; people rarely say a number they have not been shown is allowed. Two constraints: it is a **promise, not a framing** — the **first low number received is the actual calibration event**, and if the facilitator's face does anything, every subsequent number inflates and stays inflated; and it is said **once at the open**, since repeated it becomes ritual. Wording constraint: *"that's what tells me where to be clearer"*, not *"the only way for me to be more clear"* (an overclaim that re-centres the exchange on the facilitator).
- **The structural point:** friction and accuracy are controlled by **different levers**, so there is no trade. Keep the comprehension question as the measured variable; put the face-work into the permission line and the repair request (*"can you explain back what you think I meant, so I can see where I was unclear?"*).

**Falsifier:** alternate the two wordings across the next batch of sessions, run the paraphrase round, and score the paraphrase **independently of the rating**. If the clarity wording is both easier to answer *and* worse at predicting paraphrase failure, a diagnostic was traded for a social lubricant. Second falsifier: rooms that get the permission line but no facilitator-first score produce as many honest low numbers as rooms that get both ⟹ the demonstration is decoration and the grant does the work (touches H-NormFlip, a51).

**Novel prediction (P1026 forward-only format):** the permission line produces an **asymmetric, floor-specific** effect — it lowers reported comprehension at the *bottom* of the scale without moving the top, so measured variance increases **downward** and the accuracy gain concentrates in low-comprehension events. The rival affect-first / psychological-safety programme predicts a **uniform** warmth effect across the scale. Second, discriminating the wordings: **clarity ratings will track message-fluency measures rather than paraphrase accuracy, while comprehension ratings track paraphrase accuracy weakly but non-zero** — a dissociation the "it's all just social comfort" account does not predict. *Programme tag: both. Core-adjacent: no (belt).*

**Design note:** this falsifier already satisfies requirement (a) of the same-day mediation correction on the H-ComprehensionTrust battery — comprehension accuracy measured as its own variable, independently of the outcome.

**Evidence grade:** **UNTESTED, n=0**, conversation-sourced (2026-07-31). Zero tests ran in the source window; all four source conversations were pitch/copy iteration. The predicted ranking of the wordings is **conjecture, not finding** — *"people answer it more easily"* is **adoption, not validation**. See [decisions.md](decisions.md) 2026-08-03 [product]; narrative `content/articles/a59_who-pays-the-face-cost.md`.

- **The carrier mechanic — the facilitator fails first.** The highest-status person in the room says the first low number **about themselves**. Three independent routes converge, which is decent evidence it's load-bearing rather than a nice idea: **Edmondson** (safety comes from what the highest-status person does *first*, not what they permit), **Bicchieri** (norms spread via visible trendsetters entering the reference network's information set, not via pledges — consistent with "The act IS the broadcast", [lean-canvas.md](lean-canvas.md) §Channels & Flywheel), and **the installation problem itself** (someone has to make "3" survivable first). You cannot *declare* a score costless; you can only demonstrate it by paying nothing visible for a low one.
- **Falsifier:** rooms where the facilitator scores low first show no more honest low numbers (no smaller self-rating inflation gap vs speaker-verified accuracy) than rooms where they don't → the carrier mechanic is wrong; the norm needs a different vehicle. Feeds the H-AffectiveHonesty battery.
- **A SECOND carrier mechanic — the non-adopter as invoker (2026-08-12, founder-raised, `UNTESTED`).** Same mechanism as facilitator-fails-first, different vehicle: both work by **generating observable instances of a gap being admitted without consequence.** In a room that runs an opt-in, **anyone may ask anyone** — the commitment sits on the *answering* side (opt-ins owe a number or an explicit "not now, because X"; opt-outs owe nothing), never on the asking side. That is consistent with this entry's own norm statement — *"not giving a number when asked, without a good excuse, is the unsafe act"* — and an earlier draft of [p1055](../features/p1055_norm_measurement_instrument.md) that restricted invocation to opt-ins contradicted it and was retracted. **The claim:** letting a non-adopter *invoke* the protocol converts them faster than letting them only watch, because asking makes them the author of the disconfirming observation rather than its audience. Their "benefit without paying the price" is a free trial, not a leak. **Why the reciprocity objection does not bite here:** the one-way-status-transfer concern in item (b) below is about a ledger balancing over *repeated* encounters; a single session has no ledger, and the asymmetry ends when the room does. **Asymmetry that makes it safe:** adopters want others to adopt; non-adopters have no incentive to recruit non-adopters, so there is no opposing coalition for a gate to defend against. **Falsifier:** across the first events, non-adopters who invoke the protocol on someone convert (opt in later, or return) at no higher rate than non-adopters who only observe ⟹ authorship is not the lever and watching is sufficient. **Bound, kept live:** in a *durable* clarity organization a member who permanently asks and never answers is a genuine asymmetry — unaddressed, and not resolved by this entry. *Programme tag: both.*
- **Unresolved — the objection aimed at the product's own name:** individual commitment doesn't shift behaviour unless the reference network's expectations visibly change. **A pledge can change the signer and still fail to change the room** — an argument for pledging in dyads and groups, never alone. Consistent with what the docs already concede ([theory-of-change.md](theory-of-change.md): *"Pledge is decorative until practice exists"*; [lean-canvas.md](lean-canvas.md): 11 pledgers, zero practice habits). **Not resolved — do not paper over it.**
- See [decisions.md](decisions.md) 2026-07-17 [product]; narrative `content/articles/a51_the-facilitator-should-fail-first.md`.

**Four additions (2026-08-12, all `UNTESTED`) — the instrument's own threat model, the missing gate, the sequencing correction, and the item set.**

- **(a) Goodhart threat to the calibration ledger — founder-flagged, unresolved.** Once the calibration delta is logged and used as a **trust signal**, the listener's optimal strategy is to predict the *speaker's* number rather than introspect their own state. The instrument then silently converts from a comprehension measure into a **mind-reading measure** — and it degrades exactly where it is needed, in the overconfident listener who does not know they are overconfident. This is the same class as the Goodhart-gameable "calibrated parrot" bound already recorded in [lean-canvas.md](lean-canvas.md) §UVP ("Calibration as a disqualifier"), but aimed at the *ledger* rather than at the score. **Second, founder-raised confound:** the delta mixes **speaker encoding quality** with **listener decoding ability**, so attributing it to the listener is a modelling choice, not a finding. Separating the facets needs crossed designs — the same speaker across many listeners and the same listener across many speakers (generalizability theory is the named tool). **Neither is resolvable by further reasoning; both need a design.** **Falsifier:** run the ledger as an explicit trust signal for a period and listener self-estimates do *not* converge toward speaker estimates ⟹ the Goodhart channel is not live and the ledger can be published inside the group.
- **(b) Disclosure needs two gates — legibility AND safety.** Independent; both must open. **Legibility** = will they understand me. **Safety** = what happens to me once they do. The protocol raises the first only, which is why installing the norm in a low-safety team produces **compliance theatre** — the number gets said without being meant. **The normalisation assumption is wrong, on a mechanism from inside our own lineage:** Pinker's argument requires no punishment, only that **status models get updated**. You can normalise the disclosure without normalising the inference, and common knowledge is irreversible — it forecloses the graceful path where someone quietly goes and figures it out, which is exactly what deniability protects. Structural: priceable, not eliminable. **Consequence — the size ceiling has a named mechanism:** what caps a clarity organization is **reciprocity closure**, not comprehension bandwidth. In a small repeated group the ledger balances (whoever reveals today receives tomorrow); above some *n* some members never reciprocate and revelation becomes a one-way status transfer. **Do not over-read the cost:** the competence update is partly *desirable* — it is how transactive memory forms, and a group that knows who actually knows what routes questions correctly instead of overtrusting. The cost is **asymmetric** exposure, not exposure. **Novelty bound:** Morrison's voice literature already splits *voice safety* from *voice efficacy*; legibility is neither (it concerns being comprehended, not consequence or impact), but that gap needs a real lit check before anything is built on it.
- **(c) Sequencing is dose, not gate.** Nembhard & Edmondson: safety is *built by* small acts of inviting and appreciating contribution — which is what this protocol is. So a **low-stakes version installs the safety a high-stakes version needs**, and the failure mode is not "norm before safety" but **"high-stakes norm before any safety."** The facilitator-fails-first mechanic specified above **is** the leader-fallibility antecedent; the fix was already built and had simply never been connected to the sequencing problem. **The founder does not have to wait on a safety measurement to install a low-stakes version.**
- **(d) The item set — constructs specified, wording NOT yet fixed.** Behavior stated once and reused across every item: *"saying so at the time when you haven't understood **what someone meant**."* **"What they meant" is load-bearing** — "didn't understand" collapses domain ignorance (I don't know the subject) with comprehension failure (I don't know what *you* meant), and only the second is this project's construct.
  - **Two errors corrected 2026-08-12, same day, before any use — both were founder catches.** *(i)* The empirical-expectation item was first written as *"of the last 10 times someone didn't fully understand, in how many would they have said so"* — **incoherent, because it asks for a denominator nobody can observe.** Invisible non-comprehension is the premise of the whole project; an item cannot assume it away. Key the item to the **observable act** instead (*how often do people around you say they're not sure they understood what someone meant*) — frequency, no denominator. *(ii)* The predicted-vs-observed reveal was first specified as a prediction about the respondent's **colleagues** compared against a tally of admissions **in the room** — two different populations, so the comparison is invalid however good it looks on stage.
  - **Three reference networks, three jobs — never averaged, always labelled.** **(A) This room** — the only one comparable to the observed tally, because prediction and behavior concern the same people; a one-off room is a real if temporary network (Miller & McFarland's setting was a classroom of near-strangers). **(B) Their colleagues** — the network the intervention targets; unvalidatable in the room and the commercially interesting one. **(C) Respondent-named** — for attendees with no colleagues, *"the people you most often think through hard problems with."* **Do not ask about hypothetical or anticipated groups** — a group whose behavior has never been observed has no empirical expectations to report. **Product consequence:** a recurring community converts a temporary local network into a durable one, which is the precondition for conformity to be conditional on anything — an argument for the membership rung that does not appear elsewhere in the docs.
  - **Constructs.** (1) **empirical expectation** — frequency of the observed act in [R]; (2) **normative expectation** — how many in [R] think one *should*; (3) **personal normative belief** — the respondent's own view; (4) **sanction expectation** — **bipolar** (status lost … no change … status gained), because observers may rate admission *positively* and a unipolar scale cannot return that result.
  - **Conditionality is measured, not asked.** It is definitional in Bicchieri (it classifies custom vs descriptive norm vs social norm, which decides whether the pledge/spread mechanism is even the right model) — but a hypothetical *"would you still do it if nobody else did"* is a cheap self-report. Get it two better ways instead: **covariation** across respondents (do the people who do it believe others do/expect it — confounded by false consensus, so suggestive only), and **the manipulation you already run** — intention before the reveal, intention after. If intention moves on learning what others actually do, that *is* conditionality, demonstrated.
  - **The diagnostic 2×2, and it doubles as a sales qualifier.** Empirical × normative: **low-E / high-N = pluralistic ignorance, ready to flip** — reveal plus trendsetter, and the hypothesized target cell; high-E / high-N = the norm already exists, nothing to install; low-E / low-N = no normative support, so the reveal lands on nobody and persuasion has to come first; high-E / low-N = descriptive only, unstable. **Two items tell you which room you are in before the demo runs.**
  - **Sourced 2026-08-12 against three primary texts** (Bicchieri & Xiao 2009; Bicchieri, *Norms in the Wild* 2016; UNICEF *Participatory Research Toolkit for Social Norms Measurement*, Sood/Kostizak/Stevens 2020 — all three in `.private/research/papers/`, indexed at `.private/research/INDEX.md`). **Item wording routes to the instrument spec, not here** (CHARTER rule 4/7: this file holds the bet, not the build). What belongs here is the four findings that bear on whether the bet survives.
  - **THREAT — information disclosure alone flips a *descriptive* norm, not a *social* one.** Bicchieri (2016 pp. 44–45) on the social-norms-marketing tradition — i.e. exactly the reveal this project's demo is built on: it "is effective at combatting descriptive norms that suffer from pluralistic ignorance… but is not effective at changing… social norms, which are additionally supported by normative expectations," and *"any successful change must change **both** empirical and normative expectations."* Plus a double credibility problem: the audience must believe the information is accurate **and** believe others will actually change. **So the reveal mechanism is conditional on which kind of norm gap-admission is** — and that is measurable before anything is built on it. This is the first thing the instrument must settle, not a baseline to collect.
  - **The diagnostic exists, field-tested, in four binary items** (UNICEF toolkit): an approval table (*do you approve* × *do others whose opinions matter to you approve*) crossed with a behaviour table. Printed reading rule: extreme quadrants ⟹ normatively driven; middle quadrants ⟹ *"either abiding by a normative behaviour they would rather not practice, or challenging a normative behaviour that is widely practiced."* Bicchieri's own operational detector is the same comparison at the aggregate level (2016 p. 74): aggregate **personal normative beliefs** against aggregate **normative expectations**; systematic divergence ⟹ people uphold a norm they dislike. **Precondition, and it invalidates the comparison if broken** (p. 98): the personal-belief item and the normative-expectation item must use **identical predicate wording**.
  - **Absence-as-signal cannot be read off observation** (Bicchieri 2016 p. 57): for proscriptive norms *"the proscribed behavior will not be observed,"* and the beliefs sustaining it *"are not observable, nor are they inferable from observed actions."* Admitting non-comprehension is exactly that case — **so the in-room tally alone establishes nothing about the norm** and must be paired with expectation elicitation. It also separates the two measurements cleanly: the in-room predicted-vs-observed count is a **demonstration that misperception exists**, not a measurement of *their* norm.
  - **Reference network — the one genuine gap across all three sources.** Bicchieri states identification is a precondition, gives **no elicitation procedure of her own**, and hands off by citation (Fishbein & Ajzen 2011; Watts 2003); her field method *presupposes* a network "clearly identified" and known to respondents, which is the same imposition as the 2009 paper's "dividers in this room" at village scale. **Nothing in any of the three addresses transient or single-session groups** — verified as a real absence, not a search miss — and her definition cuts against treating a room of strangers as a network at all (*"people normally do not worry much about what strangers might expect of them"*). The usable answer is the toolkit's **self-anchoring stem** (*"others, whose thoughts and opinions matter to you"*), which avoids imposing a group without requiring one to be named.
  - **Attribution discipline for anything built on this.** Binary yes/no items and the quadrant diagnostic are UNICEF-field; count-out-of-N predictions and incentivised elicitation are Bicchieri-lab; the bipolar status scale proposed earlier appears in **neither** and is ours (justified separately by the advice-seeking-raises-perceived-competence finding, not by their authority). No source gives order-effects guidance, so the randomise-order proposal is also ours. **`.private/research/INDEX.md` records what each source does and does not contain** — the same session re-derived this method from memory before noticing three of the primaries were already in the repo.
  - All items are self-report and inflation-prone — *"I should admit gaps"* is the socially desirable answer. Bicchieri's countermeasure is to **incentivise the expectation items** (accuracy-rewarded prediction); she concedes personal normative beliefs generally cannot be incentivised.

**Novel prediction for this update — pre-registered 2026-08-12.** In a **low-safety** room the norm installs as a **dissociation**: stated comprehension numbers are given readily while *unprompted* admission behavior is unchanged. The predecessor threshold framing predicts low adoption to look like **low participation** (people decline to play), not like high-stated/low-behavioral compliance theatre. Discriminating, and readable from the first two events. *Corroboration status: pending. Programme tag: both.*

**Falsifier for (b)/(c) jointly:** rooms scoring low on psychological safety install the norm and show the *same* behavioral change as high-safety rooms ⟹ the safety gate is not independent, legibility alone suffices, and the two-gate model is wrong. See [decisions.md](decisions.md) 2026-08-12 [product]; narrative `content/articles/a67_disclosure-needs-two-gates.md`.
**Deferred to phase-2 (2026-06-02):** Norm-cascade propagation is a phase-2+ multiplier (parked alongside the badge cascade, franchise, and club). Build after the first paid loop closes — it is not part of the near-term coach-distribution proof. [decisions.md](decisions.md) 2026-06-02 [product].

#### H-NormRaisesSafety: A trainable gap-admission norm RAISES team psychological safety (Edmondson is our outcome, not our rival)
**Bet (2026-08-12, `UNTESTED`, n=0):** Installing the gap-admission norm **causes** a measurable rise in team psychological safety. Edmondson's scale stops being a discriminant-validity nuisance the instrument must be defended against and becomes the **outcome measure**.
**Why this is the stronger move.** In Edmondson's own model, admitting gaps and asking for help are not constructs at all — they are the **dependent variable**, downstream of climate. Her established arrow is *safety → disclosure*. Claiming ours is a *different construct* is the weaker position and loses even when it wins; claiming ours is an **upstream cause of a well-known one** dissolves the overlap instead of defending against it, and is legible to a reviewer and to a buyer without argument. What the norm battery contains and Edmondson does not: empirical expectation, normative expectation, and conditionality (see H-NormFlip item (d)); what they share is only sanction expectation and part of face cost.
**Novel prediction — recorded 2026-08-12, before any test.** Installing the disclosure norm raises Edmondson-scale psychological safety at T2. **The predecessor framing predicts the opposite arrow** — that safety must already be present for disclosure to occur — so this is a successor claim rather than an accommodation of an anomaly. *Corroboration status: pending. Programme tag: both.*
**Test:** Edmondson's 7 items at T0 and T2 around an install, plus the norm battery, plus the observed unprompted-admission count. **Control arm mandatory** — see the objection below.
**Circularity fix — the composite excludes sanction (2026-08-12).** Reading Edmondson's seven items by grammatical form, they are **predominantly sanction/consequence expectation** ("it is safe to take a risk"; "it is difficult to ask for help"; mistakes "held against you"), with one item shading quasi-empirical ("able to bring up problems") and none asking the frequency of a specific behavior. **That makes the discriminant claim stronger than first stated** — empirical expectation, normative expectation *and* conditionality all sit outside her scale, so three of four constructs are non-overlapping, not one of four. **But it means our own sanction item is the contaminated one:** put sanction on the cause side and Edmondson on the outcome side and the hypothesis is partly X-causes-X. **So the "norm installed" composite is empirical + normative expectation + conditionality only; sanction is scored and reported separately.** Our sanction item is still worth keeping for a different reason — Bicchieri norm measurement is **behavior-specific**, and *"it is safe to take a risk here"* is not the claim *"it is safe to say I didn't follow you."* General climate vs act-specific is itself the discriminant argument.
**Cheapest kill-test in the whole design:** run the norm battery and Edmondson's seven on the same ~30 people, once. **If r > .8, this hypothesis is dead** and the separate-instrument claim goes with it — for the cost of one session. The discriminant argument above is from *item content*, and two scales with different wording routinely correlate .8, so treat discriminance as a prediction rather than a fact.
**The objection is recorded, not closed.** Contradicting an established directional model means a reviewer reads **reverse causality as confound** until there is temporal separation *plus* a control arm — and a control arm is not runnable inside a commercial engagement. **This is why the entry sits at P2/deferred rather than being pushed forward:** it is filed so it is not re-derived, not because it is close to testable.
**Cost note (removes the founder's stated objection):** Edmondson's scale is **7 Likert items**, roughly two minutes — *cheaper* than the norm battery beside it, so the two together are ~13 items under five minutes. **Flagged as agent-reported from memory, not read in primary; a five-minute check before it enters any guide.** **Do not cherry-pick a 3-item subset** — a subset of a validated scale is not that scale, and it forfeits the comparability and free reviewer acceptance that were the only reasons to use a published instrument. Run it whole or not at all.
**Positioning guardrail:** psychological safety is the **outcome measure, never the pitch.** It sits adjacent to the anti-positioning guardrail in [lean-canvas.md](lean-canvas.md) §Customer Segments against labelling the buyer's problem "culture," and carries the same failure mode — a crowded category with rubrics CP does not optimise for.
**Falsifier:** install the norm and Edmondson-scale safety is flat at T2 **in rooms where unprompted admission demonstrably rose** ⟹ the norm does not cause safety, and Edmondson's original arrow stands unchallenged.

#### H-ProtocolSpreadsWithoutTool: Protocol spreads free; tool captures value
**Bet:** Explain-back protocol is valuable enough to spread without the product. Tool captures users who want measurement.
**Split into two claims (2026-07-14) — the bet was compound and the first field datum separates it:** **(a) use-without-tool** — the protocol is valuable enough to be *used* with no tool and no facilitator; and **(b) spread** — that value causes it to *propagate*. (a) is now supported; (b) is refuted at n=1. Keep them apart: evidence for (a) is not evidence for (b), and conflating them is how R₀≈0 got predicted wrong the first time.
**Transform if:** Zero organic spread after 5+ pairs experience sessions.
**Blocked by:** H-PairsReturn.
**Early signal (2026-03-26):** Hostel stranger wanted to share further after completing protocol (prevention-driven). N=1, not yet verified as actual spread. But first organic referral intent observed.
**First field datum (2026-07-14) — supersedes the "zero pairs have used protocol" status below; ADOPTION data, not validation:** an unsolicited voice memo from a practitioner **outside the founder's circle** who has been running the protocol with his developers for weeks, unprompted. **Kept:** the forced-articulation + paraphrase-back loop. **Invented** (nobody designed it): a physical somatic trigger — a rubber band — to catch his *own* comprehension gaps and loop back. **Dropped:** the 0–10 numeric scoring (reason: group size/pace). **Reported outcome (self-reported, unverified):** visible quality gain, "nothing comes back for rework," most pronounced with a developer prone to filling in assumptions. **Broke at:** 6–7 people on a call (dyadic paraphrase can't keep pace).
- **Reads on the split:** **(a) supported** — sustained use, no tool, no facilitator, reported value. **(b) refuted (n=1)** — he **did not transmit it to anyone.** He is evidence *against* spontaneous spread, and this **corroborates** the R₀≈0 retirement ([lean-canvas.md](lean-canvas.md) §Unfair Advantage, "Protocol-led growth (retired as a current advantage, 2026-06-02)") rather than contradicting it: a user who adopts and does not propagate is what R₀≈0 predicts.
- **Do not upgrade to validation.** He (a) skipped training himself, (b) stripped the measurement, (c) didn't transmit it. That combination *is* the finding — **the realistic ceiling of unassisted uptake**. Bears on H-ProtocolTransfers (H-FounderWince) and H-BadgePropagates.
- **Confound, stated not buried:** the report cannot separate the protocol's effect from simply having a domain-competent person on the call asking "why did you decide it that way?"
- **Segment caveat:** he is a dev lead doing task-handoff comprehension — adjacent to, but not, the active seed–A key-hire wedge.
- **Does not move the transform counter** (which tracks organic *spread* after facilitated pairs — this is unfacilitated use, and on spread it counts against).
- **Counter-datum on (b), so the refutation is not read as blanket (2026-07-30, founder-supplied, SOFT):** a community builder in the founder's network **intends to spread the protocol** to a community she is forming. This is **stated intent, not observed behavior**, so it is SOFT and does not offset the n=1 refutation above — but it does mean *"nobody propagates it"* no longer stands as an unqualified reading. Contrast with the practitioner datum above is itself informative: he **used it and did not transmit**; she **has not used it at scale and wants to transmit**. Different halves of the split, from different people, neither one a validation. **Promotes to HARD only when a transmission actually occurs** — a second person running the protocol traceable to her, with the founder absent. Until then (b) stands as refuted-at-n=1 with one open intent. See [decisions.md](decisions.md) 2026-07-30 [product].
**Open sub-bet — is the number load-bearing? (UNTESTED, unresolved):** the loop survived removal of the score, and by our own thesis a self-reported "7/10" **inflates most exactly when the gap is largest** — so the number is a weak *in-moment* detector by our own model. Founder's counter: the score provides *longitudinal* pre/post calibration that builds trust and reduces time-to-paraphrase. The field data is **silent** on it — he tested throughput, not training, with no multi-session baseline. **Falsifier:** run a multi-session baseline; if time-to-paraphrase does not fall across sessions for score-users vs loop-only users, the longitudinal defence fails and scoring is a context-dependent component, not the core. See [decisions.md](decisions.md) 2026-07-17 [product]; narrative `content/articles/a48_the-user-who-dropped-the-numbers.md`.
  - **Sharpened test — controlled removal (2026-07-23, UNTESTED):** the field datum is naturalistic (he dropped the score for pace) and measures throughput. Run it *deliberately* instead — remove the speaker score across matched sessions and watch a two-metric pair: **gaps-detected (predict ↓)** and **participant-satisfaction (predict ↑)**. That combination — catching *less* while feeling *better* — is the illusion's own signature, so observing it confirms the score is load-bearing as a detector; if gaps-detected holds steady with the score gone, the score is redundant and the audience critic ("it's too judgmental, drop it") is right. Cheap (~1 month on the existing session pipeline). Ties to the Min speaker-score reframe ([definitions.md](definitions.md) §Understanding Calibration — self-testimony wording).
**Mechanism (2026-04-02):** The replication unit is the person who flips, not the organization or the tool. A person who internalizes the protocol becomes a node that seeds verification in any relationship they enter. Workshop → flip → they bring it home → their partner encounters it → new node. Implication: optimize workshops for individual transformation depth, not organizational adoption breadth.

#### H-LegibilityVsCost: Why no understanding-practice ever became default infrastructure
**Bet (2026-07-21, UNTESTED):** A dozen good understanding-practices exist (NVC, Crucial Conversations, motivational interviewing, double-crux, pre-mortems) and none became default infrastructure. Two rival explanations predict the same surface fact and imply opposite strategies: **(A) legibility-binding** — people don't seek the cure because the failure is *self-concealing* (the illusion erases its own evidence), so making the burden *visible* while holding the cure's cost constant should raise uptake; **(B) cost-binding** — people perceive the problem fine but verifying is genuinely too costly (time, status), so visibility alone moves nothing.
**Test:** the **Clarity Letter is the experiment that runs the fork** — it raises visibility and lowers *no* cost. It is clean only if the letter genuinely doesn't also reduce the follow-up's cost or status-risk (if it does, two variables moved — design against this).
**Transform if:** recipients read the letter, register the gap, and still don't show up / convert → the binding constraint was cost or incentive, not legibility, and "the letter is the wedge" is falsified. (Competing non-legibility explanations to keep live: cost, incentive/strategic-ambiguity, status, partial-diffusion.)
**Distinct from H-LetterAsProduct** (killed 2026-06-02): that bet was viral-R₀ / forwarding (a *propagation* claim); this is category-level *why the cure never spreads* (a *demand-formation* claim). Narrative: `content/articles/a54_sanitation-not-eradication.md`.
**Third branch added 2026-08-12 — (C) norm-binding. `UNTESTED`.** The A/B fork above assumes the blocker sits between the person and the cure. A third possibility sits between the person and the *room*: people perceive their own confusion perfectly well, but **misperceive how willing everyone else is to admit theirs** — so nobody goes first, and going first alone looks slow. Distinct from (A): the failure is self-concealing to the room, not to the person having it, so raising *self*-directed visibility (branch A's lever) would not move it.
**Why this branch, now — the 74-year datum the A/B fork cannot explain.** Rogers & Roethlisberger published the mechanic in *HBR* in **1952** (*restate the previous speaker's ideas to that speaker's satisfaction before replying*) and it never became standard practice. A cure that is published in a mainstream management journal, costs nothing, and is still not default after seven decades is weak evidence for (B) cost-binding and awkward for (A) legibility-binding — the cure was maximally legible the whole time. Mechanism for (C): Miller & McFarland (1987), where participants handed a deliberately incomprehensible text badly overestimated how many peers would stay silent rather than ask.
**Test — now the cheapest in this file, and it is the same act as the event demo.** Predicted-vs-observed admission count, in-room. **Predicted** is one item asked about **this room specifically** — *"of the ~N people here, how many do you think will say they're not sure they followed?"* — because that is the only prediction comparable to what gets counted (see H-NormFlip item (d): asking about the respondent's colleagues and counting in the room compares two different populations). **Observed** is a tally of who says it unprompted after one deliberately dense passage — **the passage supplies the denominator**, without which a zero count cannot distinguish "nobody was confused" from "everybody hid it." **Ordering is load-bearing:** behavior first, predictions after, reveal last — asking the prediction item first *tells* people others might be confused too and contaminates the count.
**Novel prediction — pre-registered 2026-08-12, before any event has been run.** Correcting a person's belief about **others' behavior** (showing them the room's observed admission count against their prediction) moves adoption **more** than showing them their own comprehension gap. Neither predecessor branch predicts this: (A) predicts self-directed visibility is the lever, (B) predicts visibility of either kind moves nothing because cost binds. Discriminating, and it reads off event #1 with no new instrument. *Corroboration status: pending.*
**Falsifier (C):** rooms show no gap between predicted and observed admission rate ⟹ the misperception is not the binding constraint and the A/B fork stands as written. *Programme tag: both.* **Evidence grade: conversation-sourced, zero field subjects; neither Rogers 1952 nor Miller & McFarland 1987 read in primary — read both before either appears in published writing.** See [decisions.md](decisions.md) 2026-08-12 [product]; narrative `content/articles/a66_the-answer-published-in-1952.md`.
**Consequence for the rivals registry, recorded rather than absorbed:** this strengthens rival **(e)** on our own data — its core claim is already *"the gap is adoption and safety, not the absence of an instrument"* ([research-programme.md](research-programme.md)). CP is not refuted by it; CP's answer relocates onto the same ground the rival names — the product is an **adoption mechanism**, not a new instrument.

**First apparent field datum — recorded as CONFOUNDED, do not count it (2026-07-30):** a peer who fully grasps the construct refused the practice in explicitly **cost-binding** language (*"too much work"*, *"onerous"*), which reads as clean support for branch (B). **It is not usable**, because the same session established that this person's stated reasons arrived *after* visible friction and are better explained as post-hoc rationalization (H-AffectiveHonesty, "Rationalization signature"). Cost language produced by a rationalizing subject is evidence about the *rationalization*, not about the binding constraint. Logged so a later reader does not mine the quote as branch-(B) support. **What a clean datum would require:** the stated reason captured *before* any friction is visible, or a subject with no prior exposure to the construct.

#### H-AccountingLayer: Institutions can't ACCOUNT for misunderstanding-harm, so it never wins budget
**Bet (2026-07-21, UNTESTED — the scale-up of H-LegibilityVsCost):** at institutional scale the block isn't perception, it's **attribution**. Misunderstanding-harm is diffuse, deniable, and unattributable — a deal dies and everyone blames strategy, never the comprehension gap three meetings back — so it has no metric, no owner, no budget line, and loses every priority fight to things that show up in the accounts. Aviation CRM and surgical checklists became *mandatory* only where failure produced legible, attributable, countable "corpses." Implication: CP might be less a communication practice than an **accounting layer** that makes comprehension-risk countable so it can compete for institutional priority.
**Counter (recorded, not resolved):** maybe institutions don't account for it because it genuinely matters *less* at scale than raw interest-conflict and power — the absence could be correct triage, not a blind spot. If the ROI were there, some competitive org would have stumbled into pricing it and won; that none durably have is weak evidence the ROI isn't capturable.
**Transform if:** in the one domain where the "corpses" are countable (the aviation-analog to find), pricing comprehension-risk still fails to change institutional behavior → the accounting-layer thesis is dead and the counter (correct triage) wins.
**Status:** Blocked — needs a countable-corpses domain and decision volume small orgs lack early. Positioning is NOT elevated on this (founder 1A, 2026-07-24): it lives here as a bet, not as a load-bearing UVP. Relates to the institutional rung of the Cognitive Understanding Ledger ([definitions.md](definitions.md)).

#### H-Community-Retention: Support group for practitioners increases retention
**Bet:** A support group where practitioners share experiences from bringing calibrated communication into their real relationships (co-founder, partner, team) increases retention beyond 1:1 retainers. Format: sharing what happened when they tried the protocol, not practicing with strangers. Analogous to a running club for people training for a marathon, not a track meet.
**Reframe (2026-03-29):** Field data shows Partner Agreements only work with existing high-stakes relationships (boyfriend, co-founder) — not strangers. The community isn't a practice arena (strangers practicing together); it's a support group (practitioners sharing experiences from real relationships). This is testable NOW as a WhatsApp group or monthly call — doesn't need the product.
**Test:** Run a monthly call with 5+ active Partner Agreement holders. Measure: do they report practicing more because of the group? Do they bring new pairs?
**Transform if:** Group devolves into abstract discussion without practice reports.
**Blocked by:** Need 5+ active Partner Agreements first. Partially unblocked — first pair has one; more expected from workshops.

**Refined same day (2026-08-10) — milestone, free tier, guest rung.** Membership is **monthly and open-ended**; the **milestone is month 3: the member is running it in their own organization**. Retention past the milestone is needs-based and **speculative** (*"they might still want to exchange and brainstorm problems and find out how others do it"*). This is why the recorded *"community-as-academy — graduation kills retention"* rejection is **not** reversed — the open end is kept and a milestone is placed inside it. A **free tier** (the repeating events) holds the not-yet-convinced, subsuming the replay lane's job with a room rather than a recording; a **guest rung** lets members bring colleagues free, or paid as one-time spectators. **What the member actually does:** opens **their own Clarity Organization on CP** ([definitions.md](definitions.md) — *"one specific team operating internally on the protocol"*), runs clarity events inside it, and recruits colleagues **into their own org**, not into CP's community. That is what clears H-ProtocolTransfers on its own terms — a clarity shift produced in a room the founder is not in, carried by the product rather than his calendar. **Falsifier:** by ~month 3 no member has opened a Clarity Organization and run one event in it ⟹ the transfer mechanism is not doing the work.

**Unblocked and monetized (2026-08-10, `UNTESTED`).** This bet is no longer waiting on Partner Agreements — it is the **first paid rung**: the Clarity Practice Community at **€295/month**, sourced from free-event attendees who accepted the **Clarity Organization terms** in the room *(corrected same day — the earlier text said "the CPA"; the pre-registered prediction names the artifact whose acceptance predicts purchase, so the wrong artifact would make it untestable)* (§Revenue → Active focus in [lean-canvas.md](lean-canvas.md); [decisions.md](decisions.md) 2026-08-10 [product]). The 2026-03-29 reframe is what the product was built to — *practitioners sharing experiences from real relationships, not strangers practicing together* — so this is that shape sold, not a new construct. The `Transform if` above is unchanged and is now the **churn falsifier**: a paid group that devolves into abstract discussion churns inside two cycles.

**Structural tension to watch, recorded not resolved:** members are cross-organizational strangers who each practise on an **absent third party** in their own org. That is the same configuration behind the letter's R₀≈0 and the recorded caution that handing someone a tool to use with an absent counterparty reproduces it ([goals.md](goals.md) — `/align` belongs at the install, not as a free-event CTA). The community's peer-support function is the proposed mitigation; it is untested.

**Novel prediction: none identified — accommodation.** This update re-homes an existing bet at a new price and predicts nothing its predecessor did not. Recorded honestly rather than dressed as a successor. *Programme tag: business. Core-adjacent: no (belt).*
