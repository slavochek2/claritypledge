# H-Recognition-Via-Essays: Essays Reach Aligned Communities

## Hypothesis Statement

Essays positioning calibration as "infrastructure for personal AI" can reach AI safety/rationalist communities and build recognition as "the calibration expert."

## Rationale

**The opportunity:** AI labs need calibrated training data. Rationalist communities care about epistemic hygiene. Essays connecting these dots can position founder uniquely.

**Why essays work:**
- LessWrong/EA Forum/Alignment Forum are idea-distribution channels
- Rationalists value technical depth + clear thinking
- "Calibration infrastructure for AI" frames Stories as strategic, not tactical
- One high-signal post can unlock recognition threshold

## Assumptions

1. AI alignment researchers care about calibrated training data (not just behavioral data)
2. Rationalist communities will engage with calibration framing
3. Essays can build recognition faster than workshops alone
4. Recognition leads to inbound from aligned funders

## Evidence

- **Market validation:** AI labs explicitly seek alignment solutions
- **Distribution channel:** LessWrong reaches 10K+ aligned readers
- **Content gaps:** No one else framing calibration as AI infrastructure
- **Founder fit:** Technical depth + epistemic clarity matches audience

## Success Criteria

- [ ] 3+ essays published
- [ ] 50+ readers per essay (average)
- [ ] 5+ meaningful discussions/comments
- [ ] 1+ inbound "you're the expert" mention
- [ ] Content referenced in calibration/AI discussions

## Failure Criteria

- <50 readers per essay after 3 months
- Zero engagement (no comments, shares, mentions)
- No recognition signals by Month 3
