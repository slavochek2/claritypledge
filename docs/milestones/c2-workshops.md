# C2: First Founder Sessions (Coaching Validation)

## TL;DR

**Hypothesis:** Co-founder pairs will pay for calibrated communication sessions to prevent alignment drift before it becomes crisis.

**Test:** Run 3-5 sessions (each 60-90 min) with co-founder pairs, track which pairs book follow-ups.

**Success:** ≥50% of co-founder pairs book a follow-up paid session within 30 days.

**Kill signal:** <2 founder pairs across 3 sessions OR zero follow-up bookings.

---

## Deep Dive

**What we're building:** Nothing new — use C1 features (/live + stories), run co-founder sessions

**Done when:** 3-5 sessions run with founder pairs, follow-up booking rate measured

## Key Insight: Co-Founders Are the Primary ICP

Co-founder pairs are the PRIMARY ICP for C-track. Unlike generic workshop participants:
- They have recurring calibration needs (ongoing alignment on decisions)
- They have high WTP for preventive communication (10x better ROI than crisis coaching)
- They represent stable revenue (retainer potential — feeds directly into C3)
- They validate tool-as-infrastructure (not just Slava's facilitation)

**Positioning:** See [lean-canvas.md](../lean-canvas.md) Track 2 for full positioning. Core: functioning-but-misaligned pairs who recognize the gap exists — preventive, not therapeutic.

**Why focus here first:**
- C1 proves the tool works. C2 proves founders will pay.
- Creates case studies for R-track positioning (months 7-12)
- Leads directly to C3 (paid retainers for founder cohorts)

## The Session Model

**Single session (validated in C2):** €300–500 per co-founder pair
- 60-90 min calibration on a current real decision or tension
- Both founders use /live to verify understanding
- Debrief on what the gap revealed and what to do next

**Retainer (scaled in C3):** €800–1,500/month per pair
- Monthly calibration calls
- Tooling + methodology bundled
- Goal: 10+ paying pairs by end of Month 6

## Acquisition: Two Channels

**Booking channel:** ladischenski.com → 15-min intake call → paid session. Intake questions qualify the dyad (functioning but misaligned, not in crisis).

**Channel 1 — Free Calibration Lab** (primary, broadcast):
- Biweekly, open enrollment, 90 min, cast wide (founders, operators, coaches)
- After the session: route based on who showed up
  - Co-founder pairs → direct coaching sales pitch
  - Solo founders + equity advisors → briefing protocol offer (future expansion)
  - Coaches/practitioners → workshop partnership discussion

**Channel 2 — Briefing Protocol invitation** (targeted, to test after H-BriefingProtocol-ColdStart validates):
- Mirror agent reaches out directly to Person B in a known high-stakes dyad
- Person B gets value before signing up — the invitation IS the product experience
- Requires motivated Person B with pre-existing stakes in the relationship

**ICP qualifier:** High-stakes dyad — relationship health + decision frequency, not funding stage. Pre-seed to seed is typical but the criterion is: both parties have skin in the game and miscalibration is already costing them.

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Target | How to Measure |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Co-founder pairs recruited | 3-5 | Outreach + confirmed |
| Sessions completed | 3-5 | Calendar + attendance |
| Founders report gap awareness | 80%+ | Post-session survey |
| Founders find it valuable | 70%+ | Post-session survey |
| **Follow-up session booked** | **≥50%** | Conversion tracking |
| WTP signal (clear yes/no) | Clear | Conversation notes |

## Decision Points

**If <2 founder pairs join sessions:**
- Either format doesn't work for co-founders OR outreach channel wrong
- Iterate before scaling

**If founders don't book follow-ups:**
- Value not landing. Explore: Is it price? Format? Wrong founder stage?

**If ≥50% follow-up rate:**
- Proceed to C3: Build retainer product for cohort model
- Create first case study for R-track essay (month 5+)

## First Session Checklist

- [ ] Recruit 3-5 co-founder pairs (warm intros, founder communities, accelerator alumni)
- [ ] Pick session topics (current real challenge for each pair)
- [ ] Schedule sessions over 2-4 weeks
- [ ] Meta-epistemological briefing before each session (see H-MetaEpistemic-Prerequisite)
- [ ] Run session, note the gap reveal moment
- [ ] Collect specific feedback: "Would you use this regularly?"
- [ ] Ask directly: "Would you pay €300-500 for this? What about monthly?"
