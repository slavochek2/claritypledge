# C2: First Calibration Labs + Partner Agreements

## TL;DR

**Hypothesis:** Co-founder pairs who attend a free Calibration Lab will create partner agreements and return to /live regularly — the agreement operationalizes the commitment, not just records it.

**Test:** Run 3-5 Calibration Labs (free, 90 min), measure agreement creation rate + 30-day /live return rate.

**Success:** ≥50% of participants create a partner agreement; ≥50% of those return to /live within 30 days.

**Kill signal:** <30% agreement creation rate OR <30% return to /live within 30 days.

---

## Deep Dive

**What we're building:** Nothing new — use C1 features (/live + stories), run Calibration Labs and introduce partner agreement creation

**Done when:** 3-5 Calibration Labs run, agreement creation rate + 30-day /live return rate measured

## Key Insight: Co-Founders Are the Primary ICP

Co-founder pairs are the PRIMARY ICP for C-track. Unlike generic workshop participants:
- They have recurring calibration needs (ongoing alignment on decisions)
- They have high WTP for preventive communication (10x better ROI than crisis coaching)
- They represent stable revenue (retainer potential — feeds directly into C3)
- They validate tool-as-infrastructure (not just Slava's facilitation)

**Positioning:** See [lean-canvas.md](../lean-canvas.md) Track 2 for full positioning. Core: functioning-but-misaligned pairs who recognize the gap exists — preventive, not therapeutic.

**Why focus here first:**
- C1 proves the tool works. C2 proves founders will pay.
- Creates case studies for R-track positioning (months 7-12)
- Leads directly to C3 (paid retainers for founder cohorts)

## The Calibration Lab Model

**Calibration Lab (validated in C2):** Free, 90 min per co-founder pair
- Calibration on a current real decision or tension
- Both founders use /live to verify understanding
- Debrief on what the gap revealed and what to do next
- Lab output: a partner agreement — a written commitment from both parties to continue calibration practice via /live

**Why the partner agreement is the core output:** The Lab is not just a /live demo. The agreement operationalizes the commitment. A pair who signs an agreement has a concrete artifact that ties them back to recurring /live usage — it's the retention mechanism, not just a record of attending.

**Retainer (scaled in C3):** €800–1,500/month per pair
- Offered to pairs who created a partner agreement AND have returned to /live at least 2-3 times
- Monthly calibration calls
- Tooling + methodology bundled
- Goal: 10+ paying pairs by end of Month 6

## Acquisition: Two Channels

**Booking channel:** ladischenski.com → primary CTA is joining a free Calibration Lab. Intake questions qualify the dyad (functioning but misaligned, not in crisis).

**Channel 1 — Free Calibration Lab** (primary, broadcast):
- Biweekly, open enrollment, 90 min, cast wide (founders, operators, coaches)
- ladischenski.com is the entry point — the site offers the free Lab as the primary action, not a paid session
- After the Lab: route based on who showed up
  - Co-founder pairs → partner agreement creation + /live follow-up practice
  - Solo founders + equity advisors → briefing protocol offer (future expansion)
  - Coaches/practitioners → workshop partnership discussion

**Channel 2 — Briefing Protocol invitation** (targeted, to test after H-BriefingProtocol-ColdStart validates):
- Mirror agent reaches out directly to Person B in a known high-stakes dyad
- Person B gets value before signing up — the invitation IS the product experience
- Requires motivated Person B with pre-existing stakes in the relationship

**ICP qualifier:** High-stakes dyad — relationship health + decision frequency, not funding stage. Pre-seed to seed is typical but the criterion is: both parties have skin in the game and miscalibration is already costing them.

## Decision Points

**If <2 founder pairs join Labs:**
- Either format doesn't work for co-founders OR outreach channel wrong
- Iterate before scaling

**If <30% agreement creation rate:**
- Lab value not landing, or the transition to agreement is too abrupt
- Explore: Is the agreement framing clear? Does the Lab feel incomplete without it?

**If agreements created but <30% return to /live:**
- Agreement is not driving behavior — commitment doesn't translate to practice
- Explore: Is the agreement too abstract? Does /live need clearer next-step prompting?

**If ≥50% agreement creation + ≥50% return rate:**
- Proceed to C3: Offer retainers to pairs with demonstrated /live return behavior
- Create first case study for R-track essay (month 5+)

## First Calibration Lab Checklist

- [ ] Recruit 3-5 co-founder pairs (warm intros, founder communities, accelerator alumni)
