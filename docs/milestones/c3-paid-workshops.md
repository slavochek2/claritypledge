# M4: Paid Workshops ($100/person)

**Build:** Payment infra (own platform)

**Done when:** 10+ people have paid for a workshop

**Kill signal:** <5% conversion from donate to paid

## Hypothesis: People will pay for workshops

**What we're testing:** Will people pay for calibrated communication workshops?

**Why this matters:** Donation-based events (M2) prove value exists. But will people pay? And at what price point?

**How to test:**
- Run paid workshops at $100/person
- Track conversion from donate to paid
- Survey: What made you willing to pay?

**Success criteria:**
- 10+ paid participants
- >5% conversion from donate-only to paid
- Clear value articulation from participants

**Pricing hypothesis:**
- $100/person = accessible for individuals
- $1000 for 10-person team = accessible for businesses
- Goal: Prove WTP exists before building subscription product
