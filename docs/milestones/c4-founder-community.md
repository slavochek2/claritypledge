# C4: Founder Community (Coaching Scale)

## TL;DR

**Hypothesis:** Founder pairs in paid retainers want ongoing peer community (group calibration + knowledge sharing) at €200–300/month add-on.

**Test:** Launch founder community cohort (monthly group calls) for C3 retainer customers.

**Success:** 10+ paying members, >60% month-2 retention, cohort actively referencing /live in group discussions.

**Kill signal:** <5 community members OR <40% month-2 retention.

---

## Deep Dive

**What we're building:** Community layer (group calls, async discussion, case sharing)

**Done when:** 10+ founder pairs active in community, month-2 retention >60%, visible peer calibration happening

## Core Insight

**Problem:** Individual retainers are valuable, but isolation limits stickiness and referrals.

**Opportunity:** Founder pairs want peer validation ("Are we the only ones struggling with this?"). Community tier unlocks:
- Group calibration (verify understanding across 5+ pairs simultaneously)
- Peer problem-solving ("How did you handle misalignment on this decision?")
- Higher retention (community = real switching cost)
- Referral engine (pairs recommend to other pairs they know)

## The Model

**C3 retainer (individual):** Fractional Clarity Officer — Slava reviews transcripts, identifies divergence with AI, facilitates targeted sessions (€1,500/mo)

**C4 community layer (add-on):**
- Monthly cohort calibration call (all pairs together, 90 min)
- Async case studies (Slack/Discord: "Here's a decision we calibrated on")
- Quarterly member showcase (each pair demos how they use calibration)

**Pricing:**
- Community add-on: €200–300/month (on top of C3 retainer)
- Or bundled all-inclusive: €1,700/month (retainer + community)

**Target:** 10 paying members by Month 9-10

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Target | How to Measure |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Community members | 10+ | Active subscriptions |
| Monthly cohort attendance | 80%+ | Call attendance records |
| Async engagement | Active | Slack message frequency |
| Month-2 retention | >60% | Active subscriptions |
| NPS / satisfaction | ≥7.5/10 | Monthly check-in |
| Peer referrals | 2+ | New members from existing members |

## Decision Points

**If <5 community members:**
- Community tier value not resonating — iterate format or make it included in base tier

**If <40% month-2 retention:**
- Community feels obligatory, not valuable — rethink facilitation or use case focus

**If 10+ members + >60% retention:**
- Community moat is real (cohort stickiness beats 1:1)
- Consider raising to scale coaching + community to 50+ pairs

## Financial Model

**Revenue at success (Month 9-10):**
- 7 pairs × €1,500/month (FCO retainer) = €10.5k
- 5 pairs × €250/month (community add-on) = €1,250
- **Total: ~€11.75k/month**

Retention drives compounding: If >60% month-2, word-of-mouth + peer referrals grow. By Month 12: 20+ pairs possible.
