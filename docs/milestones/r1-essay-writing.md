## Hypothesis: Recognition via essays

**What we're testing:** Can essays position founder as "the calibration person" in AI/rationalist circles?

**Why this matters:**
- Recognition is the PRIMARY track (coaching is safety)
- Essays build long-term reputation, not transactional revenue
- Target audience: rationalists, LessWrong, AI researchers
- Success = "you're the calibration expert" inbound mentions

**The model:**
```
Essays (calibration infrastructure for AI) → Recognition in target communities →
  → Aligned funders notice → Potential €100-200k raise to scale recognition work
```

**Topics to explore:**
- Calibration infrastructure for personal AI
- Speaker verification as ground truth for AI alignment
- Metacomprehension accuracy in human-AI interaction
- Build-in-public: what we're learning building Clarity Pledge

**How to test:**
- Publish 3+ essays in first 3 months
- Share on LessWrong, Twitter/X, HackerNews
- Track: readers, comments, shares, inbound mentions
- Success signal: essays reach 50+ readers, spark discussion

**Success criteria:**
- 3+ essays published
- 50+ readers per essay (average)
- 5+ meaningful discussions/comments
- 1+ inbound "you're the expert" mention
- Content referenced by others in calibration/AI discussions

**Failure criteria:**
- <50 readers per essay after 3 months
- Zero engagement (no comments, shares, or mentions)
- No recognition signals by Month 3

**Alignment with dual-track:**
- This is PRIMARY track (coaching is secondary safety)
- Runs parallel to C2 workshop validation
