# R1: Essay Writing (Recognition Track)

## TL;DR (30-second summary)

**Hypothesis:** Essays positioning calibration as "infrastructure for personal AI" can reach AI safety/rationalist communities and build recognition as "the calibration expert."

**How we test:** Publish 3+ essays over 3 months (May-Aug 2026), share on LessWrong/HN/Twitter, track readers and engagement.

**Success metric:** ≥50 readers per essay (average), ≥5 meaningful discussions, ≥1 inbound "you're the expert" mention.

**Kill signal:** <50 readers per essay after 3 months OR zero engagement (no comments, shares, mentions).

**Recognition model:** Essays → Recognition in target communities → Aligned funders notice → Potential €100-200k raise

**Sequencing note:** R1 starts Month 5 (not Month 1). Reason: essays grounded in real C1/C3 founder session data are 2x more credible than theory-first pieces. First 4 months build the evidence; R1 publishes what was learned. "We ran sessions with 10+ founder pairs. Here's what we measured about calibration." is a far stronger hook than abstract theory.

## How We're Testing

**Method:**
1. Publish 3+ essays over 3 months (May 1 - Aug 1)
2. Share on LessWrong, Hacker News, Twitter/X
3. Track: readers, comments, shares, inbound mentions
4. Measure: recognition signals

**Topics to explore:**
- What 10+ founder pairs taught us about calibration gaps (aggregate session data)
- Calibration as preventive vs. reactive: evidence from founder coaching
- Calibration infrastructure for personal AI (informed by real session patterns)
- Speaker verification as ground truth for AI alignment
- Metacomprehension accuracy in human-AI interaction
- Build-in-public: what we're learning building Clarity Pledge

**Measurements:**
- **Quantitative:** Readers per essay (page views, unique visitors), LessWrong karma/views
- **Qualitative:** Engagement (comments, shares, citations), inbound mentions ("you're the calibration expert"), community discussion (threads, HN comments)

---

## What We're Measuring

**SMART Goal:** Essays reach ≥50 readers in AI/rationalist communities

**Target values:**
- ≥50 readers per essay (average across 3+ essays)
- ≥5 meaningful discussions/comments
- ≥1 inbound "you're the expert" mention
- Content referenced in calibration/AI discussions

**Kill thresholds:**
- <50 readers per essay after 3 months (poor reach)
- Zero engagement (no comments, shares, mentions)
- No recognition signals by Month 3

**Data sources:**
- Analytics: Ghost page views, LessWrong karma/views
- Engagement tracking: Comments, shares, citations
- Inbound mentions: Direct messages, references in other content
