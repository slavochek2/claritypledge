# R3: Recognition Checkpoint (Decision Point, Month 12)

**Build:** Nothing new — evaluation milestone

**Done when:** Decision made: raise to scale recognition, continue dual-track, or pivot to coaching-only

**Checkpoint (decision gate):** This IS the Month 12 checkpoint — evaluates if trajectory unlocks unbounded ambition phase or requires strategic adjustment

## Decision Point: Has recognition track validated?

**What we're evaluating:** After 12 months, has the recognition track shown enough traction to justify continuing?

**Why this matters:**
- 12 months = sufficient time to test recognition hypothesis
- Recognition is PRIMARY track, but needs validation
- Decision: raise/scale recognition, continue dual-track, or pivot to coaching-only

**Evaluation framework:**

### Recognition Track Success Signals (Month 12)

| Signal | Strong Traction | Some Traction | No Traction |
|--------|----------------|---------------|-------------|
| Essay reach | 200+ readers/essay | 50-200 readers | <50 readers |
| Spec engagement | Discussed on LW/HN, contributors active | Viewed but limited discussion | Zero engagement |
| Inbound mentions | 5+ "you're the expert" | 1-5 mentions | Zero mentions |
| Community positioning | Known as "calibration infrastructure person" | Some recognition | Unknown |
| Fundraising interest | Aligned funders interested (€100-200k) | Some interest | No interest |

### Coaching Track Success Signals (Month 12)

| Signal | Strong | Moderate | Weak |
|--------|--------|----------|------|
| Revenue | >€5k/month | €3-5k/month | <€3k/month |
| Customer count | 50+ customers | 30-50 customers | <30 customers |
| Retention | >50% month 2+ | 30-50% | <30% |

### Decision Matrix

**Scenario A: Strong recognition + moderate coaching**
- **Action:** Raise €100-200k from aligned funders
- **Focus:** Scale recognition work (essays, specs, infrastructure)
- **Coaching role:** Validation + case studies, not primary identity

**Scenario B: Moderate recognition + strong coaching**
- **Action:** Continue dual-track, reassess at Month 18
- **Focus:** Build coaching to €5k/month while continuing recognition work
- **Decision deferred:** Need more time to validate recognition

**Scenario C: Weak recognition + strong coaching**
- **Action:** Pivot to coaching-only, accept it as primary business
- **Focus:** Scale coaching to €10k+/month
- **Recognition role:** Content marketing for coaching, not primary identity

**Scenario D: Weak both tracks**
- **Action:** Hard pivot or wind down
- **Reality check:** Neither track validated after 12 months

**Scenario E: Strong recognition + weak coaching**
- **Action:** If funders exist, raise and focus recognition; else revisit coaching model
- **Key question:** Can recognition translate to revenue (consulting, infrastructure licensing)?

## What to track monthly

**Recognition metrics:**
- Essay readers (total + per essay)
- Spec engagement (views, discussions, contributors)
- Inbound mentions ("you're the expert")
- Community positioning (how you're described by others)
- Fundraising conversations (aligned funders interested?)

**Coaching metrics:**
- Monthly recurring revenue (MRR)
- Customer count (absolute)
- Retention rate (month 2+)
- Workshop attendance vs capacity
- Testimonials and case studies

**Critical questions at Month 12:**
1. Do people recognize you as "the calibration person"?
2. Are essays/specs reaching 200+ readers consistently?
3. Is there fundraising interest from aligned funders?
4. Is coaching revenue ≥€5k/month?
5. Which track feels more aligned with intrinsic motivation?
