# O-Essay-Reach: 50+ Readers Per Essay

## SMART Goal

**Specific:** Essays reach ≥50 readers in AI/rationalist communities

**Measurable:** Page views, unique visitors, LessWrong karma

**Achievable:** LessWrong posts average 100-500 views; 50 is conservative

**Relevant:** Leading indicator for recognition (R1 track)

**Time-bound:** 3-month essay publishing period

## Measurement Method

- **Analytics:** Page views (Ghost), LessWrong karma/views
- **Engagement:** Comments, shares, citations
- **Inbound:** "You're the calibration expert" mentions

## Target Value

50+ readers per essay (average across 3+ essays)

## Kill Threshold

<50 readers after 3 months → Recognition track failing
