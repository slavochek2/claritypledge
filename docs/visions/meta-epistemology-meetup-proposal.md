## The Goal

We agree on *how* to understand each other (the mini-pledge). Does that help us agree on *what* we believe?

Two questions:
1. How do we get more people to agree on the rules?
2. How do we make sure they understand what they're agreeing to?

---

## The Meetup

**Who**: 5-12 people — philosophers, AI folks, founders, people curious about how we know things.

**Entry**: Sign up via the platform, take the mini-pledge. Manifesto is optional — we discuss it live.

**Mini-pledge vs. full pledge**: The [full pledge](https://claritypledge.com/sign-pledge) is to everyone, everywhere, forever. The mini-pledge is just to this group, for this event.

**What happens**:
- I prepare topics and questions for the group
- We discuss — anyone can ask: "can you explain back what I said?"
- The other person tries; you confirm or correct
- We notice: did understanding happen? What helped? What didn't?

**What we're learning**:
- Does checking understanding change trust?
- Does agreeing on the rules help us agree on beliefs?
- What would make this work at scale?

**Cadence**: Regular meetups — enough to learn and adjust.
