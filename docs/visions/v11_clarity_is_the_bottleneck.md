# v11 — Clarity is the bottleneck

*Captured 2026-10-08. Long-range vision, `UNTESTED`.*

## The idea

As AI agents get better at execution, anything that can be written down as a clear task gets done by an agent, often many times over. What stays scarce is the step before: working out what the problem actually is, and checking that the people involved understood each other. Conversation, not execution, becomes the bottleneck.

## What follows for work platforms

Job platforms today are built around posts: someone writes a job, someone applies. If a job can be written precisely, an agent will increasingly take it. The work that remains for people is the work that cannot yet be written as a job: discovery, diagnosis, deciding what to build, education, consulting and coaching. A platform for that work would match people for **meaningful conversations** and record **verified understanding** (letters, points, positions) rather than CVs and job posts.

Connections on such a platform have levels: **unknown** (we have not met), **known** (we recognise each other's name and face), and **partner** (we have an agreement). Each person can have an agent that helps prepare, follow and check conversations (see P1431, P1437).

## Where it might start

A local quick-jobs marketplace (short engagements, simple legal paperwork for short-term work) is a concrete entry point. It is the opposite end of the thesis, jobs that *are* easy to write down, so it also tests the thesis.

## Falsifier

If, on a work platform we run, the jobs people post are mostly execution tasks and a "clarify the problem first" step goes unused, the thesis is weaker for that market. If people pay for or repeatedly book discovery conversations over execution jobs, it is supported.
