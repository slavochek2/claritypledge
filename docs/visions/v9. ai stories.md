Here is a breakdown of your idea, a formal name for the concept, and an evaluation of its potential.

1. The Concept: "The Living Narrative"
We can call this Recursive Narrative Synthesis (RNS). The core philosophy is that a story is not a static file (like an MP3), but a dynamic knowledge graph that matures through the friction of being understood.

The Lifecycle of a Living Story
The Forge (0 to 10): You tell a story to Person A. The AI mediates. Person A paraphrases until the AI (and you) agree the "semantic delta" is zero. The story is now "hardened."

The Transmission: You send the story to Person B. They don't just listen; they interact. They must prove understanding to the "Story Entity."

The Synthesis (Feedback Loop): You verify Person B. Your corrections are ingested by the AI. The story "learns" its own nuances and evolves.

The Autonomy: Eventually, the story becomes a "Digital Twin" of that specific piece of your inner world, capable of representing you and even performing tasks (the "skills" you mentioned).

2. Evaluation of the Idea
To evaluate this properly, we have to look at the Friction vs. Value trade-off.

Therapy/Coaching: A patient records their "Internal Monologue." The AI helps them see how the story changes as they heal.

3. How to Make This Work (The "Secret Sauce")
The biggest hurdle is User Friction. Most people are lazy listeners. To make this "fun" and viable, the AI needs to act as a Gamified Bridge:

The AI as "The Guardian": Instead of a boring test, the AI acts like a character in the story that only lets the listener pass once they "know the secret word" (the correct paraphrase).

The "Context Debt": The story should track "Context Debt"—the things the storyteller assumes the listener knows but hasn't explained yet.

Interactive Nuance: If Person B says something slightly wrong, the AI shouldn't just say "No." It should say, "You're 80% there, but you're missing the 'why' behind the creator's anger in that moment. Try again."

4. Technical Feasibility
This is actually very possible in 2026.

Vector Databases: Can store the "accumulated context" and versions of the story.

Voice Synthesis: Can allow the story to "speak" back to the listener in your voice, but with updated information.

2. The Final Synthesis
Problem Statement
Human knowledge is trapped in Silos of Intent. We use language to communicate, but language is a "Lossy Compression." This leads to False Agreements (we think we agree but don't) and False Disagreements (we argue over words while meaning the same thing). There is currently no "Version Control" for our inner worlds, meaning we can't track how we learn or scale our perspectives without manual repetition.

Solution Statement: The Semantic Proxy Ecosystem (SPE)
A decentralized environment where stories are Active Agents that manage and defend Falsifiable Points.

The Agent: A digital twin of a specific narrative that calibrates listeners to a 10/10 understanding.

The Glue: Points serve as the "API" between different stories.

The Chain: Every interaction is a "Commit." Stories drift and evolve, creating a visible "Learning Journey."

The Fork: Users can adopt a point by attaching their own story to it, creating a "Hybrid" narrative that supports the same truth.

3. The KISS MVP: "The Calibration Git"
To test this without the complexity of a full blockchain or multi-agent "Story War," build this:

The Record: User A records a story. AI extracts Point X (Falsifiable/Hard-to-Vary).

The Link: User A sends a link to User B.

The Loop (Reciprocity):

User B must reach a 10/10 understanding of Story A (The "Proof of Work").

Once User B hits 10/10, the "Reciprocity Gate" opens. User B is prompted: "Now, share your story on Point X."

The Drift: User A gets a notification: "User B understood you but added this nuance. Do you want to 'Merge' this into a new version of your story?"

The Stance: A simple dashboard shows the "Version Tree" of the story and how many people "Understand + Agree" vs. "Understand + Disagree."
