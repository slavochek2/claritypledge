# R1: Essay Writing (Recognition Track, Months 0-3)

## TL;DR

**Hypothesis:** Essays positioning calibration as "AI infrastructure" reach aligned communities and build recognition.

**Test:** Publish 3+ essays, share on LessWrong/HN, track reach and engagement.

**Success:** 50+ readers per essay, 5+ discussions, inbound "you're the expert" mentions.

**Kill signal:** <50 readers per essay OR zero engagement after 3 months.
