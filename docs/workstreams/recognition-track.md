# Recognition Track (R-track)

## What Belongs Here

**Definition:** Work that builds thought leadership, positioning, and visibility through essays, publishing, and technical content.

**Classification criteria:**
- Content creation (essays, specs, technical writing)
- Publishing infrastructure (Ghost, Substack, personal site)
- Audience reach and engagement
- Positioning as "calibration expert"

**Examples:**
- R1: Essay Writing (50+ readers per essay)
- R2: Spec Publishing (technical depth demonstrations)
- R3: Recognition Checkpoint (inbound requests from funders)

---

## Time Horizon

**3-12 months** — Primary track

The recognition track is the **PRIMARY** path. Essays reach aligned funders (Lightcone, Protocol Labs, SFF) who value calibration + AI alignment.

**Characteristics:**
- Medium time to payoff (3-6 months to build audience)
- High leverage (essays reach 100s, workshops reach 10s)
- Network effects via shares/citations
- Opens doors to funding conversations

---

## Resource Constraints

**Required capabilities:**
- Writing (already have — see existing essays on Substack)
- Technical depth (calibration research, AI alignment context)
- Audience reach (rationalist communities, AI safety folks)
- Publishing infrastructure (Ghost, personal site)

**Not required:**
- Large existing audience (can start with 0)
- Facilitation skills (writing > teaching)
- Payment processing (recognition ≠ revenue yet)

---

## Decision Framework

**Choose R-track if the work:**
- Creates or improves content (essays, specs, technical writing)
- Builds publishing infrastructure
- Increases audience reach (distribution, SEO, shares)
- Positions as thought leader in calibration space

**Don't choose R-track if:**
- Work is about workshop delivery → C-track
- Work requires scale to validate → X-track
- Work is product enhancement → E-track
- Work is pure infrastructure → foundation

## Success Criteria

**Track succeeds if:**
- 50+ readers per essay (R1 outcome)
- 3+ published specs with community discussion (R2 outcome)
- Inbound requests from aligned funders (R3 outcome)
- Recognition as "go-to person" for calibration

**Track fails if:**
- Essays get <10 readers after 6 months
- No community engagement (no shares, no discussion)
- Funders don't care about calibration (no inbound)
