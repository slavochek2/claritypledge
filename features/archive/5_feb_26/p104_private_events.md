# P104: Private Events (Meeting Prep)

**Status:** Placeholder
**Priority:** TBD

## Concept

Events can be private - a personal workspace to prepare cards before a 1-on-1 conversation.

## Use Cases

1. **Feedback session prep** - Prepare points you want to make, cards ready before the meeting
2. **1-on-1 meeting prep** - Dump thoughts, organize into shareable cards
3. **Difficult conversation prep** - Structure what you want to say, calibrate understanding after

## Core Insight

Feedback giving IS calibration - "Did they actually understand what I meant?"

The prep cards become the Points you want calibrated.

## Open Questions

- [ ] Does the other person need to be on the platform?
- [ ] What's the reveal moment - all cards at once, or drip during conversation?
- [ ] Is the value in prep (structuring thoughts) or verification (confirming understanding)?
- [ ] How does this connect to the existing /live verification flow?
- [ ] Privacy model: private → shared with one person → public?

## Risk

Could become generic "meeting prep" tool that loses calibration magic. Utility without insight.
