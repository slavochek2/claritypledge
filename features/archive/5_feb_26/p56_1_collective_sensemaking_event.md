# P56.1: Event as Collective Sensemaking Container

**Status:** Exploration (evolved from P56)
**Priority:** High
**Created:** 2026-01-13
**Origin:** Innovation strategy session — reframe from "verification tracker" to "collective sensemaking tool"

---

## What Changed from P56

P56 framed events as **verification tracking** — counting who verified whom.

P56.1 reframes events as **collective sensemaking** — the group builds shared understanding of ideas that no individual had before.

| P56 | P56.1 |
|-----|-------|
| Unit of value: Verification (Alice ↔ Bob) | Unit of value: Enriched Idea (Idea X gains depth) |
| Success: "12 verifications, 4 across disagreement" | Success: "3 ideas now have multi-perspective understanding" |
| Map shows: Who verified whom | Map shows: What each idea means across perspectives |
| Outcome: Trust between individuals | Outcome: Group can ACT on shared understanding |

---

## The Core Insight

**You're not building a verification tracker. You're building a collective sensemaking tool.**

The verification loop is the MECHANISM.

The outcome is: **The group now has a shared, enriched understanding of Idea X that no individual had before.**

That's different from "Alice and Bob verified."

That's: **"The group now KNOWS what Idea X means across perspectives — and everyone can see it."**

---

## The New North Star

> When a Clarity event ends, each idea discussed should be RICHER than when it started — because multiple perspectives have been captured, verified, and made visible to everyone.

The event creates **collective artifacts** (enriched ideas), not just **activity logs** (verification counts).

## The Hypothesis (Revised)

### H2.1: Multi-Perspective Visibility Changes Group Dynamics

When people see "here's what Idea X means to people who agree AND people who disagree":

- Do they feel **less polarized**? ("Oh, their reasons make sense even though I disagree")
- Do they act **more curious**? (Less defensive, more exploratory)
- Does the group **converge on what matters**? (Even while still disagreeing on positions)
- Can the group **act together** despite disagreement? (Shared understanding enables coordination)

### What We're Testing

Not: "Will people verify understanding?"
But: "Does seeing multi-perspective ideas change how a group feels and acts?"

## The Idea-Centric Model

### The Idea as Artifact

An **Enriched Idea** is an idea that has accumulated:
- Multiple positions (agree / disagree / unsure)
- Reasoning from each perspective ("I agree because...", "I disagree because...")
- Verified cross-understanding ("Alice understands why Bob disagrees")
- The delta that verification revealed ("What Alice learned about Bob's view")

1. **"Why People Agree/Disagree"** — Captured reasoning, not just positions
2. **"Verified Bridges"** — Shows WHAT was learned, not just WHO verified

## The Event Flow (Revised)

### Before Event

1. **Organizer seeds ideas** — 2-3 ideas worth exploring
2. **Participants stake positions** — Agree / Disagree / Unsure
3. **Participants add reasoning** (optional) — "I agree because..."

### During Event

1. **Everyone sees the Idea Cards** — Multi-perspective view
2. **Gaps are visible** — "These people disagree but haven't verified"
3. **Pairs self-select for verification** — Motivated by curiosity about the other view
4. **Verification captures WHAT was learned** — Not just "did they verify"
5. **Idea Cards update in real-time** — Reasoning and insights accumulate

### After Event

1. **Each idea has a summary** — "What this group now knows"
2. **The summary is the artifact** — Shareable, valuable, actionable
3. **Participants keep the insight** — Even if they still disagree on position

## Open Questions to Explore

### Q1: How Does Reasoning Get Captured? ✓ DECIDED

**Answer:** Reasoning emerges during verification, not at stake time.

At verification, the person explains not just the idea but the **WHY behind the idea** — their intention, their reasoning. The transcript captures it naturally.

**Flow:**
- Position staking = just agree/disagree/unsure (low friction)
- Reasoning emerges during explain-back (the paraphrase attempt)
- The "why" is captured in transcript: what they said + how it was understood

**Why this works:** No essays required. People SPEAK their reasoning. AI extracts it from transcripts.

### Q2: What Gets Captured During Verification? ✓ DECIDED

**Answer:** The DELTA between paraphrases reveals what was learned.

We capture the rating "how much do you feel X understands you" — updated continuously after each paraphrase. Each difference between last paraphrase and new paraphrase IS what they learned (even if they don't explicitly say it).

**The transcript shows:**
- Paraphrase 1: Got it wrong (rating: 4/10)
- Paraphrase 2: Got it closer (rating: 7/10)
- Paraphrase 3: Got it right (rating: 9/10)

The delta is visible. The AI can extract: "Here's what they learned between round 1 and round 3."

**Additional insight:** After verification completes on one idea, we learn what OTHER ideas the participants bring up. This reveals how ideas are interconnected. ("People who agree on X also tend to agree on Y.")

### Q3: Who Writes the Idea Summary? ✓ DECIDED

**Answer:** The AI writes it, from transcripts.

Each idea incorporates transcripts from verifications about it and **learns**. The AI synthesizes:
- Positions from each side
- Reasoning extracted from explain-backs
- What was learned in each verification
- Connections to other ideas that emerged

**The "wide" definition of an idea:**
- Narrow: The text of the idea itself
- Wide: Incorporation of all views, perspectives, intentions, and reasons about this idea

The AI-idea is the wide version — the enriched artifact.

### Q4: Can Ideas Learn About Themselves? ✓ DECIDED

**Answer:** Yes. Ideas learn from verifications AND from other ideas.

When a person explains why they disagree, they reference OTHER ideas (or create them on the fly). The system captures these connections.

**The AI-Idea can:**
- Accumulate perspectives from verifications
- Learn from other ideas it gets connected to
- Help future users discover their blind spots BEFORE verification

**The flow:**
```
1. BEFORE VERIFICATION
   - Person sees idea + positions
   - AI-idea says: "Based on past verifications, people who agree
     often miss X. People who disagree often miss Y."
   - Person thinks: "Hmm, do I have that blind spot?"

2. DURING VERIFICATION
   - Normal /live flow
   - Transcript captured

3. AFTER VERIFICATION
   - AI shows: "Here's what you learned" (the delta)
   - AI shows: "Here's what was already captured by others"
   - AI updates the idea's knowledge

4. FOR FUTURE PEOPLE
   - The AI-idea is now richer
   - Next person benefits from accumulated insights
```

### Q5: How Does Enriched Understanding Reduce Polarization? ✓ DECIDED

**Answer:** Measure before/after, and show people their own gaps.

**Measurement Signals:**

| Signal | Before Event | After Event | How to Capture |
|--------|--------------|-------------|----------------|
| **Felt understanding** | "They don't get it" | "They get it, we just disagree" | Survey: 1-10 |
| **Position certainty** | 10/10 confident | 7/10 confident | Survey before/after |
| **Language** | "They're wrong" | "I see why they think that" | Observed/self-report |
| **Willingness to engage** | Avoid disagreers | Seek out disagreers | Behavioral |
| **Action alignment** | "We can't work together" | "We can move forward" | Self-report |

**The Simplest Test:**
> "On a scale of 1-10, how well do you feel the people who DISAGREE with you understand your position?"

If this goes UP after verifications → polarization reduced.

**The Key Insight: Show People Their Gaps**

People are bad at knowing what they learned. Hindsight bias + curse of knowledge = "I always knew that."

But the system can show:
> "Before verification, you thought Tom believed X. After verification, you learned he actually believes Y."

That's PROOF of a gap. And proof motivates:
> "If I was wrong about Tom, what else am I wrong about?"

**Showing past unknowns motivates discovering future unknowns.** What we don't know is much bigger than what we know. The AI-idea can help people discover their unknowns BEFORE they engage in verification.

### Q6: What's the Minimum to Test This? ✓ DECIDED

**Answer:** Build the MVP. Let real usage generate real data. Let AI synthesize.

**Why skip manual testing:**
- Manual tests require humans to self-report learning (they're bad at it)
- AI can see patterns in transcripts that humans miss
- Real usage generates real data faster
- You learn faster by shipping

**The MVP Path:**

1. **Ideas + Positions in /live**
   - Stake agree/disagree/unsure on ideas
   - Low friction — no reasoning required upfront

2. **Verification records which idea it's about**
   - Link session to specific idea
   - Store transcript with timestamps

3. **AI synthesizes post-verification**
   - Input: Transcript + idea + positions + ratings over time
   - Output: "What Person A learned about Person B's view" (the delta)
   - Output: Key perspectives on this idea
   - Output: Connections to other ideas mentioned

4. **Show the gap to participants**
   - "Before: You thought they believed X"
   - "After: They actually believe Y"
   - Generated from the paraphrase delta

5. **Idea accumulates insights**
   - Each verification enriches the idea
   - AI updates: "What we know about this idea"
   - Future participants see the enriched version

**What to observe:**
- Do enriched ideas feel valuable?
- Do people refer to the AI summaries?
- Does seeing the gap change behavior?
- Do ideas get richer over time?

## Appendix: The Conceptual Shift

### From Verification Graph to Knowledge Graph

**Verification Graph (P56):**
```
Alice ──verified── Bob
  │                  │
  │                  │
Carol ──verified── Dave
```
Shows: Who talked to whom.

**Knowledge Graph (P56.1):**
```
                  IDEA X
                 /  |  \
          agree/   |   \disagree
               /    |    \
          Alice   Carol   Bob
              \    |     /
               \   |    /
            [verified understanding]
                   │
                   ▼
        "What we now know about X"
```
Shows: How the idea is understood across the group.

### The Insight

The verification is a PROCESS that happens between people.

The enriched idea is a PRODUCT that belongs to the group.

The event's job is to produce enriched ideas, not just verification logs.
