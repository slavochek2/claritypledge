# P78: User Personas

## Status: Planning

## Why This Matters

We keep referencing "event organizers" and "users" without clear personas. This creates blindspots in product decisions.

## Personas to Define

### Primary Personas

| Persona | Role | Why They Matter |
|---------|------|-----------------|
| **Event Organizer** | Hosts events, seeds Stories/Points | Growth engine — B2B2C model depends on them |
| **Event Attendee** | Participates in /live, may become Pledger | The "C" in B2B2C |
| **Pledger** | Signed the pledge, has public profile | Social proof, ~1% graduation |

### Secondary Personas

| Persona | Role | Why They Matter |
|---------|------|-----------------|
| **Organic Visitor** | Discovers via pledger profile or search | Journey B — potential direct-to-pledger path |
| **Verified Listener** | High verification score | H4 hypothesis — do they become trusted? |

## Key Questions Per Persona

For each persona, answer:

1. **Job to be Done** — What progress are they trying to make?
2. **Current Alternatives** — How do they solve this today?
3. **Pain Points** — What frustrates them about alternatives?
4. **Switching Trigger** — What would make them try Clarity Pledge?
5. **Success Metric** — How do they know it worked?

## Event Organizer Deep Dive (Priority)

This is the critical persona. Need to answer:

- **Who are they?** (Workshop facilitators? Team leads? Community organizers? Conference hosts?)
- **What's their existing pain?** (People talk past each other? Debates go nowhere? Polarization?)
- **Why would they choose Clarity events?** (Differentiation from normal events)
- **What do they need from us?** (Tooling? Training? Templates?)
- **How do we reach them?** (Channels, messaging)

## Deliverables

1. One-pager per persona with JTBD analysis
2. Event Organizer interview script (if we need to validate assumptions)
3. Persona-to-feature mapping (which features serve which personas)
