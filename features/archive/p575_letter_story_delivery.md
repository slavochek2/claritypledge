# P575: Letters — Story Delivery as Pre-Session Ritual

**Epic:** story-first (P523 vision)
**Depends on:** P561 (comprehension slider), P567 (false belief content exists)

## Problem

Stories and points exist as cards in a feed, but there's no way to *send* them to someone as a deliberate act. A co-founder, workshop participant, or Person B in the briefing protocol has no curated entry point — they land on a feed, not a letter addressed to them.

The gap between "content exists" and "someone receives it with intention" is unserved. Without a delivery container, stories are ambient content, not pre-work for a live session.

## Concept

A **letter** is a curated container of one or more stories, sent from author to reader as a ritual reading experience. The reader engages with each story (comprehension slider from P561), and the letter produces a gap map — a summary of where understanding is high/low before a /live session.

**The letter IS the pre-work.** Instead of "read these 3 stories before we meet," the letter is a full-screen, deliberate-pacing experience that guides the reader through stories one at a time, collects their comprehension self-assessment, and surfaces where the gaps are.

## How It Helps Later

1. **Workshop follow-up:** After a false-belief workshop (P567), send participants a letter with the stories/points they engaged with. Their comprehension slider data persists. Creates a return trigger.

2. **Briefing protocol (Stage 0b):** The letter IS the briefing. Person A's stories are packaged into a letter → Person B receives it → reads, assesses → gap map generated → /live session starts at depth, not cold.

3. **Clarity Partnership prep:** Between recurring /live sessions, partners send letters with new stories. The gap map shows what to discuss next — no more "what should we talk about?"

4. **Async gap revelation without Slava:** Currently Slava facilitates every gap reveal. A letter with comprehension sliders produces gap data without facilitation. Reader sees their gap after author counter-assesses. Scales the "holy shit" moment.

## Design Direction

From UX exploration (2026-03-22/23): ritual, not efficiency. Deliberate slowness — cream paper, serif typography, full-screen single-step reading, cinematic gap reveal. "The moment before a meditation teacher rings the bell." Gamification explicitly rejected in favor of this.

## Open Questions

- Can a letter contain multiple stories? (Likely yes, but UI flow for multi-story receive needs design)
- Does the reader set position on points during letter reading, or only after?
- How does the gap map relate to the scatter plot (understanding × position) from P563?
- Is a letter one-directional (author → reader) or can readers reply with their own letter (ping-pong calibration)?
