## UAT-1: Existing User — One-Click Sign from Invite Email

**Given:** Creator has sent an agreement to an existing user's email
**When:** The existing user clicks "Review & Sign Agreement" in the invite email within 1 hour
**Then:** The user arrives on the accept page already authenticated
**Verify:** The page shows "I Accept & Co-Sign" button — NOT "Sign In to Co-Sign" or OTP flow
**Verify:** Partner name is pre-filled from their profile (not editable)
**Verify:** One click on "I Accept & Co-Sign" completes the signing

**How to test:**
1. Create an agreement with a partner email that belongs to an existing account
2. Check Mailgun logs for the invitation email
3. The CTA link should be a Supabase `/auth/v1/verify?token=...&redirect_to=...` URL (not a direct accept URL)
4. Click the link — should land on accept page with active session

## UAT-8: Creator Cannot Use Partner's Magic Link

**Given:** Creator sends agreement to partner (existing user)
**When:** Creator intercepts the invite email and clicks the magic link
**Then:** The magic link authenticates as the partner (not the creator) — this is expected Supabase behavior
**Then:** The accept page loads with partner session, partner CAN sign (the magic link IS the partner's auth)
**Security note:** This is the same threat model as standard magic links — email interception = session. The mitigation is email security, not application logic.
