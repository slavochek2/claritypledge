## The Hypothesis

**Core insight**: Understanding is a trust signal.

Someone who can accurately represent others' positions demonstrates a more robust grip on reality than someone who can only defend their own. We don't need an arbiter of truth — we need a protocol that surfaces who has done the work to understand.

**What we're testing**:
- Does the protocol create the right dynamic?
- Does understanding someone's position change how much you trust their reasoning?
- What emerges that we didn't expect?

## Connection to Clarity Pledge

This group is the first "cell." We test manually what the platform will eventually scale.

The platform supports:
- **The Pledge** — public commitment to verify understanding
- **/live** — real-time verification sessions
- **Calibration profiles** — tracking how well confidence matches reality

Theory of change: You don't need everyone to verify with everyone. You need √N verified bridges (for 8 billion people, ~90,000 strategic verifications) to create common knowledge.
