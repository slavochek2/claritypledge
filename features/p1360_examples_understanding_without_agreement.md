# P1360: Short explainer examples: understanding without agreement

> **Founder framing, verbatim:** "Examples? Yeah, I heard it many times that examples are important. Maybe this is part of the spec for the website update or onboarding or whatever."

**Placeholder.** Needs `/spec-create` before work starts.

## Problem
Newcomers keep asking for concrete examples. The idea that you can fully understand a position you
still reject is easy to state and hard to grasp without seeing it happen. Several people have said
examples matter most, independently of each other.

## Direction (unvalidated)
- A few short explainer clips (video first, text transcript alongside), each one showing two people:
  paraphrase, a gap revealed by the rating, the gap closed, and both still disagreeing.
- Pick topics where both sides hold a coherent base (e.g. two religious traditions). Avoid
  topics where one side may only repeat arguments without understanding them (e.g. flat earth):
  nobody has shown that such a pair can reach a high verified score, so it would make a weak
  example.
- Reusable in onboarding, events, outreach, and the website.
