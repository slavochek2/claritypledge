# P1362: AI condenses what the speaker means before the listener paraphrases

> **Founder framing, verbatim:** "Using AI to condense what the speaker means, because sometimes the gap comes from the person explaining, not the listener. - Yes, you can create a placeholder spec for this"

**Placeholder. Idea only**, not validated. Needs `/spec-create`.

## Problem
Sometimes a gap in understanding starts with the speaker, not the listener: the point is long,
tangled or badly structured, and the listener spends a long time just finding it.

## Direction
An optional step where AI compresses and structures the speaker's statement. The speaker confirms
the summary, and only then does the listener paraphrase. The speaker's own rating stays the oracle;
the AI summary must never stand in for verified understanding.

## Open questions
- Does it reduce the time to reach a shared 10, or does it hide gaps behind the AI's wording?
- Should it be a speaker-side draft aid only, never shown to the listener as "the meaning"?
