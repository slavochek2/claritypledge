# P603: Clarity Practice Community (Support Group)

**Hypothesis:** H-Community-Retention
**Related:** P92 (platform communities — different concept, deferred)

---

## Problem

Clarity Partner Agreements are signed but practice is unstructured. Pledgers (11) have no practice habit. Partners need accountability and shared learning to actually use the protocol in their real relationships.

## Concept

A **support group** (not a practice group) for people bringing calibrated communication into their real relationships. Members share experiences: "I tried the excuse protocol with my co-founder and here's what happened."

**Key distinction:** Participants do NOT practice with each other (strangers). They practice in their real relationships and come to the group to share, troubleshoot, and encourage.

**Format options (test cheaply):**
- WhatsApp group (async, lowest friction)
- Monthly video call (structured sharing)
- Both (async + monthly sync)

**Analogy:** AA meeting, not a debate club. Running club where you share training experiences, not a track meet.

## Field Evidence (2026-03-29)

Stranger at event rejected Partner Agreement with Slava ("we don't know each other") but immediately saw value with boyfriend and future business partner. Confirmed: the protocol lives in real relationships, the community supports it from outside.

## Open Questions

1. What's the minimum number of active Partner Agreements before a group is viable? (Guess: 5)
2. Does the group accelerate new Partner Agreement creation (members recruit their own pairs)?
3. What format works — structured sharing rounds or organic conversation?

## When to Build

After 5+ active Partner Agreements exist. No product feature needed — this is a facilitated group, not a platform feature. P92 (platform communities) may eventually serve this but is not required.
