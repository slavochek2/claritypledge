# P605: Pledge as Graduation (Future Concept)

**Status:** Idea capture only. No immediate action — current pledge flow stays as-is.

---

## Idea

The Pledge currently functions as an entry point (anyone can sign). Field data (2026-03-29) suggests it should eventually become a **graduation milestone** — earned after demonstrated practice, not aspirational.

**Proposed progression:**
```
Positions on points (low cost, concrete, public)
  → Partner Agreement with someone specific (bilateral, private)
    → Practice (~10 partners, real asks/excuses over time)
      → Pledge (earned credential, not entry point)
```

## Evidence

- 11 current pledgers — all decorative (no practice habit, no active Partner Agreements for most)
- Stranger at event (2026-03-29): agreed to all points, rejected pledge — "doesn't want to display something that might be misunderstood"
- The Pledge has social display risk when viewers don't know what it means

## Why Not Now

- Current pledge flow gathers early supporters — useful signal even if decorative
- The full graduation flow (track practice, count partners, verify habit) doesn't exist yet
- Rebuilding pledge semantics requires rethinking `has_pledged` in the product + the 11 existing pledgers
- Higher priority: build P581 (letters) and get Partner Agreements working first

## When to Revisit

After P603 (practice community) validates that a support group drives real practice. If partners are actively practicing, the graduation concept becomes testable.
