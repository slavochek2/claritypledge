# P687: Post-Flip Journaling — Structured Weekly Reflection

## Problem

Badge proves comprehension at a point in time. But the goal is ongoing practice — incorporating verified understanding into daily conversations. Currently no bridge exists between "I experienced the flip" and "I use verification in my life." People forget, habits don't form, the flip evaporates.

## Approach

Structured weekly reflection prompt: "Which conversations this week contained important agreements or disagreements? Did I verify understanding in any of them?" This surfaces WHERE verification applies in real life — the discovery step that turns abstract comprehension into situated practice.

**Ideas explored (from P685 discussion):**
- Weekly journaling surfacing real situations for verification
- Cohort/clarithon model — participants compete to verify more people in first 10 hours
- Volunteer tutor assignment for high-R₀ participants
- Intra-cohort competition + developers improving the app (hackathon hybrid)

## Research Questions

1. What format produces journaling compliance? (Push notification, email, WhatsApp prompt?)
2. Does journaling increase badge-to-badge conversion (R₀)?
3. Does naming specific conversations increase likelihood of applying verification?
4. Is this a product feature or a community practice (WhatsApp group, monthly call)?

## Time Box

Research after workshop #1 + badge Step 1 observations. No build before P686 ships.

## Deliverable

Decision: product feature vs. community practice. If product: implementation spec. If community: facilitation guide addition.
