# P1010 UAT — Clarity Organizations community container

Manual verification checklist for the founder-facing acceptance criteria. Run against a deployed
feature branch, not localhost mocks — several of these depend on real RLS/auth behavior.

## Org page rendering

- [ ] **Given** I visit `/org/cm` **when** the page loads **then** I see the org name, blurb,
      member count, and the Events tab selected by default (has_events=true).
- [ ] **Given** I visit `/org/champions` **when** the page loads **then** the About tab is
      selected by default (has_events=false) and there is no Events tab at all.
- [ ] **Given** I visit `/org/some-made-up-slug` **when** the page loads **then** I see a
      not-found state, never a create-org form.

## Join

- [ ] **Given** I am NOT logged in **when** I click Join **then** I am redirected to log in,
      with a way to return to the org page afterward.
- [ ] **Given** I am logged in and not a member, viewing the About tab **when** I read it
      **then** I see the note "You're not a member yet" above the pending Clarity Organization
      Agreement.
- [ ] **Given** I am logged in and not a member **when** I click Join **then** I see the
      Clarity Organization Agreement with the intro "By joining this clarity organization,
      I commit to every other member:" and an "I Accept & Join" action.
- [ ] **Given** I accept **when** the accept completes **then** I appear on the Members roster
      immediately with an accepted date, and the header CTA becomes "Manage membership ▾".
- [ ] **Given** I click Join twice in a row **when** the second click resolves **then** nothing
      breaks (no duplicate row, no error toast) — Join is idempotent.

## Leave

- [ ] **Given** I am a member **when** I open "Manage membership ▾" and click Leave **then**
      I am removed from the Members roster and the CTA reverts to "Join".

## Members roster

- [ ] **Given** an org with zero members **when** I open the Members tab **then** I see the
      org blurb plus a "Be the first to join" prompt — never a blank grid.
- [ ] **Given** I am a member of `champions` only **when** I view `cm`'s Members tab **then**
      I do NOT appear there — rosters are per-org, not global.
- [ ] **Given** the Members tab **when** it renders **then** cards use the same PledgerCard
      look as `/pledgers` (avatar, name, role, reason if present).

## Security / RLS (spot-check — full coverage is in the integration test)

- [ ] **Given** a public org **when** I view it signed out **then** the roster is visible
      (no login wall on read).
- [ ] **Given** I am signed in **when** I inspect network requests during Join **then** no
      request sends a `terms_version` or `role` field — both are server-defaulted.

## Regression

- [ ] **Given** I visit `/cm` (the standalone calendar page, unrelated to `/org/cm`)
      **when** it loads **then** it is unchanged — same calendar embed, no org chrome.
- [ ] **Given** I visit `/events` **when** it loads **then** it is unchanged (the redirect
      to `/org/cm` was deferred by founder decision — confirm it was NOT built).
