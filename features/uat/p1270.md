## UAT-1: Profile fetches story media (§1)

### UAT-1.1: A nested story with a video renders the video
**Given:** a point on a profile has a linked story whose `video_url` is set
**When:** the reader expands that point's linked stories
**Then:** the nested `QuotedStory` card renders the video (via `StoryMedia`'s thumbnail/player path)
**Verify:** `src/tests/p1270-profile-story-media.test.tsx` — "renders the video for a nested story that has one"

### UAT-1.2: The nested story's timecoded quote pills render
**Given:** the same linked story also has `video_quotes` with at least one quote
**When:** the point is expanded
**Then:** `StoryVideoQuotes` renders the quote pills, each opening the source at its timecode
**Verify:** `src/tests/p1270-profile-story-media.test.tsx` — "renders the timecoded quote pills for a nested story with video quotes"

### UAT-1.3: A nested story with only a photo renders the photo
**Given:** a linked story has `image_url` set and no `video_url`
**When:** the point is expanded
**Then:** the photo renders via `StoryImage`
**Verify:** `src/tests/p1270-profile-story-media.test.tsx` — "renders a photo for a nested story whose only media is an image"
