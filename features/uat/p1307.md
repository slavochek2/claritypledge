# UAT — P1307: Event transcription — starts at the ready screen, follows you across pages, lands in your sessions

Given/When/Then scenarios covering every Acceptance Criterion in
`features/p1307_event_transcription_from_ready_across_pages_into_sessions.md`, numbered to
match that spec's four AC groups (Start and consent, Across pages, End and saved
transcript, Live text). Each scenario names a Verify method. T-numbered real-device
scenarios (§8) cover the Open Questions the spec left to `/verify` (T1, T3, T4).

---

## 1. Start and consent

**UAT-1.1 — switch off by default, Continue captures nothing**
- Given: a signed-in, RSVP'd attendee at `/events/:slug/ready` who has never been transcribed for this event
- When: they press Continue without touching the switch
- Then: they land on `/meet` with no bar; server-side, no `transcribe_room_members` row with `consent_given_at` exists for them, no slice was received, no archive chunk exists
- Verify: `e2e/p1307-event-transcription.spec.ts` "switch off + Continue lands on /meet with NO bar" + Supabase query

**UAT-1.2 — switch on + Continue starts capture**
- Given: the same attendee at `/events/:slug/ready`
- When: they tap the switch on, then Continue
- Then: they land on `/meet`, the bar shows "● Transcribing for AI insights", and their speech appears in the room transcript
- Verify: `e2e/p1307-event-transcription.spec.ts` "switch on + Continue lands on /meet with the bar showing"; manual: speak, watch the transcript update

**UAT-1.3 — switch off, then re-visit and switch on**
- Given: the attendee went to `/meet` with the switch off
- When: they navigate back to `/events/:slug/ready` and switch it on, then Continue
- Then: capture starts from that point
- Verify: `e2e/p1307-event-transcription.spec.ts` "going back to the ready screen and switching on starts capture from there"

**UAT-1.4 — room RPC failure never raises the mic prompt**
- Given: `enter_transcribe_room` is forced to fail (network kill, or a server-side flag in a test build)
- When: the attendee taps the switch on and presses Continue
- Then: they land on `/meet` with no bar, a failure message, and the browser's microphone permission prompt never appears
- Verify: manual — throttle/block the RPC endpoint via devtools, confirm no `getUserMedia` prompt; automated coverage limited to the unit level (`p1307-capture-state-machine.test.ts` "starting -> idle on join failure")

**UAT-1.5 — the room carries the event's id; a bare /transcribe visitor is not pulled in**
- Given: an attendee has started transcription for event E
- When: a different signed-in user opens `/transcribe` with no code
- Then: they do not land in event E's room
- Verify: `e2e/integration/p1307-enter-room-event-access.spec.ts` (`p_event_id`-scoped room selection) + manual cross-check

**UAT-1.6 — a return visit reaches /ready with the saved slider value (D10)**
- Given: an attendee already set a readiness value on an earlier visit, and is not yet being transcribed for this event
- When: they open `/events/:slug/room` again
- Then: they land on `/ready` (not skipped to `/meet`), with their saved slider value shown, and the switch visible
- Verify: `e2e/p1307-event-transcription.spec.ts`; `src/tests/p1307-ready-screen.test.tsx` (gate routing source assertions)

**UAT-1.7 — already-transcribed attendee goes straight to /meet**
- Given: an attendee is already being transcribed for this event (capture running or paused)
- When: they open `/events/:slug/room` again
- Then: they land directly on `/meet`
- Verify: manual — start transcription, navigate to `/room` again, confirm no readiness re-prompt

**UAT-1.8 — a latecomer 2h55 in joins the SAME room (D11)**
- Given: an event room has been running 2h55m with at least one member
- When: a new attendee presses Continue with the switch on
- Then: they are transcribed in the SAME room (not a second one), and their own 3-hour cap starts from their own Continue
- Verify: `e2e/integration/p1307-enter-room-event-access.spec.ts` "a latecomer 181 minutes after room creation joins the SAME still-open room"

## 2. Across pages

**UAT-2.1 — capture survives profile/feed navigation, End works from any page**
- Given: an attendee is being transcribed
- When: they visit their profile, the feed, and back
- Then: capture continues throughout, and the bar's End session works from any of those pages
- Verify: `e2e/p1307-event-transcription.spec.ts` "the bar persists across profile and feed navigation"

**UAT-2.2 — the bar is visible on chromeFree/embed/lobby routes**
- Given: an attendee is being transcribed
- When: they visit `/letter/:docId/preview`, `/cm`, a `?embed=true` URL, and the `/transcribe` lobby
- Then: the bar is visible on all of them (screenshot each)
- Verify: manual screenshots, one per route, attached to the `/verify` run

**UAT-2.3 — opening the room from the bar has no second consent screen or second recording**
- Given: an attendee is being transcribed
- When: they tap Open on the bar
- Then: the room view renders directly (no consent/join screen, no second `startCapture`), and its End ends only this person's capture
- Verify: `e2e/p1307-event-transcription.spec.ts` "opening the room from the bar shows the room view with no second consent screen"

**UAT-2.4 — two tabs, exactly one captures**
- Given: an attendee is being transcribed in one tab
- When: they open a second tab to the same app while signed in as the same user
- Then: the second tab shows the bar (Open/End) but does not capture; exactly one `transcribe_room_members` row exists for that profile in that room
- Verify: `e2e/p1307-event-transcription.spec.ts` "two tabs while transcribing: exactly one captures"; unit: `p1307-web-locks-single-capture.test.ts`

**UAT-2.5 — sign-out stops the mic within 1s**
- Given: an attendee is being transcribed
- When: they sign out
- Then: the microphone turns off within 1 second and no further chunk reaches the bucket
- Verify: manual — sign out while watching the browser's mic indicator + GCS bucket listing; unit: `p1307-capture-state-machine.test.ts` "AUTH_CHANGED"

**UAT-2.6 — joining /live pauses room capture with no message; leaving resumes it**
- Given: an attendee is being transcribed and joins a `/live` session from `/meet`
- Then: room capture pauses silently (no "paused" message); ending the `/live` session brings the bar and capture back; it does not start anything if transcription was off before; leaving `/live` by browser Back only resumes after the session has actually ended
- Verify: manual two-tab-of-behavior walkthrough; unit: `p1307-pause-resume.test.ts`

**UAT-2.7 — explain-back recording pauses room capture, resumes after**
- Given: an attendee is being transcribed and opens an explain-back recording
- Then: room capture pauses for its duration and resumes afterward
- Verify: manual — open an explain-back flow while transcribing, watch the bar; unit: `p1307-pause-resume.test.ts` (routing half); explain-back's own mount/unmount signal is out of this file's pure-function scope — flagged for /dev's own component test if one is added

**UAT-2.8 — two devices, one navigates away and returns: both segments land, transcript has both**
- Given: two participants on two physical devices in the same room
- When: one navigates away mid-room and returns
- Then: the bucket holds both segments (no overwrite) and the saved transcript contains speech from both
- Verify: manual, two real devices — this is explicitly a physical-device AC, not automatable in CI

## 3. End and saved transcript

**UAT-3.1 — two members both press End without opening the room page**
- Given: two members are being transcribed via the bar only (never opened `/transcribe/:code`)
- When: both press End session
- Then: the room ends on the server and both members' sessions show a saved transcript
- Verify: `e2e/integration/p1307-end-capture-rpc.spec.ts` + `p1307-sweep-tick.spec.ts` (room ends once every member is ended) + manual for the saved-transcript half (Part 3)

**UAT-3.2 — nobody presses End, all tabs closed: room ends within N minutes**
- Given: a room with members who all close their tabs without pressing End
- When: N (10) minutes pass
- Then: the room ends and transcripts are saved
- Verify: `e2e/integration/p1307-sweep-tick.spec.ts` "a member whose last_seen_at is 11 minutes stale is ended" + "a room ends once every member has capture_ended_at"

**UAT-3.3 — at the cap, mic releases and bar clears (injected 1-minute cap)**
- Given: a test build with the per-person cap injected as 1 minute
- When: that minute elapses
- Then: the microphone is released, the bar clears, no further archive chunks arrive
- Verify: manual, test-build only — the injected cap is not part of the standard E2E run; unit coverage of the underlying rule: `handler.test.ts` "a member 3h01 past THEIR OWN joined_at is refused"

**UAT-3.4 — after room end, each member's session shows the transcript, attributed and ordered**
- Given: a room has ended and Part 3's whole-recording pass has completed
- When: each member opens their sessions page
- Then: the transcript is attributed by speaker and in spoken order; a non-member cannot read it; a room with no speech shows an empty state
- Verify: manual (needs Part 3 deployed) + `e2e/integration/p1307-jobs-transcripts-rls.spec.ts` (member-scoped SELECT, non-member sees 0 rows)

**UAT-3.5 — a 20-minute two-member room's saved transcript covers its last 5 minutes**
- Given: a 20-minute two-member room
- When: the whole-recording pass completes
- Then: word overlap against the live rows in the last 5-minute bucket is measured and recorded in the spec's Evidence section
- Verify: manual measurement, written into the spec per the AC's own instruction — not an automated test

**UAT-3.6 — two phones, one speaker: the merged timeline shows the sentence once or labelled**
- Given: two members' phones both hear one speaker
- When: the whole-recording pass merges their segments
- Then: the sentence appears once, or duplicated and labelled "also heard by X" per Decision 5's rule
- Verify: manual, real audio — this is the cross-member duplicate-speech AC and needs Part 3's actual merge logic running

**UAT-3.7 — the saved transcript is compared against the room's live text**
- Given: a completed room
- When: the whole-recording pass finishes
- Then: the saved version is compared to the live text of the same room and the comparison is written into the spec (mirroring the D5 Evidence table)
- Verify: manual measurement — not an automated test

## 4. Live text

**UAT-4.1 — a 13s slice returns 200 with text**
- Given: the deployed `transcribe-slice` function with P1307's widened bounds
- When: a 13s + 1s lead-in slice is sent
- Then: it returns 200 with text
- Verify: `supabase/functions/transcribe-slice/handler.test.ts` "a 13s + 1s lead-in slice (448,044 bytes) is accepted"; manual smoke against the deployed function per the Pre-deploy Checklist

**UAT-4.2 — one continuous speaker reads as one message; alternating speakers stay separate**
- Given: a room with live text rendering
- When: one person speaks continuously for 30s, then two people alternate
- Then: the continuous speech renders as one merged message, not one per slice; alternating speakers stay in separate rows
- Verify: `src/tests/p1307-transcript-merge.test.ts`; manual real-speech check

**UAT-4.3 — "…" shows against the current speaker**
- Given: someone is actively speaking
- Then: "…" shows against their name in the room view, and never as a persisted row
- Verify: manual (transient realtime state); `p1307-transcript-merge.test.ts`'s "never a persisted row" documentation test

**UAT-4.4 — /live shows the relabelled switch and banner**
- Given: a user on `/live`
- Then: the start switch reads "Transcribe for AI insights" and the in-session banner reads "Session transcribed for AI insights"
- Verify: `src/tests/live-mode-view.test.tsx` (updated assertions); manual screenshot of `/live`'s start switch (`clarity-live-page.tsx`)

## 5. Security / access (from the Security Review, folded into AC coverage)

**UAT-5.1 — registration-or-host gate on the event room**
- Given: a signed-in user with no RSVP and who is not the host
- When: they attempt to enter the event's transcription room
- Then: they are refused, both server-side (RPC) and in the UI
- Verify: `e2e/integration/p1307-enter-room-event-access.spec.ts`

**UAT-5.2 — no client can end a room for anyone else**
- Given: any authenticated member
- When: they attempt a direct `transcribe_rooms.ended_at` UPDATE, or call `end_transcribe_room_capture` with another member's id (not possible by signature)
- Then: both are refused
- Verify: `e2e/integration/p1307-end-capture-rpc.spec.ts`; `e2e/integration/20260824000000_p1149_room_end_policy_column_guard.spec.ts` (updated)

## 8. Real-device-only scenarios (T1, T3, T4 — flagged in the spec's Open Questions)

**UAT-8.1 (T1) — resume after the phone locks**
- Given: capture is running on a real iPhone (Safari) and a real Android phone (Chrome)
- When: the phone's screen locks and is unlocked again
- Then: record whether capture resumes without a tap, or whether a tap is required (if required, the bar's resume affordance needs founder-approved copy per the spec's own note — D3's "no paused message" was decided for `/live`, not this case)
- Verify: manual, both platforms, recorded in the spec's Evidence section

**UAT-8.2 (T3) — hold vs. release the stream while paused**
- Given: capture is paused (entering `/live`) on a real iPhone
- Then: measure whether holding the `getUserMedia` stream open (Architecture Decision 8's recommendation) causes any observable problem, confirming or flipping the recommendation
- Verify: manual, iPhone, recorded in the spec

**UAT-8.3 (T4) — phone in pocket, screen off, Android, body-size checked**
- Given: capture is running on a real Android phone
- When: the phone is put in a pocket with the screen off
- Then: confirm capture continues (per D8's accepted risk) and record the OS mic indicator's visibility as the only available signal in that state
- Verify: manual, Android device only

**UAT-8.4 — edge-function request body limit (T4 in the spec's Open Questions numbering, distinct from the T4 above which is this file's body-size item — see spec's own T4 wording)**
- Given: a ~600 KB base64-encoded 14s slice
- When: it is sent to the deployed `transcribe-slice` function
- Then: confirm it is accepted (no platform-level body-size 413) — this is the spec's own "measure in /verify" item
- Verify: manual, against the deployed function
