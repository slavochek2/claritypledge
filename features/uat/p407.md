## UAT-1: Author — points auto-expand on detail view

### UAT-1.1: Points section expanded by default
**Given:** Author is logged in and navigates to the detail page of their own story with linked points
**When:** The page finishes loading
**Then:** The linked points section is already expanded — individual point cards are visible
**Then:** No click or tap on a toggle is required
**Verify:** Navigate to `/story/:id` for a story you own with at least 1 linked point — confirm points are visible immediately without tapping the toggle

### UAT-1.2: Expand/collapse toggle reflects expanded state
**Given:** Author is on the story detail page with points auto-expanded
**When:** Author looks at the footer of the story card
**Then:** The toggle shows a ChevronDown icon (expanded state)
**Then:** Author can click the toggle to collapse, then click again to re-expand
**Verify:** Click toggle to collapse — ChevronRight appears. Click again — ChevronDown appears and points are visible again.

## UAT-2: Author — ✕ unlinks point (with undo)

### UAT-2.1: ✕ button present on each QuotedPoint card
**Given:** Author is on the story detail page with at least 1 linked point
**When:** Author hovers over or focuses a QuotedPoint card
**Then:** A ✕ (X) button appears on the card (it is opacity-0 by default, visible on hover/focus)
**Then:** There is exactly one ✕ per linked point
**Verify:** Hover over a QuotedPoint card and confirm the ✕ button appears. Tab to the card with keyboard to verify focus also reveals it.

### UAT-2.2: Clicking ✕ removes point optimistically
**Given:** Author is on the story detail page with a linked point visible
**When:** Author clicks the ✕ button on a QuotedPoint card
**Then:** The point card disappears from the UI immediately (optimistic removal)
**Then:** A "Point unlinked" toast notification appears at the bottom of the screen
**Then:** The toast includes an "Undo" action button
**Verify:** Click ✕ — point vanishes immediately, toast appears within ~1 second

### UAT-2.3: Toast auto-dismisses after 5 seconds
**Given:** Author has just clicked ✕ and the undo toast is visible
**When:** 5 seconds pass without clicking Undo
**Then:** The toast disappears
**Then:** The point remains unlinked (not re-added)
**Verify:** Wait 5s after unlinking — toast gone, point still absent from card

### UAT-2.4: Clicking Undo in toast re-links the point
**Given:** Author has just unlinked a point and the undo toast is visible
**When:** Author clicks the "Undo" button in the toast
**Then:** The point card reappears in the story card's linked points section
**Then:** The toast is dismissed
**Verify:** Unlink a point → immediately click Undo → point reappears in the list

## UAT-3: Author — add form works (text + position required)

### UAT-3.1: Add form visible for author on story with no points
**Given:** Author is on the detail page of their own story with zero linked points
**When:** The page finishes loading
**Then:** An "Add a Point" button or expanded add form is visible below the story card
**Verify:** Navigate to a story you own with no points — confirm the add form or CTA is present

### UAT-3.2: Add Point button disabled with neither text nor position
**Given:** Author has opened the add point form
**When:** The textarea is empty and no position is selected
**Then:** The "Add Point" button is disabled (greyed out)
**Verify:** Open form without typing anything — button is disabled

### UAT-3.3: Add Point button disabled with text but no position
**Given:** Author has typed text into the add point textarea
**When:** No position button (Agree / Disagree / Unsure) has been selected
**Then:** The "Add Point" button remains disabled
**Then:** Hovering over the button shows tooltip: "Pick your position first"
**Verify:** Type a point, hover the button without selecting position — tooltip appears

### UAT-3.4: Add Point button disabled with position but no text
**Given:** Author has selected a position (e.g., Agree)
**When:** The textarea is empty
**Then:** The "Add Point" button remains disabled
**Then:** Hovering shows tooltip: "Write your point first"
**Verify:** Select a position without typing — tooltip on hover

### UAT-3.5: Add Point button enabled when both text and position are filled
**Given:** Author has typed a point statement AND selected a position
**When:** Both fields are non-empty
**Then:** The "Add Point" button becomes active (not disabled)
**Verify:** Type text AND click a position button — "Add Point" button turns blue/active

### UAT-3.6: Submitting add form adds point to the story card
**Given:** Author has filled in text and selected a position
**When:** Author clicks "Add Point"
**Then:** The new point appears in the story card's linked points section
**Then:** The form clears for sequential adds
**Then:** Focus returns to the textarea
**Verify:** Fill form → click "Add Point" → new point card appears, form is empty again
