# UAT: P411 — Position Breakdown: Show Linked Stories

**Feature:** Show each position holder's linked story (or a compact row if no story) on the point detail page.

**Page:** `/point/:id`

---

## UAT-1: Holder WITH a linked story

### UAT-1.1: Story card renders in position breakdown
**Given:** A point has a position holder who has a public story linked to it
**When:** You navigate to `/point/:id`
**Then:** The holder's row shows a full story card:
- Author name (clickable)
- Story text clamped to 3 lines
- Date (relative, e.g. "3 days ago")
- Position badge (Agree/Disagree/Unsure)
- Ear count badge next to the author name
**Verify:** Visually check the position breakdown section

### UAT-1.2: Clicking the story card navigates to the story
**Given:** A story card is visible in the position breakdown
**When:** You tap/click anywhere on the story card
**Then:** You navigate to the story detail page or the author's profile
**Verify:** URL changes away from `/point/:id`

---

## UAT-2: Holder WITHOUT a linked story

### UAT-2.1: Compact row shows with ear count and "No story yet"
**Given:** A point has a position holder who has NOT linked a story to it
**When:** You navigate to `/point/:id`
**Then:** The holder's row shows:
- Small avatar
- Author name (clickable, navigates to their profile)
- Ear count badge (shows 0 if they have none)
- Position badge
- "No story yet" label (italic, subtle)
**Verify:** Visually check; should be clearly less prominent than a full story card

### UAT-2.2: Clicking compact row navigates to the holder's profile
**Given:** A compact row (no story) is visible
**When:** You click anywhere on the row
**Then:** You navigate to the holder's profile page (`/p/:slug`)
**Verify:** URL changes to `/p/...`

---

## UAT-3: Filter tabs

### UAT-3.1: Filtering by position works after the change
**Given:** A point has holders in multiple positions (Agree, Disagree, Unsure)
**When:** You click the "Agree" filter tab
**Then:** Only agree holders (with or without stories) are shown
**Verify:** Disagree/Unsure holders disappear

### UAT-3.2: Clicking active tab deselects it (shows all)
**Given:** The "Agree" tab is active
**When:** You click "Agree" again
**Then:** All positions are shown again
**Verify:** All holders reappear

---

## UAT-4: Edge cases

### UAT-4.1: Private story is not shown (holder appears as compact row)
**Given:** A holder has a private story linked to this point
**When:** You navigate to the point detail page as a different user (or anonymously)
**Then:** The holder appears as a compact row ("No story yet"), not with their private story content
**Verify:** No story text from the private story is visible

### UAT-4.2: Page loads correctly with zero position holders
**Given:** A point has no position holders
**When:** You navigate to `/point/:id`
**Then:** The position breakdown shows "No one has taken a position yet"
**Verify:** No errors, no blank section

### UAT-4.3: Page works for unauthenticated visitors
**Given:** You are not logged in
**When:** You navigate to `/point/:id` via a shared link
**Then:** The full position breakdown renders (positions are public)
**Verify:** Position holders (with/without stories) are visible without login
