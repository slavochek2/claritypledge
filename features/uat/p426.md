## UAT-1: Profile story card — expand/collapse

### UAT-1.1: Long story shows truncated text + "Show more"
**Given:** A profile with a story longer than 3 lines (>180 chars)
**When:** Any visitor navigates to the profile page (`/p/{slug}`)
**Then:** Story text is truncated after 3 lines with an ellipsis; "Show more" button appears in blue below
**Verify:** `mcp__claude-in-chrome__read_page` — confirm "Show more" visible; full story text not shown

### UAT-1.2: Clicking "Show more" expands to full story
**Given:** A long story card showing "Show more"
**When:** User clicks "Show more"
**Then:** Full story text becomes visible; button label changes to "Show less" at the same position
**Verify:** `mcp__claude-in-chrome__click` on "Show more" → confirm full text visible + button now reads "Show less"

### UAT-1.3: Clicking "Show less" collapses back
**Given:** A story card expanded (showing "Show less")
**When:** User clicks "Show less"
**Then:** Story collapses back to 3-line truncated view; button returns to "Show more"
**Verify:** `mcp__claude-in-chrome__click` on "Show less" → confirm truncation restored

### UAT-1.4: Short story has no toggle
**Given:** A profile with a story ≤ 3 lines (~180 chars)
**When:** Visitor views the profile
**Then:** Story displays in full with no "Show more" button
**Verify:** `mcp__claude-in-chrome__read_page` — confirm story text visible, no "Show more" present

## UAT-2: Live story card — expand/collapse

### UAT-2.1: Long story in /live shows "Show more"
**Given:** Creator has selected a long story (>180 chars) in an active /live session
**When:** The story card (`LiveStoryCardExpanded`) renders
**Then:** Story is truncated to 3 lines with "Show more" below (was: hard-cut at 120 chars, no expand)
**Verify:** Open /live, start session, pick a long story from content picker → confirm "Show more" appears

### UAT-2.2: "Show more" in /live expands story inline
**Given:** Long story in /live showing "Show more"
**When:** Creator clicks "Show more"
**Then:** Full story text visible; "Show less" replaces "Show more"; page doesn't navigate away
**Verify:** Click "Show more" → full text visible, URL unchanged, no navigation

### UAT-2.3: Points footer unaffected in /live
**Given:** A long story card in /live with the "Show more" toggle visible
**When:** The session is active
**Then:** Points footer ("> N points by [name]") remains below the toggle, unaffected
**Verify:** Points count and creator name visible below "Show more" / "Show less"

## UAT-3: Edge cases

### UAT-3.1: Each card expands independently
**Given:** Profile page with multiple long stories
**When:** User expands one story
**Then:** Only that card expands; other cards remain collapsed
**Verify:** Click "Show more" on card 1 → card 2 still shows "Show more"

### UAT-3.2: "Open story" button still works
**Given:** Profile story card (collapsed or expanded)
**When:** User clicks the `[↗]` (Open story) button
**Then:** Navigates to the story detail page (no regression)
**Verify:** Click `[↗]` → arrives at `/story/{id}`
