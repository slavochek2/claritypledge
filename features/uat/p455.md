## UAT-1: Idle screen — story owner

### UAT-1.1: Story card is at top
**Given:** Story selected in live session (owner view, first round — no history)
**When:** Page loads
**Then:** Story card is visible near top of screen, WITHOUT scrolling
**Verify:** Avatar + name visible, story text shows max 2 lines

### UAT-1.2: Check CTA is visible without scrolling
**Given:** Same as 1.1
**When:** Page loads
**Then:** "Does [partner] understand you?" button is fully visible within viewport
**Verify:** No need to scroll to see the button

### UAT-1.3: Speak freely is immediately below Check
**Given:** Same as 1.1
**When:** Page loads
**Then:** "Speak freely" text link appears directly below the Check button with no large gap
**Verify:** Both Check and Speak freely visible on screen simultaneously

### UAT-1.4: Journey card is below CTAs
**Given:** Idle screen after at least one completed round (history exists)
**When:** Page loads
**Then:** Journey card (confidence/belief dots) appears BELOW the Check button, not above
**Verify:** Scroll down to find journey card if needed — it should not be at top

---

## UAT-2: Story card compact/expand

### UAT-2.1: Story text truncates to 2 lines
**Given:** Story with long text selected
**When:** Card first appears
**Then:** Story body shows exactly 2 lines, rest is hidden
**Verify:** "Show more" link is visible

### UAT-2.2: Show more expands full text
**Given:** Story card collapsed (2 lines shown)
**When:** Tap "Show more"
**Then:** Full story text visible, "Show less" replaces "Show more"
**Verify:** No layout jump, Check button still visible after expand (may need scroll)

### UAT-2.3: Show less collapses back
**Given:** Story card expanded
**When:** Tap "Show less"
**Then:** Returns to 2-line truncated view
**Verify:** "Show more" reappears
