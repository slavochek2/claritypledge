# UAT: P459 — Agreements to Connections Page

Manual validation checklist. Run on staging/prod after P459 implementation ships.

**Test account setup:** You need three accounts:
- **Owner** — has agreements (at least one public active, one private)
- **Visitor-Party** — is the partner in at least one agreement with Owner
- **Stranger** — has no agreements with Owner (no public visible to them)

---
