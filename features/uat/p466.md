# UAT: P466 — Agreement Creation HelloSign Redesign

Manual checklist. Run after implementation ships to staging/feature branch.

**Feature:** Redesigns `create-agreement-page.tsx` so the certificate is the form. Adds `partner_display_name` field — editable in the PARTNER signature slot at creation time and at acceptance time.

**Test accounts needed:**
- Account A (Creator): a verified account with a name in their profile.
- Account B (Partner): a verified account that will receive the invitation.
- Account C (New user): does not yet have a ClarityPledge account (use a fresh email).
- Account X (Visitor/non-party): a third verified account.
- Fresh browser session (no cached session) for anonymous/unauthenticated tests.
