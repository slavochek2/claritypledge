### UAT-1: Transcript appears after processing completes

**When** the transcription pipeline completes (status = completed)
**And** the participant navigates to /sessions and opens the session detail
**Then** a "Transcript" row appears below the round list
**And** the row shows Copy and Open buttons

**Verification:** Open /sessions, tap session card, confirm transcript row is visible with both buttons.
