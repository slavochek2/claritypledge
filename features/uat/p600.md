# P600 UAT: Free Mode Polish — Fix 5 (Speaker Re-rating)

## UAT-5.1: Speaker sees re-rating drawer after listener paraphrases
**Given:** Free mode session, guided first round completed (both rated), listener clicked "Explain back"
**When:** Listener clicks "Done explaining"
**Then:** Speaker sees re-rating drawer ("How well do you believe [partner] understands your intention?")
**Verify:** Two browser windows on localhost:5100, speaker window shows rating drawer

## UAT-5.2: Speaker re-rating stores as second Journey row
**Given:** Speaker sees re-rating drawer after paraphrase (UAT-5.1)
**When:** Speaker submits re-rating (e.g., 7/10, not a 10)
**Then:** Free mode sliders unlock; Journey shows 2 committed rows (row 0: sealed-bid, row 1: re-rating) + live blue dots row
**Verify:** Visual inspection of Journey section — 3 rows total (2 black committed + 1 blue live)

## UAT-5.3: Speaker re-rating of 10 triggers celebration
**Given:** Speaker sees re-rating drawer after paraphrase
**When:** Speaker submits 10/10
**Then:** Celebration screen appears (same as guided mode: sparkle + "understood perfectly!")
**Verify:** Both windows show celebration, not free mode sliders

## UAT-5.4: Listener sees waiting state during speaker re-rating
**Given:** Listener clicked "Done explaining"
**When:** Speaker is rating
**Then:** Listener sees waiting indicator (e.g., "Waiting for [speaker]...")
**Verify:** Listener window shows waiting state, not sliders
