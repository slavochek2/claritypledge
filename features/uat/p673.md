# P673 UAT: Letter Reading Flow Reuses /live Components

## UAT-1: Story card visual parity with /live

### UAT-1.1: Story card matches /live appearance
**Given:** A sealed letter with stories
**When:** Receiver opens the letter and reaches a story
**Then:** Story card looks identical to /live's `LiveStoryCardExpanded` — blue left border, author avatar + name, story text
**Verify:** Side-by-side screenshot comparison with /live story card

### UAT-1.2: Points section hidden on story card
**Given:** A story with 3 points
**When:** Story card renders in letter reading
**Then:** No "N points" toggle visible, no point rows visible inside the card
**Verify:** Visual inspection — compare to /live where points ARE visible

## UAT-2: Rating Drawer experience

### UAT-2.1: Drawer slides up over story card
**Given:** Receiver is at story-rate phase
**When:** Phase renders
**Then:** Bottom drawer slides up with rating question, story card stays visible above
**Verify:** Screenshot — drawer overlays bottom, story card visible at top

### UAT-2.2: No "I've read this" step
**Given:** Receiver reaches a story
**When:** Story card appears
**Then:** Rating drawer appears immediately — no intermediate gate button
**Verify:** Visual inspection — no button between story card and rating

## UAT-3: Gap reveal experience

### UAT-3.1: JourneyToUnderstanding sealed-bid reveal
**Given:** Receiver submits their rating (e.g., 7)
**When:** Rating is submitted
**Then:** JourneyToUnderstanding card appears above story showing both numbers (receiver: 7, sender's prediction revealed)
**Verify:** Screenshot — both numbers visible, matches /live's JourneyToUnderstanding appearance

### UAT-3.2: Gap banner insight message
**Given:** Receiver rated 5, sender predicted 8 (3-point gap)
**When:** Gap reveal phase
**Then:** Blue banner shows "3 points gap" badge + "{SenderName} thinks you understand more than you think"
**Verify:** Screenshot — banner styling matches /live's gap-revealed phase

## UAT-4: Point engagement experience

### UAT-4.1: Point card with position buttons
**Given:** Story has 2+ visible points (anti-point lead)
**When:** First phase of story reading
**Then:** Point card shows point text + agree/disagree/unsure buttons + Submit
**Verify:** Visual inspection — buttons match /live's PositionButtons styling

### UAT-4.2: Sender position reveal after receiver positions
**Given:** Receiver clicked "agree" and submitted
**When:** Position is submitted
**Then:** Sender's position appears (PositionBadge) + gap/match notification
**Verify:** Screenshot — position badge styling matches /live

## UAT-5: Flow ordering

### UAT-5.1: Anti-point lead (2+ visible points)
**Given:** Story with 3 visible points
**When:** Receiver starts reading this story
**Then:** Flow is: Point 1 → Story → Rate → Reveal → Point 2 → Point 3 → Transition
**Verify:** Walk through full flow, verify ordering

### UAT-5.2: Story-first (1 visible point, D36)
**Given:** Story with 1 visible point
**When:** Receiver starts reading this story
**Then:** Flow is: Story → Rate → Reveal → Point 1 → Transition
**Verify:** Walk through flow

### UAT-5.3: No points (0 visible)
**Given:** Story with 0 visible points (all hidden)
**When:** Receiver starts reading this story
**Then:** Flow is: Story → Rate → Reveal → Transition (no point phases)
**Verify:** Walk through flow

## UAT-6: Preview fidelity

### UAT-6.1: Preview matches reading identically
**Given:** Sender opens preview of their letter
**When:** Preview renders
**Then:** Same story card, same Drawer, same JourneyToUnderstanding, same point cards as reading
**Verify:** Side-by-side screenshot of preview vs reading for same letter

## UAT-7: Chrome-free

### UAT-7.1: No navigation on reading/preview
**Given:** Receiver opens a letter
**When:** Reading page renders
**Then:** No top nav (Home, Letters, Events, My Profile), no bottom nav
**Verify:** Screenshot — only FocusHeader/preview banner + letter content visible
