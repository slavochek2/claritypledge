# UAT Scenarios — P682: Private Letter Multi-Recipient & Simplified Flow

**Format:** Given / When / Then
**Tester:** Founder
**Branch:** feature/letters-ship
**Device coverage required:** Desktop + mobile (375px width)

---

## UAT-1: Private doc skips mode selector

**Given** I am signed in and own a private doc with at least one story
**When** I click "Send letter" on the compose page
**Then** the receiver modal opens directly showing recipient input fields
**And** I do NOT see "Specific people" or "Anyone with a link" option cards
**And** I see the title "Who is your letter for?"

---

## UAT-2: Public doc mode selector unchanged

**Given** I am signed in and own a public doc with at least one story
**When** I click "Send letter" on the compose page
**Then** the receiver modal opens showing the mode selector
**And** I see both "Specific people" and "Anyone with a link" option cards
**And** the flow proceeds exactly as before P682

---

## UAT-3: Single recipient card — no X button

**Given** the receiver modal is open for a private doc
**When** only one recipient card is shown
**Then** there is no X (remove) button on that card
**And** I see the "+ Add another person" link below the card
**And** I see the "Continue" button

---

## UAT-4: Add second recipient

**Given** the receiver modal is open for a private doc with one empty card
**When** I click "+ Add another person"
**Then** a second recipient card appears below the first
**And** the email field of the new card receives focus
**And** both cards now show an X button in the top-right corner
**And** the "+ Add another person" link is still visible

---

## UAT-5: Remove second recipient

**Given** there are 2 recipient cards in the modal
**When** I click the X button on the second card
**Then** the second card disappears
**And** only one card remains
**And** that remaining card has no X button

---

## UAT-6: Footer text

**Given** the receiver modal is open for a private doc
**When** I look below the Continue button
**Then** I see the text: "Each person receives their own personal invitation."

---

## UAT-7: Duplicate email validation

**Given** I have 2 recipient cards
**When** I enter the same email address in both cards
**And** I click "Continue"
**Then** the second card shows an inline error: "Already added"
**And** no toast is shown
**And** I remain on the same form

---

## UAT-8: Self-send validation

**Given** I am on the recipient form
**When** I type my own email address into any recipient card
**Then** that card shows an inline error: "You can't send a letter to yourself"
**And** this error appears before I click Continue (on email input, after debounce)

---

## UAT-9: Soft cap at 20

**Given** the receiver modal is open
**When** I have added 20 recipient cards
**Then** the "+ Add another person" link disappears
**And** no error message is shown
**And** I can still edit and remove existing cards

---

## UAT-10: Email lookup auto-fills name

**Given** the receiver modal is open
**When** I type the email of a registered ClarityPledge user into any recipient row
**Then** after ~400ms the name field auto-fills with their registered name
**And** the name field becomes read-only
**And** I see hint text: "Using their registered name"

---

## UAT-11: No account — invited to join

**Given** the receiver modal is open
**When** I type an email that has no ClarityPledge account
**Then** I see hint text: "No account — they'll be invited to join"
**And** the name field remains editable so I can type their name manually

---

## UAT-12: Desktop layout — fields side-by-side

**Given** I am on a desktop viewport (>=640px wide)
**When** the receiver modal is open with at least one recipient card
**Then** the Email and Full name fields appear side-by-side within each card
**And** each field takes roughly equal horizontal space

---

## UAT-13: Mobile layout — fields stacked

**Given** I am on a mobile viewport (<640px wide)
**When** the receiver modal is open with at least one recipient card
**Then** the Email field appears above the Full name field (stacked) within each card
**And** each field spans the full card width

---

## UAT-14: Empty rows cleaned silently on Continue

**Given** I have added a second recipient card but left it completely empty (email + name blank)
**When** I fill in the first card with a valid email and name
**And** I click Continue
**Then** the empty second row is discarded silently
**And** I proceed to the prediction walk with only 1 recipient

---

## UAT-15: 3 valid recipients all receive emails

**Given** I fill in 3 recipient cards with valid emails and names
**When** I complete the prediction walk and seal the letter
**Then** all 3 recipients receive individual invitation emails
(Verify: check 3 test email addresses or check `letter_deliveries` table for 3 rows)

---

## UAT-16: P664 add-recipient mode unaffected

**Given** I have a sealed letter
**When** I click "Add recipient" from the sealed letter view
**Then** the add-recipient modal opens with its existing UI (not the multi-recipient form)
**And** the flow works exactly as before P682

---

## UAT-17: Prediction walk — single recipient personalized

**Given** I fill in exactly 1 recipient with name "Jordan Patel"
**When** I proceed to the prediction walk
**Then** the prompt references "Jordan Patel" by name (personalized)

---

## UAT-18: Prediction walk — multiple recipients use "readers"

**Given** I fill in 2+ recipients
**When** I proceed to the prediction walk
**Then** the prompt uses the generic word "readers" (not a specific name)

---

## UAT-19: Modal scrolls with many recipients

**Given** I have added 5+ recipient cards
**When** the modal content exceeds the viewport height
**Then** the modal content scrolls internally
**And** the Continue button remains accessible at the bottom
**And** the modal header remains visible at the top
**And** the modal does not overflow outside the viewport

---

## Pass criteria

All 19 UAT scenarios must pass on desktop. UAT-12 and UAT-13 require verification on both desktop and mobile viewports. UAT-15 requires either sending to real test addresses or checking the database.
