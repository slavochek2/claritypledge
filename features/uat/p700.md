# UAT: P700 — Letter Overview (per-letter author view)

**Spec:** [p700_letter_results_aggregate_overview.md](../p700_letter_results_aggregate_overview.md)
**Status:** ready-for-uat

---

## UAT-1: Navigation to Overview Page

### UAT-1.1 — Desktop: [Open overview] CTA in sent-tab card

**Given** I am the author of a sealed letter with at least one recipient
**And** I am on the Sent tab (`/letters?tab=sent`)
**When** I see the letter card
**Then** a solid blue `Open overview` button is visible in the card's action area, to the right of any other action buttons and directly before the `[···]` dropdown

**When** I click `Open overview`
**Then** the browser navigates to `/letter/:id/overview`

---

### UAT-1.2 — Mobile: [Open overview] in ··· dropdown

**Given** I am on the Sent tab on a mobile viewport (≤ 767px)
**When** I tap the `[···]` dropdown on a letter card
**Then** `Open overview` appears as the first item in the dropdown

**When** I tap `Open overview`
**Then** the browser navigates to `/letter/:id/overview`

---

### UAT-1.3 — Sent-tab cards default collapsed

**Given** I navigate to the Sent tab for the first time in this session
**Then** all letter cards are collapsed — no recipient rows are visible
**And** I must expand a card manually to see recipient details

**When** I reload the page
**Then** all cards return to collapsed state (no persistence between reloads)

---

## UAT-2: Overview Page — Landing State

### UAT-2.1 — Landing state renders View 1

**Given** I navigate to `/letter/:id/overview` for a letter with 2 stories and 3 recipients
**Then** the Story selector shows the first story's title (not "— none —")
**And** the Person selector shows "— none —"
**And** the cohort list renders one row per recipient (3 rows)
**And** the first story's title appears as a subtitle above the list

---

### UAT-2.2 — Back link present

**Given** I am on the overview page
**Then** I see a `← Sent` or back link in the top-left corner
**When** I click it
**Then** I am returned to the Sent tab

---

## UAT-3: View 1 — Cohort on One Story

### UAT-3.1 — Verified recipient row

**Given** View 1 is active (Story selected, Person = none)
**And** recipient Alice has completed the letter and has a `source='letter'` story verification
**Then** Alice's row shows:
- Her display name
- An understanding value (single integer, e.g. `8`)
- A CLAIM position value (signed, e.g. `+2 agree`)
- An ANTI position value (`◌ no position` if no ANTI point set or no response)
- Status `★ Verified`
- An `[open]` drill-in link

---

### UAT-3.2 — Recipient with /live movement row

**Given** recipient Bob has a `source='letter'` AND a `source='live'` story verification for the selected story
**Then** Bob's row shows:
- Understanding as `X → Y` (letter value → live value, e.g. `4 → 7`)
- Status `→ Moved`

---

### UAT-3.3 — Waiting recipient row

**Given** recipient Carol has not completed the letter
**Then** Carol's row shows:
- Status `· Waiting`
- No understanding value (shows `—`)
- No position values for CLAIM or ANTI (shows `—`)
- No `[open]` drill-in link (or link is inactive)

---

### UAT-3.4 — No position shown correctly

**Given** a recipient has completed the letter
**And** the story's ANTI point had no response from that recipient
**Then** the ANTI column shows `◌ no position` in muted text
**And** NOT a signed value or zero

---

### UAT-3.5 — Drill-in link URL

**Given** I click `[open]` on Alice's row in View 1 (Story 1 selected)
**Then** the browser navigates to `/letter/:id/results?delivery=<alice_delivery_id>&story=<story1_id>`

---

### UAT-3.6 — Story selector changes list

**Given** View 1 is active showing Story 1 results
**When** I open the Story selector and choose Story 2
**Then** the cohort list updates immediately (no page reload)
**And** Story 2's title appears as the list subtitle
**And** the recipient rows now show understanding and position values for Story 2

---

### UAT-3.7 — Story selector has clear option

**Given** I open the Story selector
**Then** the first item is `— none —`
**When** I select `— none —`
**Then** the Story selector shows `— none —`
**And** the cohort list clears (View 1 is no longer shown)

---

## UAT-4: View 2 — Person Across All Stories

### UAT-4.1 — Switch to View 2

**Given** I am on the overview page
**When** I open the Person selector and choose `Bob`
**And** I open the Story selector and choose `— none —`
**Then** View 2 renders:
- A header line showing Bob's name and completion status (e.g. `Bob · Letter only`)
- One section per story in the letter
- Each section shows the story title, understanding line, and CLAIM/ANTI lines
- A footer link `[Open Bob's full results →]` at the bottom

---

### UAT-4.2 — View 2 understanding format

**Given** Bob has a `source='letter'` verification (letter_rating = 4) for Story 1
**And** Bob has a `source='live'` verification (live_rating = 7) for Story 1
**Then** Story 1's understanding line shows `4/10 → 7/10`

**Given** Bob has only a `source='letter'` verification (letter_rating = 6) for Story 2
**Then** Story 2's understanding line shows `6/10` (no arrow)

---

### UAT-4.3 — View 2 waiting story

**Given** a story has no response from the selected person
**Then** that story's section shows `Waiting for first completion` in muted text on the understanding line
**And** no CLAIM or ANTI lines appear for that story

---

### UAT-4.4 — View 2 footer link URL

**Given** View 2 is showing Bob's journey
**When** I click `[Open Bob's full results →]`
**Then** the browser navigates to `/letter/:id/results?delivery=<bob_delivery_id>`
**And** the URL does NOT include a `story=` param

---

### UAT-4.5 — No story sections omitted

**Given** the letter has 3 stories
**Then** View 2 shows all 3 story sections for the selected person
**And** stories are ordered by author authoring position (0, 1, 2)

---

## UAT-5: View 3 — Zoom Card (Both Selectors Set)

### UAT-5.1 — Zoom card structure

**Given** I select Story 1 in the Story selector
**And** I select Bob in the Person selector
**Then** a zoom card renders with:
- Title: `Bob on "P700 Story 1: The AI Threat Narrative"` (or equivalent)
- A meta line: Bob's name + completion status (`Letter only` / `Verified` / `Waiting`)
- An Understanding line showing `4/10 → 7/10` (if live data present) or single value
- A CLAIM line: point text + signed value
- An ANTI line: point text + `◌ no position` if no response
- A footer link `[Open Bob's full results →]`

---

### UAT-5.2 — View 3 zoom card drill-in URL

**Given** View 3 is showing Bob on Story 1
**When** I click `[Open Bob's full results →]`
**Then** the browser navigates to `/letter/:id/results?delivery=<bob_delivery_id>&story=<story1_id>`

---

### UAT-5.3 — View 3: Waiting state

**Given** the selected person has not completed the letter on the selected story
**Then** the zoom card shows `Waiting for first completion` instead of Understanding / CLAIM / ANTI values

---

## UAT-7: Edge and Empty States

### UAT-7.1 — All recipients waiting

**Given** none of the letter's recipients have completed the letter
**Then** View 1 shows all rows with `· Waiting` status
**And** no CLAIM, ANTI, or understanding values appear in the rows
**And** the page renders without crashing

---

### UAT-7.2 — No means or averages

**Given** I view the overview page in any of the three views
**Then** no mean, average, or aggregated value appears anywhere on the page
**And** each row shows raw per-recipient values only

---

### UAT-7.3 — No grid, chart, or dot-plot

**Given** I view any of the three views
**Then** no chart, grid, SVG visualization, dot-plot, or axis is rendered
**And** all data is presented as text lists

---

### UAT-7.4 — Error state with retry

**Given** the `get_letter_overview` RPC fails (network error or 500)
**Then** the list area shows: `Could not load results.`
**And** a `[Retry]` button is visible
**When** I click `[Retry]`
**Then** the RPC is re-fired without a full page reload
**And** the selector state is preserved

---

### UAT-7.5 — Anonymous recipient

**Given** a delivery has no `receiver_name` and no `receiver_email`
**Then** the recipient appears as `(Anonymous)` in all views
**And** the fallback does not expose any email address

**And** no letter data is visible
