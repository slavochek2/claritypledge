# P703 UAT: Letter-sourced /live Verification

Manual validation scenarios. Run after implementation before `/ship`.

**Prerequisites:**
- Two test accounts (facilitator + listener) both logged in
- P699 story walk visible with results for at least one story
- P703 migration applied (`./scripts/migrate.sh`)

---

## Scenario 1 — Happy path (co-present workshop)

**Goal:** Facilitator starts a session from story walk; listener joins co-presently; /live starts at explain-back.

**Steps:**
1. Log in as facilitator. Open P699 story walk → select a story with a visible gap.
2. Tap **"Start a clarity session"** button below the story card.
3. Verify: browser navigates to `/live/<code>` as facilitator (speaker role).
4. Verify: waiting screen shows **"Invite sent to {listener name}"** — NOT the share link.
5. Log in as listener on a second device (or browser). Open inbox → Letters → Inbox tab.
6. Verify: inbox row reads **"{author name} invited you to verify {story title} — Join"**.
7. Tap **Join**. Verify: listener lands on `/live/<code>` as listener.
8. Verify: first screen on BOTH devices is the **Explain back / paraphrase prompt** — NOT a prediction slider.
9. Complete paraphrase round. Verify: both reach free-mode sliders.
10. End session. Verify: invite disappears from listener's inbox.

**Pass criteria:** All 10 steps verified with no errors.

---

## Scenario 2 — Happy path (remote listener)

**Goal:** Same as Scenario 1 but listener is on a different network.

**Steps:** Repeat Scenario 1 with listener on a different device/connection.

**Pass criteria:** Invite arrives via realtime within 5 seconds of facilitator tapping the button.

---

## Scenario 3 — Cancel path (facilitator closes before listener joins)

**Goal:** Cancelling the room leaves no orphaned verification data and removes the invite.

**Steps:**
1. Facilitator taps "Start a clarity session". Lands on /live waiting screen.
2. Listener's inbox shows the invite (do NOT tap Join).
3. Facilitator taps the "End" / "Leave" button from the waiting screen.
4. Verify: listener's inbox invite disappears (without page refresh).
5. Verify: no `story_verifications` row with `source='live'` was created.

**Pass criteria:** Invite gone within 10s of facilitator ending. No stale data.

---

## Scenario 4 — Logged-out listener

**Goal:** A logged-out listener who taps Join is redirected through login and returns to /live.

**Steps:**
1. Facilitator creates a session (Scenario 1 steps 1–3).
2. Copy the `/live/<code>` URL.
3. Open a new incognito window (not logged in). Navigate to the `/live/<code>` URL.
4. Verify: redirected to `/login` (or `/signup`).
5. Log in as listener.
6. Verify: redirected back to `/live/<code>` after login.
7. Verify: listener lands at the explain-back step.

**Pass criteria:** No data visible before login. Seamless return after auth.

---

## Scenario 5 — Role enforcement (button visible to author only)

**Goal:** "Start a clarity session" button is invisible to the letter receiver.

**Steps:**
1. Log in as listener (letter receiver). Open the same P699 story walk (as a receiver).
2. Verify: **no "Start a clarity session" button visible** on any story card.
3. Log in as a third party (unrelated user). Attempt to navigate to the story walk.
4. Verify: no button (or page gated by author-only access).

**Pass criteria:** Button absent for non-authors.

---

## Scenario 6 — Singleton overlap attempt

**Goal:** Author cannot send a second invite to the same listener while one is pending.

**Steps:**
1. Facilitator creates a session → invite is open for listener.
2. Facilitator navigates BACK to P699 story walk (without listener joining).
3. Tap "Start a clarity session" again for the SAME listener.
4. Verify: button is **disabled** with tooltip **"Invite already pending for {listener}"**.
5. Verify: no second invite row appears in DB.

**Pass criteria:** Button disabled. DB has exactly one open invite for listener.
