## Scenario Details

### UAT-1: Inbox registered sender name links to profile

**Given** I am logged in as a receiver who has received a letter from a registered user with a slug
**When** I open /letters (Inbox tab)
**Then** the sender's name appears as a clickable link
**And** clicking the name navigates to `/p/<sender-slug>`
**And** the browser URL changes to the sender's profile page

---

### UAT-2: Inbox anonymous sender shows "Someone" plain text

**Given** I am logged in as a sender whose letter was responded to via a public link (no profile)
**When** I open /letters and view the Inbox responses
**Then** the actor name renders as "Someone"
**And** "Someone" is plain text — no hyperlink, no /p/ URL

---

### UAT-3: Sent tab known recipient name links to profile

**Given** I am logged in as a sender who sent a letter to a registered user with a slug
**When** I open /letters and switch to the Sent tab
**Then** the recipient's name appears as a clickable link
**And** clicking the name navigates to `/p/<recipient-slug>`

---

### UAT-4: Sent tab public link letter shows placeholder

**Given** I am logged in as a sender who created a public link (one-to-many) letter
**When** I open /letters and switch to the Sent tab
**Then** the letter row shows "Public link letter" as the recipient placeholder
**And** no broken link or empty field is visible

---

### UAT-5: Letter-reading page sender identity visible and linked

**Given** I am logged in as a receiver reading a letter
**When** I open the letter reading page (`/letter/<deliveryId>`)
**Then** the sender's name appears prominently near the top of the reading surface
**And** the name is linked to the sender's profile `/p/<sender-slug>`
**And** the avatar is visible alongside the name

---

### UAT-6: Results page other-participant identity row visible

**Given** I have completed reading a letter and reached the results page
**When** I view `/letter/<letterId>/results`
**Then** an identity row showing the other participant's name and avatar renders above the story walk
**And** the row is visible regardless of whether the other participant has taken positions on points

---

### UAT-7: Results role label — recipient sees "Letter from [Name]"

**Given** I am logged in as the receiver of a letter
**When** I view the results page
**Then** the identity row shows the label "Letter from [sender name]"
**And** the sender name is linked to their profile

---

### UAT-8: Results role label — author sees "Letter to [Name]"

**Given** I am logged in as the sender of a letter
**When** I view the results page
**Then** the identity row shows the label "Letter to [receiver name]"
**And** the receiver name is linked to their profile

---

### UAT-9: Results page point avatars link to /p/:slug

**Given** I am on the results page and the story has points with the other participant's position
**When** I see the other participant's avatar above a point
**Then** clicking the avatar navigates to the participant's profile page

---

### UAT-10: Navigation tab order — Inbox first

**Given** I navigate to /letters
**When** the page loads
**Then** the Inbox tab is displayed first (leftmost)
**And** Sent is second
**And** Drafts is third
**And** Inbox is the active/selected tab by default

---

### UAT-11–13: New Draft CTA visible on all three tabs

**Given** I am on /letters
**When** I view the Inbox tab
**Then** the "New Draft" button is visible
**When** I switch to the Sent tab
**Then** the "New Draft" button is still visible
**When** I switch to the Drafts tab
**Then** the "New Draft" button is still visible
**And** clicking "New Draft" from any tab opens the same letter creation flow

---

### UAT-14: Name with null slug renders as plain text

**Given** a sender whose profile has `slug = NULL` (legacy account)
**When** their letter appears in my inbox
**Then** their name renders as plain text
**And** no hyperlink is attached to the name
**And** no broken `/p/null` or `/p/undefined` URL appears

---

### UAT-15–16: Long name truncation

**Given** a participant with a name longer than 24 characters (mobile) or 40 characters (desktop)
**When** their name appears in any identity row (inbox, sent, reading page, results)
**Then** on a 375px viewport: the name truncates at approximately 24 characters with an ellipsis
**And** on a 1280px viewport: the name truncates at approximately 40 characters with an ellipsis
**And** hovering the name (desktop) shows the full name via browser title or tooltip

---

### UAT-17: stopPropagation — inbox name vs card body (REGRESSION RISK)

**Given** I am on the Inbox tab and see a letter card with a sender name link
**When** I click the sender name link
**Then** I navigate to the sender's profile page `/p/<slug>`
**And** I do NOT navigate to the letter reading page

**When** I return to the Inbox and click the card body (not the name)
**Then** I navigate to the letter reading page
**And** I do NOT navigate to the sender's profile

> Note: This stopPropagation behavior is easy to miss visually. Test with real taps/clicks, not keyboard navigation.

---

### UAT-18: stopPropagation — sent name vs card body (REGRESSION RISK)

**Given** I am on the Sent tab and see a sent letter card with a recipient name link
**When** I click the recipient name link
**Then** I navigate to `/p/<recipient-slug>`
**And** the letter does NOT open

**When** I click the card body (not the name)
**Then** the letter opens or expands
**And** I do NOT navigate to the recipient's profile

---

### UAT-19: stopPropagation — results identity link

**Given** I am on the results page and the identity row shows the other participant
**When** I click the participant's name link
**Then** I navigate to their profile page
**And** the results page does not interfere with navigation

---

### UAT-20: Dark mode visual pass

**Given** my device or browser is in dark mode
**When** I open /letters (Inbox, Sent, Drafts), the reading page, and the results page
**Then** all identity rows render without contrast issues
**And** name links are visually distinguishable from plain text
**And** avatars and role labels are legible

---

### UAT-21: Mobile 375px visual pass

**Given** I am viewing the app on a 375px wide viewport (iPhone SE)
**When** I open /letters, a reading page, and a results page
**Then** identity rows do not overflow their containers
**And** name links and avatars are fully visible
**And** the "New Draft" CTA is reachable without horizontal scrolling
**And** tab labels (Inbox/Sent/Drafts) are readable without truncation
