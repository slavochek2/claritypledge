# P967 UAT: Listening Calibration Breakdown

Manual acceptance scenarios. Run against a local dev build with the migration applied (`supabase db reset`).

---

## Setup

You need a Supabase test account with at least 5 eligible `story_verifications` rows where `listener_id = your user id` AND both `speaker_rating` and `listener_rating` are non-null. Use the Supabase SQL editor to seed rows, or use the helper `createTestUser()` + direct inserts.

---

## Scenario 1 — Empty state (0 eligible rows)

**Given** I am logged in as a user with zero listener verification rows (never completed a /live session as listener)

**When** I navigate to `/me/calibration`

**Then**
- I see the text: "Finish your first listening session to start seeing your calibration diffs."
- I see a "Start a session" CTA button/link
- I do NOT see any table rows
- I do NOT see any verdict label ("overconfident", "calibrated", etc.)
- I do NOT see the footer sum/avg row

---

## Scenario 2 — Pre-unlock state (3 eligible rows, fewer than 5)

**Given** I am logged in as a user with exactly 3 eligible listener verification rows

**When** I navigate to `/me/calibration`

**Then**
- I see exactly 3 rows in the diff table
- Each row shows: partner name, date, col1 value (0–10), col2 value (0–10), signed col3 (col2 − col1)
- I see the progress string: "3 of 5 — your score unlocks after 2 more"
- I do NOT see a verdict label (hidden until 5 rows)
- I do NOT see the empty-state copy

---

## Scenario 3 — Unlocked state (≥5 eligible rows)

**Given** I am logged in as a user with 5+ eligible listener verification rows

**When** I navigate to `/me/calibration`

**Then**
- I see all my rows in the diff table, one per verification record
- Column headers read: "you believed you understood their intended meaning" / "they believe you understood them after you explained back" / "gap"
- Each row shows numeric signed col3 only — no per-row word labels ("overconfident" does not appear in any row cell)
- The footer row shows: `sum {S} ÷ {N} diffs = {avg}` where N is the total row count (not meeting count)
- The "Practice in a session" button/link is visible (blue primary CTA, → /live)
- The "Learn more about the Co-Founder Program →" link is visible (text link secondary CTA, → /program)
- The verdict label ("Somewhat overconfident" or similar) is visible at the top bar

---

## Scenario 4 — Faithfulness eye-check (sum reconstructs bar value)

**Given** I am on the unlocked `/me/calibration` page

**When** I manually sum the col3 column in the displayed rows

**Then**
- My manual sum equals the "S" value shown in the footer: `sum {S} ÷ {N} diffs = {avg}`
- The footer average equals the "avg" in the footer string
- The sign of the footer average MATCHES the bar direction:
  - If the bar shows "overconfident" (you rated yourself higher than partner rated you), the footer avg is NEGATIVE
  - If the bar shows "underconfident", the footer avg is POSITIVE
- The footer avg does NOT show the opposite sign of the bar (this would indicate the raw service calibrationGap was used instead of actual − self)

---

## Scenario 5 — Self-only guarantee (you cannot see another user's diffs)

**Given** I am logged in as User B (not the page owner)

**When** I directly navigate to `/me/calibration`

**Then**
- I see MY OWN diffs only (User B's listener rows)
- I do NOT see User A's partner names, ratings, or dates
- There is no way to supply a different userId in the URL or browser devtools that would return User A's data
  (verify by checking network tab: the RPC call has no userId param, only returns data for the authenticated session)

---

## Scenario 6 — Mobile 320px column abbreviation + tooltip

**Given** I am viewing `/me/calibration` on a 320px-wide screen (or Chrome DevTools mobile emulation at 320px)

**When** I look at the table headers

**Then**
- Col1 header shows the abbreviated form: "you believed" with an `(i)` icon
- Col2 header shows the abbreviated form: "they believe" with an `(i)` icon
- Col3 header shows "gap" (unchanged at all widths)
- No horizontal scroll bar appears

**When** I tap/click the `(i)` next to "you believed"

**Then**
- A tooltip appears with the full text: "Your own rating, before feedback: how well you thought you understood what your partner actually meant."
- The tooltip auto-closes after ~3 seconds (CalibrationTooltip behavior)

**When** I tap/click the `(i)` next to "they believe"

**Then**
- A tooltip appears with the full text: "Your partner's rating, after you explained their point back to them: how well they felt you actually understood."

---

## Scenario 7 — Navigation: bar link on own profile

**Given** I am logged in and on my own profile (`/me`)
**And** I have ≥1 eligible listener verification row

**When** I look at my calibration bar

**Then**
- The bar is a link labelled "See your {N} diffs →" (where N is my row count)
- Clicking/tapping the link navigates to `/me/calibration`
- No dead-end tooltip appears (the tooltip has been replaced by the link on own profile)

---

## Scenario 8 — Focus page chrome

**Given** I am on `/me/calibration`

**Then**
- The BottomNav is NOT visible (focus page)
- A back button/arrow is visible at the top
- Clicking/tapping the back button navigates to `/me` (own profile)
