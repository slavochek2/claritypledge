The rule

Both say a number out of 10.
The lower one counts.

  1. 1One speaker, one listener. Your card shows which you are.
  2. 2Say your number out loud: “I’m at a 7.”
  3. 3Above 8, the partner may challenge. If it falls, back to understanding.
  4. 4The owner can take the speaker card back at any time.
“I’ll accept the lower of our two numbers as my verified understanding of your intended meaning.”Clarity Meeting Principle
